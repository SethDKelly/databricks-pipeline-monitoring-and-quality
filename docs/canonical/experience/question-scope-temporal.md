# Question Proposition, Intent, Scope & Temporal Perspective

**Canonical key:** `experience.question-scope-temporal`

**Kind:** EXPERIENCE CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.EXPL`

**Owns current question:** How is a business/operational question bounded and routed before Explanation selects evidence or composes communication?

**Stable IDs:** EXPL-001–EXPL-012

## Current semantics

### EXPL-001 — Question Request Proposition & Identity
A question request is identified by requested conclusion, subject, scope, temporal perspective and material context; natural-language wording alone is not proposition identity and creates no source truth.

### EXPL-002 — Requested Conclusion Type & Question Family
Classify the requested conclusion only to route semantics and evidence burdens; question-family labels do not establish truth, authority, sufficiency or permission.

### EXPL-003 — Subject Identity & Target Resolution
Resolve the exact Entity Identity and material environment/version/field/metric/execution/consumer/control dimensions before answering; names and aliases alone do not establish identity.

### EXPL-004 — Question Scope Dimensions & Boundary
Bind the requested proposition to the dimensions material to its meaning, and never expand broad wording beyond the scope actually supported by evidence and authorization.

### EXPL-005 — Temporal Perspective: Event Window & Knowledge Cut
Keep event/effective-time target and framework knowledge cutoff independently explicit whenever the answer depends on what happened versus what could be known by a particular time.

### EXPL-006 — Current, Historical & Retrospective Question Semantics
Distinguish current-state, as-known-at-cut historical and current-retrospective questions; a historical event target without an earlier knowledge cut defaults to current retrospective interpretation when context does not say otherwise.

### EXPL-007 — Compound Question Decomposition & Subquestion Identity
Decompose compound requests when conclusions have different truth owners, evidence burdens, scopes or temporal perspectives so an answerable sibling cannot silently resolve an unresolved sibling.

### EXPL-008 — Truth-Owner Routing & Cross-Concept Join Boundaries
Route every material substantive proposition to its accepted owner; Explanation may compose explicit joins but cannot convert Lineage, timing, Deployment, health or proximity into causality or other stronger truth.

### EXPL-009 — Ambiguity, Underspecification & Competing Interpretations
Preserve material ambiguity and competing interpretations when the request does not uniquely bind subject, conclusion, scope or time rather than choosing a convenient reading that strengthens the answer.

### EXPL-010 — Answerability, Evidence Availability & Authorization Separation
Keep semantic answerability, evidence availability/sufficiency and current disclosure authorization separate; a question may be valid yet partially evidenced, internally answerable but disclosure-limited, or historically unreconstructable.

### EXPL-011 — Question-Bound Negative & Absence Burdens
Negative wording never lowers the applicable REF/HLTH/OPS/AUTH burden for bounded opportunity, coverage and conclusion-specific evidence.

### EXPL-012 — Answer-Composition Handoff
Provide Group 02 with bounded subquestion identities, routed truth families, temporal perspective, ambiguity, answerability and evidence/authorization limitations without manufacturing an answer conclusion.

## Invariants / boundaries

- question ≠ truth ≠ authorization;
- wording ≠ proposition identity;
- name ≠ Entity Identity;
- broad wording ≠ broad evidence coverage;
- current retrospective ≠ what was known then;
- compound presentation ≠ composite truth;
- answered sibling ≠ unresolved sibling;
- asking for a negative ≠ lower negative-evidence burden;
- Explanation remains a projection over source-owned truth.

## Architecture boundary

This contract does not select NLP/intent classification, semantic parsing, LLM/prompting, chat UX, query language, search/index architecture, persistence, retrieval or clarification-dialog implementation.
