# Investigation, Causality, Impact, Control & Governance Question Semantics

**Canonical key:** `experience.investigation-impact-control-governance-questions`

**Kind:** EXPERIENCE CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANONICAL CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.EXPL`

**Owns current question:** How are inferential, Impact, control and governance questions decomposed into accepted truth-family propositions without inventing RCA, affected, control-effectiveness, blame, compliance or authority shortcuts?

**Stable IDs:** EXPL-050–EXPL-080

## Current semantics

### EXPL-050 — Inferential & Governance Shorthand Decomposition
Decompose `why`, root-cause, affected, protected, blocked, responsible, authorized, compliant and similar shorthand into exact accepted truth-family propositions before composing an answer.

### EXPL-051 — Investigation Status, Scope & Lifecycle Question
Answer Investigation state from its exact question/scope/lifecycle history; an open, paused, closed or reopened inquiry says nothing automatic about causal status.

### EXPL-052 — Investigation Lead, Candidate & Disposition Question
Project lead/candidate basis and disposition as Investigation state only; priority, rank, analyst interest or proximity does not turn a lead into a Causal Claim.

### EXPL-053 — Localization & First-Deviation Boundary Question
Keep first observed deviation, earliest evidenced state change, first transformation/reconciliation boundary and first consumer effect distinct and bounded to searched scope/evidence.

### EXPL-054 — Causal `Why` Proposition Identity
Causal wording requires an explicit cause→effect proposition with role, scope, time/context and motivating evidence; operational correlation is insufficient.

### EXPL-055 — Causal Role, Mechanism & Contribution Question
Preserve whether a claim asserts caused, contributed, enabled, triggered, prevented or materially influenced and retain mechanism/transmission assumptions rather than flattening to one `cause` label.

### EXPL-056 — Causal Claim Status & Confirmation Question
Project proposed/supported/weakened/unresolved/rejected/confirmed exactly; `confirmed` remains evidence-gated by REF-017 and authority-gated by AUTH-034.

### EXPL-057 — Competing & Compatible Causal Claims Question
Represent multiple competing or compatible claims independently; no narrative ranking, majority or singular winner is invented without accepted evidence/authority resolution.

### EXPL-058 — Root-Cause Singular/Plural Wording
Treat `root cause` as request wording, not a requirement for one causal winner; explanations may report multiple contributors, unresolved alternatives or no confirmed cause.

### EXPL-059 — Causal Negative, Rejection & Exclusion Question
Lack of support does not equal rejection; causal exclusion/rejection requires discriminating or negative evidence adequate for the exact proposition and alternatives under review.

### EXPL-060 — Impact Candidate & Reachability Question
Answer prospective or realized candidate/reachability at the appropriate Impact layer without promoting reachable to opportunity, exposure, effect, consequence or cause.

### EXPL-061 — Encounter Opportunity & Exposure Question
Separate opportunity, availability/publication, actual encounter and exposure; bind exposure to exact origin state/version, consumer/use/path and time.

### EXPL-062 — Downstream Effect Question
Downstream effect requires source-owned Observation/Assessment/Change evidence for the exact dimension/scope/time and does not follow automatically from exposure.

### EXPL-063 — Consequence Question
Technical/operational, analytical and business/process consequences are evidence categories; publication/view/reliance/consequence remain separate boundaries.

### EXPL-064 — Impact Causal Attribution Question
A statement that an origin caused/contributed to an effect or consequence is a Causal Claim and must not be inferred merely because exposure and effect coexist.

### EXPL-065 — Impact Aggregation, `All/None/Only` & Affected Wording
Resolve `affected` to the intended Impact layer and require explicit population/path/coverage semantics for aggregate all/none/only claims; no universal Impact percentage substitutes.

### EXPL-066 — Safeguard Lifecycle & Administrative-State Question
Keep Safeguard proposal, authorization, request/issuance, acceptance, enforcement, extension, expiry and release facts separate; administrative state alone does not prove protection.

### EXPL-067 — Safeguard Enforcement Question
Project evidence-established path/scope/time enforcement under REF-027 and preserve partial, conflicting or unavailable telemetry rather than assuming active configuration equals enforcement.

### EXPL-068 — Prevented-Exposure Question
Use REF-028 plus opportunity/path/enforcement/non-exposure/alternate-path evidence; `Safeguard active + not exposed` is insufficient prevention credit by itself.

### EXPL-069 — Safeguard Release, Expiry & Recovery Question
Separate scheduled/effective expiry, release request/effective release and independently evidenced post-protection freshness/health/delivery/recovery.

### EXPL-070 — Gate Criterion, Suitability & Readiness Question
Keep health/result truth, exact-use evidence suitability and readiness result separate from Gate decisions and control enforcement.

### EXPL-071 — Gate Decision, HOLD/ADMIT & Exceptional Admission Question
Project normal HOLD/ADMIT plus override/fallback/escalation with exact opportunity/profile context; override/fallback never rewrites underlying readiness.

### EXPL-072 — Gate Delivery & Enforcement Question
Separate decision issuance, delivery, acceptance and effective Gate enforcement; HOLD is not failed execution and ADMIT is not execution occurrence.

### EXPL-073 — Gate/Safeguard Overlap & Control-Effect Question
Gate and Safeguard remain independent controls with no hidden precedence; broader delay/staleness/prevention/business-effect attribution requires source evidence and, where causal, Causal Claim semantics.

### EXPL-074 — Responsibility Assignment Question
Responsibility Assignment answers who is assigned a bounded responsibility; it does not establish fault, cause, Assertion Authority, Capability Authorization or action success.

### EXPL-075 — Semantic Definition & Meaning Question
Semantic Definition answers governed meaning; it does not establish realized state, health, policy applicability, authorization or compliance.

### EXPL-076 — Classification Question
Classification answers the applicable classification scheme/context result and does not automatically establish Policy Context, authorization, Impact, severity or compliance.

### EXPL-077 — Policy Context Question
Policy Context answers which policy/rule context applies; it does not establish enforcement, legal interpretation, compliance outcome or capability permission by itself.

### EXPL-078 — Capability Authorization Question
Capability Authorization answers whether a principal may perform an exact action on a subject/context/time; it is independent from responsibility, Assertion Authority and whether the action actually succeeded.

### EXPL-079 — Assertion Authority Question
Assertion Authority answers who has standing to assert a bounded proposition under governing rules; it does not create factual truth, evidence sufficiency, permission or enforcement.

### EXPL-080 — Epistemic-Language Handoff
Provide Group 05 exact source proposition/status, evidence conditions and material limitations so uncertainty language can remain proposition-faithful rather than generic reassurance.

## Invariants / boundaries

- Investigation ≠ Causal Claim;
- localization ≠ root cause;
- supported ≠ confirmed;
- candidate/reachable ≠ opportunity ≠ exposure ≠ effect ≠ consequence ≠ causal attribution;
- Safeguard administration ≠ enforcement ≠ prevented exposure ≠ recovery;
- readiness ≠ Gate decision ≠ enforcement ≠ execution;
- HOLD ≠ failed execution; ADMIT ≠ run;
- Gate ≠ Safeguard;
- responsibility ≠ fault/cause/authority/permission;
- Assertion Authority ≠ factual truth/evidence sufficiency/Capability Authorization;
- no universal RCA/Impact/control-effectiveness/governance-confidence score.

## Architecture boundary

This contract does not choose RCA algorithms, graph ranking, LLM/prompt architecture, Impact scoring, control integrations, policy engines, IAM implementation, governance vendors, UI templates, citation widgets or persistence/retrieval architecture.
