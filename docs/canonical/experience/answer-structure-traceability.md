# Answer Structure, Statement Selection & Basis Traceability

**Canonical key:** `experience.answer-structure-traceability`

**Kind:** EXPERIENCE CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.EXPL`

**Owns current question:** How does a bounded question become an evidence-grounded Explanation without turning composed statements into independent truth?

**Stable IDs:** EXPL-013–EXPL-028

## Current semantics

### EXPL-013 — Material Answer Statement Proposition Identity
A material answer statement retains stable subject, predicate/conclusion, scope, event/effective target and knowledge perspective independent of rendered wording.

### EXPL-014 — Statement Source Ownership & Epistemic Type Preservation
Every substantive answer statement preserves its accepted source truth owner and epistemic type; Explanation communication cannot become the owner merely by restating it.

### EXPL-015 — Direct Projection vs Derived Cross-Concept Statement
Distinguish direct projection of one source-owned proposition from an explicit derived statement that joins several propositions; derived statements require declared join logic and their own sufficiency review.

### EXPL-016 — Question Relevance, Materiality & Statement Selection
Select statements because they materially answer the bounded question, not because Criticality, recency, graph distance, source count or presentation priority makes them appear more true.

### EXPL-017 — Answer-Bearing, Contextual & Limitation Statement Roles
Keep answer-bearing conclusions, contextual clarification and limitations as distinct communication roles so context or caveats cannot silently become the conclusion.

### EXPL-018 — Supporting, Contradicting & Limiting Basis Roles
Basis items are statement-relative and may support, contradict or limit the conclusion while retaining their original source ownership and epistemic status.

### EXPL-019 — Conclusion-Relative Basis Sufficiency & Traceability
Evaluate sufficiency for the exact statement being communicated and retain internal statement-to-basis traceability sufficient to reconstruct why the statement was eligible.

### EXPL-020 — Basis Cardinality, Independence & Common Derivation
Evidence count is not confidence; duplicates and common-derived observations are not independent corroboration and must not be inflated by presentation.

### EXPL-021 — Explicit Cross-Concept Join Logic & Non-Transitive Composition
A derived statement must name the semantic relationship joining its dependencies; juxtaposition, temporal proximity, shared Lineage or shared source does not create a transitive conclusion.

### EXPL-022 — Headline, Summary & Detail Semantic Equivalence
Headline, summary and detail views may vary in compression but must preserve proposition scope, polarity, epistemic state and material qualifications.

### EXPL-023 — Omission, Compression & Material-Qualification Preservation
Omission or compression is valid only when it does not remove a material qualification in a way that strengthens or broadens the apparent conclusion.

### EXPL-024 — Multi-Statement Composition & Sibling Independence
Several statements can coexist in one answer without forming a synthetic global truth; support for one sibling never upgrades another sibling's unresolved or limited state.

### EXPL-025 — Primary-Answer Selection & Ordering
Primary ordering is question/context specific and may optimize usefulness, but no universal severity, importance, certainty or answer-priority score becomes truth.

### EXPL-026 — Internal Basis-Inspection Identity & Provenance
Each inspectable statement retains a stable internal linkage to its basis, derivation and provenance even when current authorization later limits visible basis disclosure.

### EXPL-027 — Answer Coverage Without Confidence Substitution
Coverage is evaluated against bounded subquestions and material statement obligations; it is not converted into a universal completeness, confidence or answer-quality percentage.

### EXPL-028 — Domain-Question Handoff
Provide later question-family contracts with stable statement identity, source ownership, basis roles, join logic, limitation behavior and sibling-safe composition without selecting domain conclusions in advance.

## Invariants / boundaries

- wording ≠ statement identity;
- answer statement ≠ independent truth;
- direct projection ≠ derived cross-concept statement;
- juxtaposition ≠ semantic join;
- materiality/ordering ≠ evidence strength/authority;
- basis count ≠ confidence;
- common derivation ≠ independent corroboration;
- summary/detail compression cannot strengthen truth;
- sibling statements remain epistemically independent;
- answer coverage ≠ universal completeness/confidence score.

## Architecture boundary

This contract does not select response templates, LLMs, deterministic generation engines, citation UI, retrieval/search architecture, persistence schema or basis-storage model.
