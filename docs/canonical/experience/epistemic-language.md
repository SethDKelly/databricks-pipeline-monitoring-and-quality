# Uncertainty, Conflict, Negative Claims & Epistemic Language

**Canonical key:** `experience.epistemic-language`

**Kind:** EXPERIENCE CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANONICAL CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.EXPL`

**Owns current question:** How does Explanation communicate uncertainty, conflict, restriction and negative-evidence states without inventing confidence arithmetic or reassuring completion?

**Stable IDs:** EXPL-081–EXPL-100

## Current semantics

### EXPL-081 — Epistemic-State Preservation
Visible language preserves the exact source-owned result/status and may not upgrade, soften or collapse it for readability.

### EXPL-082 — Unknown & Unresolved Language
Unknown/unresolved means the bounded proposition is not currently resolved; it does not imply false, absent, safe or benign.

### EXPL-083 — Unavailable Evidence Language
Unavailable source/evidence condition is an evidence-access state, not negative truth about the proposition.

### EXPL-084 — Insufficient Evidence & Indeterminate Language
Insufficient evidence/indeterminate means the conclusion burden is not met; it is neither a weak positive nor weak negative.

### EXPL-085 — Conflict Language
Evidence conflict, authoritative assertion conflict, authority-rule conflict, authorization conflict and competing causal claims remain distinct and are not resolved by convenient majority/recency phrasing.

### EXPL-086 — Restricted, Redacted & Opaque Language
Restricted/redacted/opaque means disclosure is limited; it never means evidence or state is absent.

### EXPL-087 — Stale-for-Use Language
Stale-for-use communicates failure to satisfy an exact freshness/currentness use criterion and does not automatically imply broader quality failure or policy violation.

### EXPL-088 — Non-Comparable Language
Non-comparable means the applicable comparison lacks a valid empirical basis; it is distinct from not applicable, unhealthy or policy failure.

### EXPL-089 — Not-Applicable Language
Not applicable means the exact criterion/metric/proposition does not semantically apply to the bound subject/context; it is not a pass or missing evidence state.

### EXPL-090 — Not-Evidenced, Not-Recorded & Not-Known-by-Cutoff Language
Keep `not evidenced`, `not recorded` and `not known by cutoff` separate from evidence-backed absence and from one another when their provenance differs.

### EXPL-091 — Strong Negative & Positive Calibration
Strong negatives preserve conclusion-specific opportunity/coverage burdens; positive conclusions use accepted source status rather than invented probability/certainty adjectives.

### EXPL-092 — Causal Epistemic Language
Project proposed/supported/weakened/unresolved/rejected/confirmed exactly; support is not confirmation and rejection requires evidence rather than mere non-support.

### EXPL-093 — Impact Epistemic Language
Keep candidate, opportunity, exposed/not-exposed/safe-other/unknown, effect/no-effect and consequence/no-consequence states distinct and scope bound.

### EXPL-094 — Execution & Control Epistemic Language
Missing or degraded run/control telemetry does not prove no run, successful HOLD, fail-open, fail-closed, fallback application or control success/failure.

### EXPL-095 — Governance Epistemic Language
Missing responsibility, classification, policy, authorization or authority metadata remains unknown/unavailable rather than defaulting to benign, permitted, compliant or authoritative.

### EXPL-096 — Competing Claims Without Forced Winner
Retain multiple compatible/competing claims when no accepted evidence/authority rule resolves them; Explanation may summarize the competition without synthesizing a winner.

### EXPL-097 — Material Limitation Visibility
A material limitation that constrains the conclusion must remain visible at every summary level where omission would strengthen or broaden the apparent answer.

### EXPL-098 — No Cross-Statement Confidence Aggregation
Independent sibling statements retain independent epistemic states; no average, score, badge or narrative synthesis creates a global confidence/answer-quality state.

### EXPL-099 — Historical Epistemic Evolution
As-known-at-cut uncertainty remains historically valid even when later evidence resolves the current retrospective proposition; later resolution does not rewrite earlier knowledge.

### EXPL-100 — Authorized-Projection Handoff
Provide Group 06 the internally valid statement/status/limitation/basis so disclosure rules can reduce visible detail without changing epistemic strength.

## Invariants / boundaries

- unknown/unresolved ≠ false/absent/safe;
- unavailable evidence ≠ negative evidence;
- insufficient/indeterminate ≠ weak positive/negative;
- restricted/redacted ≠ absent;
- stale-for-use ≠ violation by default;
- non-comparable ≠ not applicable;
- not evidenced/not recorded/not known by cutoff ≠ did not occur;
- absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence;
- supported ≠ confirmed;
- competing claims remain multiple absent accepted resolution;
- no universal confidence/probability/completeness/RCA/answer-quality score.

## Architecture boundary

This contract does not choose confidence algorithms, probabilistic models, score calibration, LLM hedging prompts, UI badges/color scales, alert severities, citation widgets, source precedence, authorization systems or technical architecture.
