# Disclosure, Explanation & Audience Governance

**Canonical key:** `auth.disclosure-governance`

**Kind:** AUTHORITY

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.AUTH`

**Owns current question:** What may be disclosed to an audience and how must projection preserve source truth, status and restricted-basis limitations?

**Stable IDs:** AUTH-044–AUTH-053

## Current semantics

### AUTH-044 — Disclosure Target, Audience, Purpose, Context, and Delivery Binding
Disclosure binds requester/audience, information/detail class, subject/context, purpose, temporal perspective and delivery scope. Audience labels are context, not permission sources.

### AUTH-045 — Result, Basis, Provenance, and Detail-Level Disclosure Separation
Permission to see a conclusion can differ from permission to see its measurements, thresholds, schema, evidence, source, actor or authority basis. Hidden basis remains restricted, not absent.

### AUTH-046 — Safe Abstraction, Redaction, Opaque Existence, and Minimization
Permitted projection may expose exact, coarse/ranged, redacted, generalized, opaque or withheld detail only when independently authorized and semantically valid.

### AUTH-047 — Composite, Mosaic, and Repeated-Query Inference-Leakage Governance
Disclosure safety considers combinations of facts and repeated/differencing queries; individually permitted details can jointly reveal restricted state.

### AUTH-048 — Audience Projection Consistency Across Technical, Business, Executive, and Audit Views
Audience views are projections over one truth. Detail/vocabulary may differ, but scope, polarity, uncertainty, causal/Impact status and control evidence cannot be strengthened for convenience.

### AUTH-049 — High-Consequence Communication Review, Approval, Release, Correction, and Retraction
Internal view permission does not automatically grant external/client/regulatory/high-consequence publication. Compose/review/approve/release/correct/retract may be separately governed; approval cannot make a statement true.

### AUTH-050 — Status-Preserving Language and Non-Overstatement in Disclosure
`supported` cannot become `confirmed`; reachable cannot become exposed/affected; HOLD decision cannot become enforcement; safeguard active cannot become prevented exposure; release cannot become healthy; waiver cannot become clean pass.

### AUTH-051 — Disclosure of Human Attribution, Authority, Authorization, and Control Metadata
Actor, confirmer/operator, approval chain, delegation, authority/authorization rule and control metadata may be independently sensitive even when the result can be disclosed.

### AUTH-052 — Historical Disclosure, Retained Explanation, and Current-Authorization Separation
Retained historical communication, reconstructed as-known-then view, current retrospective Explanation, historical authorization and current requester disclosure are separate. Current authorization governs present disclosure.

### AUTH-053 — Disclosure Conflict, Unknown/Unavailable Review State, and Safe Non-Disclosure
Unknown, conflicting, unavailable or unsafe-to-project disclosure state never becomes permission. Safe withholding does not fabricate an explicit deny or imply hidden facts do not exist.

## Invariants / boundaries

Safe abstraction is epistemically monotone: reduce detail/narrow when required, never strengthen status or negative coverage. Explanation remains a projection, not an independent truth source.
