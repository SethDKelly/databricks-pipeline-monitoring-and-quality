# Normative Health, Metric & Threshold Governance

**Canonical key:** `auth.normative-health-governance`

**Kind:** AUTHORITY

**Authority:** CANONICAL CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.AUTH`

**Owns current question:** Who/what may authoritatively establish, revise, waive, retire or approve high-consequence use of normative health and metric/schema rules?

**Stable IDs:** AUTH-016–AUTH-023

## Current semantics

### AUTH-016 — Expectation-Class and Normative Authority
Expectation authority is dimension/property/context/time/action scoped; descriptive Baseline behavior cannot become normative merely through frequency.

### AUTH-017 — Metric Profile Selection, Purpose, and Critical-Metric Authority
Metric/check profiles are governed selection/applicability structures with purpose and lifecycle, not truth-owning concepts or permission to compute every available statistic.

### AUTH-018 — Threshold, Margin, Tolerance, and Severity Authority
Metric meaning/profile inclusion, threshold/margin/tolerance and severity are separate governed layers and may have different authoritative holders.

### AUTH-019 — Structural / Schema Compatibility Expectation Authority
Required/optional fields, type/nullability/key/grain evolution, additive-change policy and consumer compatibility are explicit normative Expectations with scoped authority.

### AUTH-020 — Metric Applicability, Baseline Use, and Structural-Change Review Authority
Governance may require scoped review of metric/profile/Baseline applicability after structural Change, but cannot manufacture empirical comparability.

### AUTH-021 — Exception, Waiver, Suspension, and Retirement Governance
Bounded exceptions/waivers/suspensions/retirement alter normative applicability or response obligations without rewriting Observation, realized schema, Baseline deviation or historical evidence into a false pass.

### AUTH-022 — Normative Conflict, Business/Technical Coexistence, and Rule Composition
`strictest wins`, `business wins`, `technical wins`, highest severity and latest record are not implicit conflict resolvers. Same-target co-authoritative conflict remains explicit.

### AUTH-023 — High-Consequence Metric / Expectation Use Eligibility
Authority can mark a metric/Expectation eligible for Gate/Safeguard/high-consequence use, but eligibility does not grant control capability, prove evidence readiness or prove enforcement.

## Invariants / boundaries

Metric meaning ≠ profile inclusion ≠ threshold ≠ severity ≠ waiver ≠ control-use eligibility. Baseline remains descriptive unless an authoritative Expectation adopts a criterion.
