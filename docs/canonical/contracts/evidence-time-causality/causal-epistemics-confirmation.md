**Owns current question:** What evidence and epistemic transitions are permitted for causal propositions, including confirmation and later challenge?

**Stable IDs:** REF-013–REF-020

## Current semantics

### REF-013 — Causal Proposition and Role Binding
A causal proposition binds cause, effect, causal role, relevant context/time and material mechanism/transmission assumptions. Vague `root cause` labels are insufficient proposition identity.

### REF-014 — Causal Epistemic Status Vocabulary and Transition Semantics
Accepted states are `proposed`, `supported`, `weakened`, `unresolved`, `rejected`, `confirmed`. Transitions are non-linear and history preserving.

### REF-015 — Causal Support, Contradiction, and Evidence-Dimension Evaluation
Evaluate applicable dimensions such as cause/effect occurrence, temporal order, historical relationship/mechanism, encounter/transmission, semantic compatibility, contrast/intervention, alternatives and coverage. No universal numeric confidence score is accepted.

### REF-016 — Material Alternatives and Causal Discrimination
Confirmation considers a bounded material alternative set. Mutually exclusive unresolved alternatives can block confirmation; compatible contributors may coexist.

### REF-017 — Confirmation Evidence Gate and Authority Separation
`confirmed` is a high-consequence evidence gate, not `strongly supported`. The confirmation profile must require a bounded proposition, sufficient cause/effect evidence, applicable temporal/mechanism/transmission requirements, decisive contradiction review, material-alternative treatment, sufficient exclusion coverage, standard provenance, independently resolved confirmation authority and a provenance-bearing confirmation action.

### REF-018 — Multiple Contributors and Qualitative Causal Roles
Multiple claims may be supported or confirmed as direct, contributing, enabling, triggering, preventing, primary or another justified qualitative role. `Primary` is comparative and requires comparative evidence; qualitative contribution does not imply percentage attribution.

### REF-019 — Progressive RCA Maturity and Fast-Path Causal Communication
RCA may progress from proposed candidates to early support/weakened/unresolved results to deeper investigation and confirmation review. Latency or elapsed analysis time cannot inflate epistemic state.

### REF-020 — Challenge, Reversal, and Historical Preservation After Confirmation
Confirmed claims remain challengeable. Material late/corrected evidence may revise current status while preserving who confirmed, under which standard/evidence cut and when; historical actions/communications are not counterfactually rewritten.

## Invariants / boundaries

- Lineage, temporal proximity, deployment correlation, first deviation or Investigation priority ≠ cause;
- lack of contradiction ≠ proof;
- rejection requires sufficient contradictory/exclusion evidence, not mere lack of support;
- evidence gate ≠ confirmation permission: AUTH-034 supplies the authority side.
