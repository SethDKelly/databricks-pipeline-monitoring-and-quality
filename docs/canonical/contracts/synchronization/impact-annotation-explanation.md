# Impact, Annotation & Explanation Synchronizations

**Canonical key:** `contract.synchronization.impact_annotation_explanation`

**Kind:** CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.SYN`

**Owns current question:** How are downstream candidates/exposure/effects/consequences, human context, authorization and Explanation coordinated without manufacturing Impact, cause, declassification or new truth?

**Stable IDs:** SYN-023–SYN-031

## Current semantics

Downstream reasoning is layered and evidence-bound; human/audience projections preserve source truth and authorization.

### SYN-023 — Incident Context + Downstream Lineage → Impact Candidates
Use time-applicable authorized Lineage to identify downstream candidates only. Reachability is not exposure/effect/consequence.

### SYN-024 — Candidate + Encounter/Consumption Evidence → Exposure
Resolve exposure/non-exposure from exact consumer/use/state/time evidence with sufficient positive or bounded negative opportunity/path/version coverage. Missing consumer telemetry is indeterminate, not `not exposed`.

### SYN-025 — Downstream Observation/Assessment/Change → Effect
A downstream effect is established by the downstream subject's own source-owned evidence. Exposure alone does not create degradation/effect.

### SYN-026 — Effect + Provenance-Bearing Use Evidence → Consequence
Technical/analytical/business consequence requires evidence of the relevant use/process/outcome. Criticality, Classification, client-facing status, or reachability are context—not consequence proof.

### SYN-027 — Origin → Downstream Effect/Consequence Attribution
Any proposition that the origin caused/contributed to a downstream effect or consequence belongs to Causal Claim with its evidence/epistemic status.

### SYN-028 — Effective Safeguard + Negative Consumption Evidence → Prevented Exposure
A bounded prevented-exposure conclusion requires effective safeguard enforcement plus sufficient alternate-path/opportunity/encounter coverage. Coincident non-exposure or active protection alone is not prevention credit; protection-induced delay/non-delivery remains separate evidence.

### SYN-029 — Human Context → Annotation or Structured Owner
Human commentary remains attributed Annotation unless it crosses into a structured assertion; then the appropriate concept (Change Intent, Expectation, Responsibility, Classification, Policy Context, Causal Claim, etc.) records it separately.

### SYN-030 — Capability Authorization + Concept State → Authorized Analytical Projection
Construct a task/requester-specific projection from independently authorized concept state. Authorization can hide/abstract detail but is not declassification, truth mutation, authority, or a new concept.

### SYN-031 — Authorized Projection → Explanation
Explanation composes only the authorized projection, preserving proposition type, source truth owner, statement-to-basis traceability, causal/Impact layers, Annotation attribution, policy/authority distinctions, Gate/Safeguard state, and temporal perspective. It cannot strengthen hidden/uncertain truth for readability.

## Invariants / boundaries

- Reachability ≠ exposure ≠ effect ≠ consequence ≠ causality.
- Strong negative claims retain opportunity/coverage burden.
- Capability Authorization controls projection, not semantic truth.
- Explanation/Annotation are not independent truth sources.
