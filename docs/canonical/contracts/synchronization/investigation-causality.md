# Investigation & Causality Synchronizations

**Canonical key:** `contract.synchronization.investigation_causality`

**Kind:** CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.SYN`

**Owns current question:** How does a bounded Investigation assemble evidence/localization and hand causal propositions to Causal Claim without promoting topology, timing, leads, or closure into cause?

**Stable IDs:** SYN-016–SYN-022

## Current semantics

Investigation organizes question-relative evidence and competing leads. Causal Claim alone owns cause/contribution/enabling/preventing propositions and their epistemic state.

### SYN-016 — Investigation Question + Scope
Bind Investigation to an explicit question/outcome, identified subjects, event-time window and knowledge cut. The opening symptom/leading theory is not presumed cause; scope revisions are non-rewriting.

### SYN-017 — Historical Lineage → Evidence Candidates
Traverse time-applicable typed Lineage to identify upstream/downstream evidence candidates. Reachability/localization provides search context, not causal status.

### SYN-018 — Candidate Evidence Assembly
Assemble relevant Execution History, timing, Observation, Assessment, Change, Deployment, Change Intent, reference, Safeguard/Gate and governance evidence with supporting/contradicting roles, gaps, restrictions, common derivation and provenance. Linking does not transfer source truth.

### SYN-019 — Causal Proposition → Causal Claim
The moment inquiry asserts cause, contribution, enabling, triggering, preventing, or material influence, represent it as an explicit Causal Claim rather than an Investigation lead/localization label.

### SYN-020 — Causal Claim Evaluation
Evaluate a claim using proposition-specific temporal ordering, evidence applicability/coverage, actual encounter/consumption where material, realized state/change, mechanism compatibility, alternatives/contradictions, and conclusion-specific sufficiency. Proximity, Deployment, Lineage, or ranking alone are insufficient.

### SYN-021 — Competing/Contributing Claims + Closure
Multiple compatible, contributing, competing, weakened, rejected or unresolved claims may coexist. Investigation closure/operational resolution never promotes claim status; `confirmed` remains independently evidence- and authority-gated.

### SYN-022 — Analyst/Automated Research → Structured Truth Owners
Analyst or automated assistance follows the same model: factual evidence → Observation/Change/etc.; causal proposition → Causal Claim; attributed human commentary → Annotation. Tool/model output never becomes canonical truth/authority merely by generation.

## Invariants / boundaries

- First deviation/localization ≠ cause.
- Investigation lead ≠ Causal Claim status; closure ≠ confirmation.
- Evidence supporting and contradicting claims remains eligible.
- No universal causal-confidence score or forced single root cause.
