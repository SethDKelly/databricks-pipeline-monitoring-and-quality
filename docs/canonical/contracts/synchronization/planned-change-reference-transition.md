# Planned Change & Reference Transition Synchronizations

**Canonical key:** `contract.synchronization.planned_change_reference_transition`

**Kind:** CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.SYN`

**Owns current question:** How does planned Change Intent coordinate with normative/descriptive references, Deployment realization, and prospective downstream review without being promoted to realized truth?

**Stable IDs:** SYN-004–SYN-008

## Current semantics

Planned context may prepare later interpretation but never rewrites current empirical/normative/topological state by registration alone.

### SYN-004 — Change Intent + Prospective Reference Review
A Change Intent may trigger explicit Expectation review/revision and/or register a prospective Baseline comparability break. Anticipated effects remain descriptive plan context until separately established as normative criteria or empirically observed behavior.

### SYN-005 — Change Intent + Deployment Association
Associate exact intent revision/component with Deployment attempt/activation only through provenance-bearing linkage. Association may be many-to-many and is not proof of activation, conformance, intended effect, health, or causality.

### SYN-006 — Realization + Reference Context Transition
Expectation/Baseline applicability/comparability transitions only when sufficient evidence establishes the relevant realized operating context/effective boundary. Intent-registration or workflow timestamps are not substitutes.

### SYN-007 — Post-Transition Expectation + Baseline
A separately authorized post-change Expectation may support immediate normative validation; a post-change Baseline remains empirical and requires sufficient comparable post-change Observations.

### SYN-008 — Change Intent + Lineage → Prospective Impact Profile
Construct prospective downstream candidate/review context from exact intent component plus then-effective Lineage and explicit planned topology changes. This may prioritize review but does not create active Lineage, exposure, downstream effect, business consequence, probability, or realized Impact.

## Invariants / boundaries

- Planned ≠ deployed ≠ realized ≠ healthy ≠ causal.
- Baseline remains descriptive; Expectation remains normative.
- Prospective candidate state never populates realized Impact layers.
- No universal risk score is accepted.
