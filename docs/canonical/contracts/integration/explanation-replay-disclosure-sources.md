# Explanation, Historical Replay, Basis Inspection & Disclosure Sources

**Canonical key:** `integration.group-07`

**Kind:** INTEGRATION CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANDIDATE / NOT CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.INTG`

**Stable IDs:** INTG-201–INTG-238

**Owns current question:** Which source history/provenance can support Explanation basis, as-known-at-cut replay, retained communication, current retrospective interpretation and requester-specific basis inspection/disclosure?

## Canonical source findings

Preserve the chain:

**source-owned proposition/evidence + stable provenance → event/effective state + source availability/knowledge coordinates → as-known-at-cut basis eligibility → statement-relative basis assembly → internal inspectability → requester-specific authorized projection → retained actual communication where independently evidenced → later retrospective re-evaluation without historical rewrite**.

The four Explanation views remain distinct: historical source state; as-known-at-cut Explanation; actual retained communication; current retrospective Explanation. Current authorized projection applies separately to whichever view is requested.

Stable source references do not guarantee current retrievability. Delivery evidence does not prove exact communication content. Source replay does not prove what was actually communicated. Current requester authorization ≠ historical actor authorization. Visible citation ≠ permission to inspect source basis. Safe coarse/redacted/opaque projection may reduce detail but cannot strengthen truth. Source/basis count never becomes confidence. Missing history or source outage is a basis limitation, not a product-level negative.

## Stable contracts

### INTG-201 — Explanation Statement ↔ Source-Basis Identity
Each material Explanation statement retains internal links to exact source-owned propositions and basis identity; rendered sentence/citation is not the truth owner.

### INTG-202 — Stable Source Reference & Provenance
Retain durable source-surface and source-local object/event/result provenance sufficient to distinguish renamed, recreated or mutated objects; current labels/URLs alone are insufficient.

### INTG-203 — Basis Role, Status & Limitation Retention
Supporting, contradicting and limiting roles remain statement-relative and retain source epistemic status, common derivation and material limitations through composition.

### INTG-204 — Explanation Temporal Coordinates: T / K / Availability
Keep event/effective time `T`, source recorded/availability time, knowledge cut `K`, retrieval time and communication time separate whenever historical reasoning depends on them.

### INTG-205 — As-Known-at-Cut Source Inclusion Rule
A basis item may enter an as-known-at-K Explanation only when evidence establishes that it was reliably available by `K`; old event time alone is insufficient.

### INTG-206 — Late Evidence & Backfill Non-Rewriting
Late-arriving/backfilled evidence may change current retrospective interpretation but never becomes evidence that was available in an earlier knowledge cut.

### INTG-207 — Source Correction & Supersession History
Preserve source correction/supersession/mutation history where available; current corrected state must not silently rewrite prior source/knowledge state.

### INTG-208 — Current Retrospective Re-Evaluation
Current retrospective Explanation may use later accepted evidence/corrections while remaining explicitly distinct from what was knowable or communicated then.

### INTG-209 — Historical Source-State Replay Support
Historical source-state replay requires proposition-specific retained source history sufficient for the requested effective interval; current state is not a replay substitute.

### INTG-210 — History Loss, Deletion & Truncation
Expired retention, disabled history, deletion, truncation or blanked fields remain explicit replay limitations and are not reconstructed from present state or proximity.

### INTG-211 — Retained Communication ≠ Reconstruction
An authentic retained communication/snapshot and a reconstruction of what could have been explained are different evidence classes and may legitimately disagree.

### INTG-212 — Communication Identity, Audience, Context & Time
Actual communication evidence binds exact content/snapshot identity where retained plus audience, purpose/context, delivery channel and communication time.

### INTG-213 — Delivery Evidence vs Exact Communication Content
Delivery metadata can prove bounded delivery without proving exact rendered content, human reading, comprehension or decision reliance.

### INTG-214 — Mutable Communication & Edit History
For mutable messages/comments/discussions, current content is not arbitrary-cut historical content; retained edit history and deletion limits remain material.

### INTG-215 — Missing Retained Communication
Missing authentic prior communication remains missing; a reconstructed historical Explanation may be labeled reconstruction but cannot become an actual retained snapshot.

### INTG-216 — Statement Identity Across Refresh
Preserve statement proposition identity across refresh only when subject/predicate/scope/event target materially match; wording/basis visibility changes alone need not create a new proposition.

### INTG-217 — Basis Enrichment & Explanation Delta
Additional basis may enrich an Explanation without changing conclusion/status; source-owned reevaluation determines semantic deltas rather than basis count or recomposition.

### INTG-218 — Partial Answerability Under Source Gaps
Source/retention/authorization gaps can make one statement/subquestion unresolved while siblings remain valid; partial Explanation is an accepted result.

### INTG-219 — Source Outage / Degraded Explanation Limitation
Source outage/lag/auth failure/permission filtering is a basis-availability limitation and never evidence that the underlying domain proposition is false/absent/safe.

### INTG-220 — Internal Basis Inspectability
Internal basis resolution and current requester disclosure are independent capabilities; complete internal traceability may coexist with restricted visible detail.

### INTG-221 — Source Retrievability vs Visible Reference
A stable citation/reference may remain visible after source history expires or access is revoked; reference existence does not prove current retrievability or permission.

### INTG-222 — Current Requester Authorization for Historical Basis
Current authorization governs present disclosure of historical basis; earlier actor access does not grant current requester access.

### INTG-223 — Historical Actor Authorization vs Current Disclosure
Historical actor authorization, current requester disclosure authorization and retained prior communication are separate records and must not rewrite one another.

### INTG-224 — Result / Context / Limitation / Basis / Detail Authorization
Conclusion, context, material limitation, basis existence/provenance and exact basis detail can have distinct current disclosure decisions.

### INTG-225 — Safe Coarse / Redacted / Opaque Basis Projection
Safe coarse/redacted/opaque projection may reveal less only when authorized and semantically valid; it cannot broaden scope, strengthen polarity/status or fabricate absence.

### INTG-226 — Sensitive Existence / Count / Type / Provenance Metadata
The existence, count, class or provenance of restricted evidence may itself be sensitive and requires an explicit disclosure decision.

### INTG-227 — Sensitive Query Text, Parameters & Error Detail
Query text, parameters and errors are basis data with independent sensitivity/availability/truncation constraints and cannot be assumed inspectable from statement metadata.

### INTG-228 — Sensitive Actor / Consumer Identity
Actor/principal/consumer identities can be sensitive basis metadata and remain subject to identity reconciliation and disclosure authorization.

### INTG-229 — Observer-Relative Source Filtering
Permission-filtered source views are observer-relative; hidden rows/resources/evidence remain restricted/unavailable, not absent.

### INTG-230 — Databricks System-History Retention
Databricks system-history surfaces have heterogeneous documented history/latency/field coverage; `system` does not imply one universal replay horizon.

### INTG-231 — Databricks Query-Content Inspectability
Query-history content is conditionally inspectable due privilege, preview/status, truncation and encryption/blanking behaviors; statement identity may survive when exact text does not.

### INTG-232 — Databricks Alert Delivery Evidence
Alert configuration/evaluation/delivery records can evidence definition-at-time, evaluation and bounded delivery; they do not prove exact rendered message content or human reliance.

### INTG-233 — GitHub Audit History & Replay
GitHub audit history is event-class/retention/export bound and may require external retention for longer replay; exported duplicates remain common-derived evidence.

### INTG-234 — GitHub Comment / Discussion Edit History
GitHub comment/discussion history is mutable and bounded; current content and retained edit revisions may differ, and deleted revision content cannot be reconstructed as authentic history.

### INTG-235 — Collibra Resource History & Visibility
Collibra history can support bounded governance replay but is facet/configuration/permission dependent; visible history for one facet is not universal completeness.

### INTG-236 — Immuta Audit Retention, Export & Inspection
Immuta audit replay/inspection is integration/version/registration/retention bound; long-horizon basis requires verified exported/retained history where native retention is insufficient.

### INTG-237 — Comparative Explanation Source Equivalence
Before comparing Explanation sides, bind equivalent proposition identity, event window, knowledge cut, source-set/coverage and current disclosure; availability/detail differences are not automatically truth changes.

### INTG-238 — Group 07 Source Matrix & Group 08 Handoff
Pass replay/basis/disclosure feasibility forward while preserving source state, as-known Explanation, retained communication and current retrospective interpretation as distinct capabilities.

## Architecture boundary

This contract selects no snapshot persistence, event sourcing, archival store, materialization, retrieval/indexing, citation UI, redaction engine, LLM/template composition, cache invalidation, communication channel or authorization implementation.
