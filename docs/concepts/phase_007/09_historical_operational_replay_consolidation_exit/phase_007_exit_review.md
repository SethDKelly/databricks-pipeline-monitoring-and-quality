## Final operational reasoning structure

Phase 007 establishes a layered operational reasoning model rather than one global incident/control state.

The final structure preserves:

**historical typed Lineage/topology**
→ **exact Change Intent + Deployment realization + realized Change comparison**
→ **prospective blast-radius/change-aware review**
→ **actual execution/opportunity/attempt/sequence/version reconstruction**
→ **bounded Investigation/localization**
→ **explicit Causal Claim handoff/evaluation**
→ **consumer candidate/encounter/exposure/effect/consequence Impact**
→ **Propagation Safeguard protection/enforcement/prevention/release**
→ **Execution Gate criterion/readiness-basis/decision/enforcement/execution separation**
→ **historical replay under event/effective time + knowledge cut**.

No arrow automatically creates the next proposition.

## Historical replay model

The phase exits with three distinct historical views:

1. **actual retained historical state** — what actually happened/was recorded/decided at the historical time;
2. **as-known-at-cut reconstruction** — what can legitimately be reconstructed for event/window `T` using evidence available by cutoff `K`;
3. **current retrospective interpretation** — what is currently concluded about the same historical event using later permitted evidence.

Current authorized projection is applied independently for disclosure and cannot strengthen any of these internal states.

This extends SYN-033/SYN-034 without creating a new historical-state concept.

## Lineage/topology exit check

Lineage is a bounded typed temporal proposition rather than a generic graph edge.

Five minimum operational families remain accepted: `data_derivation`, `production`, `operational_dependency`, `publication`, and `consumption_path`.

Historical replay resolves then-effective topology and then-known relationship evidence. Current topology is never projected backward. Planned topology remains separate from effective topology, and restricted/unknown edges are not converted into absence.

Question-bound relevance remains distinct from reachability, execution, encounter, Impact and causality. Multi-hop relevance composes semantic scope rather than blindly inheriting descendant status.

No graph database, graph query language, universal edge confidence or topology-completeness score is implied.

## Change realization exit check

Change Intent, Deployment and realized Change remain separate truth owners.

The accepted realization chain is:

**exact intent revision/component → evidence-backed Deployment association → attempt/outcome → target/facet activation → realized before/after Change → derived intent-to-realization comparison**.

Temporal/name/repository proximity never proves association. Repository commit is not runtime identity absent evidence. Partial/canary rollout is slice specific. `not evidenced` remains weaker than `not realized`.

Rollback/reversion creates new history rather than erasing the intervening state, and code rollback does not automatically restore data/schema/topology/Impact/health.

Historical replay can improve realization comparison with late evidence while preserving the original intent, deployment actions and what was known then.

## Prospective review exit check

Prospective review uses then-effective Lineage plus explicit planned topology deltas to create a derived scenario only.

Candidate bases can include effective paths, planned-added paths, path-loss/change and indeterminate paths. Candidate/relevance remains below exposure/effect/consequence/cause.

Proposal-bound structural/metric/reference/reconciliation/readiness/control review remains distinct from realized state. Review relevance does not create review obligation, approval, deployment decision or control enforcement.

Later actual topology, Impact or Causal Claim evidence is never backfilled into an earlier prospective review.

## Execution reconstruction exit check

Expected work, execution opportunity, Gate state and actual execution remain independent.

Actual run reconstruction preserves partial lifecycle evidence, attempt/retry/restart/rerun/backfill distinctions, logical execution assembly, actual precedence/waiting, run-specific implementation/input/output version binding, multi-input sets, duplicate/common-derived telemetry and clock limitations.

Effective dependency or schedule order does not prove actual waiting or consumption. Latest upstream output does not prove consumed version. Successful run does not prove qualifying output, currentness, freshness, health or readiness.

Strong `no run`, `no output` and `no consumption` conclusions retain their REF opportunity/coverage burden.

Late run/version/sequence evidence can revise current retrospective reconstruction without fabricating what the earlier knowledge cut knew.

## Investigation and causal exit check

Investigation remains a bounded inquiry, not a causal truth owner.

First observed deviation, earliest evidenced state change, first transformation/reconciliation boundary and first downstream consumer effect remain separate. Search/localization stopping points never become root cause by convenience.

Multiple competing/compatible leads are first-class. Lead exclusion requires bounded negative/discriminating evidence. Lack of support is not exclusion.

Causal language creates an explicit Causal Claim. Investigation priority, closure, remediation or analyst/model confidence does not transfer into Causal Claim status.

`confirmed` remains governed by REF-017 and AUTH-034. Late evidence may reopen an Investigation or alter Causal Claim support/status while preserving historical closure/status history.

No universal RCA/hypothesis/confidence score is accepted.

## Impact exit check

Impact preserves:

**candidate/reachable ≠ encounter opportunity ≠ available/published/served ≠ actual encounter/exposure ≠ downstream effect ≠ consequence ≠ causal attribution**.

Encounter evidence is consumer-mode specific. Safe prior-version use can support `not exposed to suspect V` while still being stale. Exposure is not transitively propagated through multi-hop Lineage, and one safe path does not establish consumer-wide non-exposure.

`not exposed`, `no effect` and `no consequence` retain bounded negative-evidence requirements.

Technical/operational, analytical and business/process consequences remain evidence categories rather than severity scores. Publication is not view, and view is not decision reliance or adverse consequence.

Confirmed upstream causality does not prove every reachable consumer was exposed or affected.

Historical replay allows late consumer evidence to change current retrospective exposure/consequence understanding without rewriting incident-time knowledge.

## Propagation Safeguard exit check

Propagation Safeguard owns bounded output/publication/consumption protection, not general asset health or recovery.

The accepted chain is:

**protected state/surface/path/cohort → proposal/authorization/request → control acceptance/effective enforcement → opportunity-specific protection → REF-028 prevented-exposure determination → extension/expiry/release → independently evidenced post-protection state**.

Proposal/authorization/request does not prove enforcement. One protected path does not prove global protection. `active + not exposed` does not establish prevention without an actual material opportunity and sufficient path/version coverage.

No encounter opportunity can coexist with valid active protection without creating prevention credit. Blocking suspect state can also create stale serving, delay or non-delivery.

Scheduled expiry differs from effective expiry; release request differs from effective release; release differs from recovery.

Late alternate-path/consumer evidence can change retrospective prevention attribution without rewriting the actual Safeguard action/enforcement history.

## Execution Gate exit check

Execution Gate owns start/admission control for a bounded opportunity.

The accepted chain is:

**exact Gate/profile/opportunity → exact criterion + eligible/suitable evidence → readiness result → normal/override/fallback decision basis → decision issuance/delivery/acceptance → effective Gate enforcement → actual execution/non-execution → independently evidenced operational/Impact effects**.

Health/result truth, evidence suitability, readiness, Gate decision, enforcement and execution remain separate.

HOLD and ADMIT remain asymmetric: reliable start during an unsuperseded HOLD contradicts full enforcement, while no run supports HOLD only with sufficient opportunity/run coverage. ADMIT removes this Gate barrier but does not create a run.

Override preserves underlying readiness. Fallback is pre-authorized policy application and remains distinct from override. Timeout is a trigger, escalation is not an admission decision, and control restoration does not automatically reevaluate or ADMIT/HOLD.

Multiple prerequisites and multiple Gate barriers use explicit composition; there is no universal `most restrictive wins` or Gate-effectiveness score.

Historical replay preserves actual Gate decisions even when later evidence changes readiness/enforcement interpretation.

## Gate and Safeguard separation exit check

Execution Gate and Propagation Safeguard remain independent active controls:

- Gate HOLD ≠ Safeguard hold/protection;
- Gate ADMIT ≠ Safeguard release;
- Gate override ≠ Safeguard override/release;
- Safeguard release ≠ Gate ADMIT;
- a Gate-held execution can coexist with old/suspect/unknown published state;
- a Gate-admitted run can produce output that remains safeguarded.

Where the controls overlap, materiality and enforcement remain separately evidenced. No hidden precedence or combined control-state scalar is accepted.

Operational effects such as delay, stale serving, skipped execution and non-delivery remain source-owned facts/Assessments/Impact. Broader causal attribution belongs to Causal Claim.

## Authority, authorization and restricted replay exit check

Phase 005 boundaries remain intact.

Assertion Authority, Capability Authorization, evidence sufficiency, control eligibility and actual enforcement remain separate.

Historical authorization/authority is historical evidence. Current requester authorization governs present disclosure. A current requester cannot obtain restricted historical values merely because a historical actor once had access.

Internal replay can use authorized restricted evidence and produce a safe current projection when permitted, but redaction/opacity cannot be mistaken for absent evidence or declassification.

## Negative-evidence discipline confirmed

Across the completed phase, missing/restricted/unavailable telemetry never becomes zero/false/no-event/no-path/no-exposure/no-effect/no-enforcement.

Strong negatives remain proposition specific and require suitable opportunity/coverage, including:

- no Lineage relationship/path;
- not realized;
- no run/output/consumption;
- lead exclusion;
- not exposed/no effect/no consequence;
- no bypass/alternate path;
- successful HOLD/non-enforcement;
- not known by historical cutoff.

Historical uncertainty is preserved as historical knowledge state even when later evidence resolves the retrospective question.

## Phase 008 handoff

Phase 008 — **Business Questioning and Explanation** is next.

It receives the completed functional substrate from Phases 002–007 and should define how a user asks questions such as:

- What is unhealthy, stale, missing, delayed or uncertain now?
- What changed and what evidence supports that conclusion?
- Where was the first observed/earliest evidenced deviation?
- What causal claims are supported, contradicted, unresolved or confirmed?
- Which consumers were candidates, actually exposed, affected or consequentially impacted?
- What did Safeguard/Gate controls actually do and what remains uncertain?
- What was known at incident time versus what is known retrospectively?

Phase 008 should define question identity/scope/time/audience, authorized analytical projection, explanation structure, statement-to-basis traceability, uncertainty/status communication, progressive result maturity and historical comparison.

It must not let narrative presentation:

- promote candidate to exposure;
- promote localization to cause;
- promote supported claim to confirmed;
- promote control decision to enforcement/outcome;
- promote hidden evidence through unauthorized summarization;
- flatten `what was known then` and `what we know now` into one rewritten story.

Explanation remains a projection over accepted state and evidence, not a new independent truth source.
