# OPS-056 — Reconciliation, Structural & Health Boundary Localization

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 05

## Purpose

Turn Phase 006 structural/health/reconciliation evidence into useful localization without converting a mismatch into causal attribution.

## Contract

A boundary-localization proposition may identify that:

- upstream local evidence met/violated/was unresolved under its own criteria;
- downstream local evidence changed or violated its own criteria;
- a transformation-specific reconciliation first ceased to hold or became unresolved at a named transformation/version;
- structural/interface compatibility changed at a particular boundary;
- a Baseline/comparability regime break limits before/after interpretation.

Localization must bind exact metric/check/reconciliation/Expectation/Baseline versions, population/grain/window and transformation/input/output versions where material.

A reconciliation mismatch can strongly localize where data behavior diverged from an expected relationship but does not by itself identify why the mismatch occurred.

## Invariants

- local violation ≠ upstream/downstream cause.
- reconciliation mismatch ≠ Causal Claim.
- structural incompatibility ≠ causal transmission.
- healthy upstream criterion ≠ proof upstream is irrelevant to every other dimension.
- absent monitoring in a dimension ≠ healthy dimension.
