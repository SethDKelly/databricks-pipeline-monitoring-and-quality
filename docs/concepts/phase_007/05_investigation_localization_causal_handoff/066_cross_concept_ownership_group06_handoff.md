# OPS-066 — Investigation/Causality Cross-Concept Ownership & Group 06 Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 05

## Purpose

Freeze Group 05 ownership boundaries and define the exact input to Impact encounter/exposure refinement.

## Ownership

**Investigation owns:** bounded inquiry identity/question/scope, linked evidence/lead references, localization results, inquiry lifecycle/closure/reopen history and explicit gaps/restrictions.

**Causal Claim owns:** cause→effect proposition, causal role, epistemic status, supporting/contradicting evidence rationale, alternatives, confirmation/rejection provenance and status history.

**Lineage owns:** topology/relationship truth used for candidate discovery.

**Execution History owns:** run/attempt/sequence/version truth used for localization.

**Observation/Assessment/Baseline/Expectation/Change own:** their respective evidence/health/reference/state truths.

**Impact owns:** downstream candidate, actual encounter/exposure, downstream effect and consequence layers.

## Group 06 handoff

Group 06 may consume:

- Investigation question/scope and prospective/retrospective candidate context;
- OPS-054 localization results as context only;
- Causal Claim state where causal attribution is relevant;
- OPS-039/040/047 run-specific input/output/version evidence;
- Lineage consumption/publication paths.

It must independently establish consumer encounter/exposure/effect/consequence. Investigation localization or a supported/confirmed upstream cause does not manufacture downstream exposure.

## Permanent boundaries

- Investigation ≠ evidence truth store.
- localization ≠ Causal Claim.
- Causal Claim ≠ Impact encounter/exposure.
- confirmed upstream cause ≠ every reachable consumer was exposed.
- downstream effect ≠ consequence ≠ causal attribution.
- Group 06 must preserve REF-021–REF-023 exposure/non-exposure evidence burdens.
