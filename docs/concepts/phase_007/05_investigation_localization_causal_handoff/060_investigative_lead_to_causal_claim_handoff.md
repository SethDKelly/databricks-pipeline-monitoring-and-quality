# OPS-060 — Investigative Lead → Explicit Causal Claim Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 05

## Purpose

Define the exact semantic boundary where operational inquiry becomes causal assertion.

## Contract

An Investigation lead remains non-causal while it asks where/when/what changed, which evidence is relevant, or which conditions merit examination.

When the product or an actor asserts that a condition **caused, contributed to, enabled, prevented, triggered or materially influenced** a defined outcome, the proposition must be represented as a **Causal Claim**.

The handoff binds:

- proposed cause condition/event/state/change;
- defined effect/outcome;
- causal role where asserted;
- applicable context/time;
- material mechanism/transmission assumptions;
- evidence references motivating the proposal;
- originating Investigation/lead reference.

The new Causal Claim begins at the strongest REF-014 status justified by its own evaluation; lead priority/localization does not carry over as causal status.

## Invariants

- lead ≠ proposed Causal Claim until a causal proposition is asserted.
- localization result ≠ causal claim.
- `likely place to investigate` ≠ `likely cause` by wording convenience.
- Investigation cannot smuggle causal meaning through labels such as `root`, `culprit`, or `responsible` without Causal Claim semantics.
