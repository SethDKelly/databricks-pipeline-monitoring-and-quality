# OPS-061 — Causal Claim Evaluation & Confirmation Independence from Investigation

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 05

## Purpose

Keep Investigation organization separate from Causal Claim epistemic evolution and high-consequence confirmation authority.

## Contract

Once linked, each Causal Claim is evaluated under REF-013–REF-020 using its own applicable evidence dimensions, alternatives, support, contradiction and exclusion burden.

Investigation may display or organize claim state but does not own transitions among:

- `proposed`;
- `supported`;
- `weakened`;
- `unresolved`;
- `rejected`;
- `confirmed`.

`confirmed` additionally requires the applicable REF-017 confirmation profile plus independently resolved AUTH-034/Capability Authorization and provenance-bearing confirmation action.

Investigation workflow state, analyst consensus, ranking, closure, remediation success or elapsed time cannot substitute for either the evidence gate or confirmation authority.

## Invariants

- leading Investigation theory ≠ supported Causal Claim automatically.
- supported ≠ confirmed.
- reviewer/incident-owner title ≠ confirmation authority.
- operational fix success ≠ confirmation by itself.
- claim status can change after Investigation closure/reopen while preserving history.
