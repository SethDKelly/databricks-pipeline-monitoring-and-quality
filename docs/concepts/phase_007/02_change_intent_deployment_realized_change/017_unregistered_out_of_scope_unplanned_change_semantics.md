# OPS-017 — Unregistered, Outside-Declared-Scope & Unplanned Change Semantics

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 02

## Purpose

Avoid overstating organizational intent or process failure merely because realized Deployment/Change evidence cannot be matched to a registered Change Intent.

## Vocabulary

### No matching registered intent known
Under the selected event window/knowledge cut, the framework has no sufficiently linked Change Intent for the bounded Deployment/Change proposition.

This is an epistemic statement, not proof that no person intended the change.

### Unregistered deployment/change
A bounded deployment/change has no matching registered intent in the monitored registration universe under adequate identity/time search. This still does not by itself mean unauthorized, improper or humanly unplanned.

### Outside declared intent scope
Sufficient intent-scope and realized-state evidence establishes that a realized target/facet/effect lies outside the matched intent's declared scope.

This is stronger than missing anticipated-effect detail but remains descriptive.

### Unplanned
Use `unplanned` only when the proposition actually means that under an applicable planning/governance process the realized change lacked the required prior plan, and evidence/coverage/authority are sufficient to support that conclusion.

Group 02 does not infer this stronger process conclusion from absence alone.

## Undeclared side effects

A realized effect not listed among anticipated effects is `not declared/anticipated in the registered intent` unless evidence establishes the actor actually intended or did not intend it. The framework does not claim human mental state from documentation silence.

## Invariants

- no matching registered intent known ≠ no human intent;
- unregistered ≠ unauthorized;
- outside declared scope ≠ defective;
- undeclared effect ≠ proven unintended effect;
- unplanned process conclusion requires an applicable planning rule plus sufficient evidence/coverage;
- realized Change remains valid even when planned context is missing;
- absence of intent cannot erase Deployment/Lineage/Change evidence.

## Handoff

Group 03 can use registered proposed scope prospectively, but must preserve missing/partial planning context rather than treating it as zero blast radius or wrongdoing.
