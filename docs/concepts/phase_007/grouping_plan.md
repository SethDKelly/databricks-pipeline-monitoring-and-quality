# Phase 007 Grouping Decision Summary

**Status:** Accepted planning artifact

Phase 007 is decomposed into nine dependency-ordered functional design groups:

1. Lineage Relationship Taxonomy, Historical Topology & Operational Relevance.
2. Change Intent, Deployment Realization & Realized Change.
3. Prospective Blast Radius & Change-Aware Review.
4. Execution Reconstruction, Dependency Sequence & Version Use.
5. Investigation Lifecycle, First-Deviation Localization & Causal Handoff.
6. Impact, Consumer Encounter, Exposure & Consequence.
7. Propagation Safeguard Scope, Enforcement, Release & Recovery.
8. Execution Gate, Fallback/Override & Control-Induced Operational Effects.
9. Historical Operational Replay & Consolidation / Exit Review.

Phase 007 will use `OPS-###` refinement contracts when substantive group work starts. No OPS contract is accepted by this grouping artifact.

The grouping is intentionally functional and implementation-neutral. It does not imply service boundaries, repositories, deployment units, data stores, event schemas, graph models, scheduler topology or control-plane implementation.
