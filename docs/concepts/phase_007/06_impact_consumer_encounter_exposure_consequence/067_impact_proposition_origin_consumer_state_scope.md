# OPS-067 — Impact Proposition: Origin, Consumer, State & Scope

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 06

## Purpose

Make every realized Impact inquiry precise enough that candidate, encounter, effect and consequence evidence refer to the same bounded originating condition and downstream use context.

## Contract

A realized Impact proposition binds, where material:

- originating subject/condition and exact affected/suspect state, output, version or bounded window;
- downstream candidate/consumer identity and relevant use/interface/population scope;
- historical relationship/path context that makes encounter possible;
- encounter mode or boundary being evaluated;
- event/effective-time window and evaluation/knowledge cut;
- whether the question concerns reachability, actual encounter, downstream effect, consequence or causal attribution;
- evidence/authorization limitations.

An Impact record may organize several layers for one bounded origin/consumer pair, but one layer cannot manufacture another.

## Invariants

- generic `consumer affected` is insufficient when the affected state/use/time is ambiguous.
- prospective candidate identity may seed the realized inquiry but does not prove realized encounter.
- current consumer/path state is not projected backward onto an incident-time proposition.
- Impact remains a downstream-analysis concept, not an originating health or causal truth owner.
