# OPS-085 — Impact Cross-Concept Ownership & Group 07 Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 06

## Ownership

- **Lineage** owns historical relationship/path propositions and bounded completeness.
- **Execution History** owns actual execution/input/output/version reconstruction.
- **Observation / Assessment / Change** own downstream measured/interpreted/realized state.
- **Impact** owns downstream candidate/reachability, encounter/exposure resolution and provenance-bearing effect/consequence associations.
- **Investigation** owns inquiry organization/localization only.
- **Causal Claim** owns origin→effect/consequence attribution.
- **Semantic Definition / Responsibility Assignment / Classification / Policy Context** provide context without proving Impact.
- **Propagation Safeguard** owns protection configuration/lifecycle/enforcement state, not exposure truth.

## Group 07 handoff

Group 07 receives from Group 06:

- exact suspect/affected state/version/window;
- relevant consumer/path/encounter-mode propositions;
- known encounter opportunities;
- exposed/not-exposed/safe/unknown path results and limitations;
- material alternate encounter paths;
- downstream delay/staleness/non-delivery/effect/consequence evidence where present;
- historical/authorization constraints.

To claim **prevented exposure**, Group 07 must establish materially operative Safeguard enforcement over the relevant path/opportunity plus sufficient negative encounter/alternate-path evidence. `Safeguard active + no observed exposure` is insufficient.

## Invariants

- Impact does not own Safeguard enforcement.
- Safeguard enforcement does not rewrite Impact evidence.
- prevented exposure ≠ healthy/current delivery.
- safeguard-induced consequence remains Impact/health evidence and requires Causal Claim if attributed to the safeguard.
