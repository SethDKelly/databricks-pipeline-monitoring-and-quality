# Causal Evaluation Architecture

## Canonical proposition

A Causal Claim binds exact cause, effect, role, mechanism/transmission assumptions, subject/population, event/effective interval, status and evidence/authority context.

Accepted causal roles remain distinct: caused, contributed, enabled, triggered, prevented, materially influenced and other explicitly defined roles.

## Status state machine

Allowed statuses:

- `proposed`;
- `supported`;
- `weakened`;
- `unresolved`;
- `rejected`;
- `confirmed`.

Status transitions are appended with rule revision, basis, evaluator origin and authority information. Current status is derived from the non-rewriting journal.

## Evidence evaluation

Evidence roles are claim-relative. Timing, Lineage, run/version contrast, reconciliation, intervention/remediation and consumer effects can support or contradict a claim, but none is causality by category alone.

Basis count or source count does not create confidence because duplicate/common-derived evidence is not independent.

## Rejection

`rejected` requires evidence that contradicts or excludes the exact claim under adequate opportunity/coverage. Lack of support generally remains `unresolved` or may weaken the claim depending on the accepted rule.

## Confirmation

`confirmed` requires both:

1. REF-017 evidence sufficiency for the exact causal proposition; and
2. AUTH-034 Assertion Authority eligibility for the confirming actor/source/context.

A model cannot invoke a privileged confirmation path. Investigation owners, admins, remediation owners and reviewers have no automatic confirmation authority.

## Counterfactuals

What-if/counterfactual results are analytical artifacts with their own assumptions. They may inform a lead or claim but cannot be recorded as realized history or confirmed causal evidence.

## No causal score

Group 06 introduces no probability, confidence, root-cause rank or single winning cause requirement. Multiple compatible contributors and unresolved alternatives remain valid.
