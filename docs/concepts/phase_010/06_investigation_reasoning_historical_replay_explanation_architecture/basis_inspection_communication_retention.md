# Basis Inspection, Communication and Retention

## inspectBasis

`inspectBasis` is a separately authorized action over one or more Statement IR records.

For each internal basis item the visible projection may be:

- exact;
- coarse;
- redacted;
- opaque reference;
- withheld.

Internal traceability remains complete regardless of projection.

## Independent states

The following are not synonyms:

- basis reference visible;
- source/basis still resolvable;
- payload retained/available;
- requester authorized to inspect;
- exact detail authorized;
- source itself currently accessible.

## Historical basis view

The exact basis projection shown to a historical requester is recoverable only from an authentic retained snapshot/projection. Current authorization cannot recreate earlier disclosure.

## Explanation snapshot

A communicated Explanation snapshot binds exact rendered/canonical content, Statement IR identities, limitations, audience/requester/purpose/delivery context, projection/authorization decision, communication time and renderer/prompt/model version when relevant.

## Communication evidence

Composition, review, approval, release, delivery, receipt/read and reliance are separate events. A delivery log without a content-bound snapshot proves delivery evidence, not necessarily exact wording.

## Refresh and supersession

A refreshed Explanation creates a new instance linked to its predecessor with typed deltas. Prior authentic communication remains immutable historical evidence.

## Retention and noise/cost

Explanation snapshots and exact basis are pinned only for the retention promise that justified them. Routine model traces, intermediate candidates or rendered drafts are not automatically retained forever.

Retention may age non-material operational artifacts under Group 02 lifecycle policy while preserving the exact statement/basis/communication identity needed for promised replay, audit or incident use.
