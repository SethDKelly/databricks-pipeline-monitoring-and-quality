## Consolidation conclusion

No contradiction requires reopening SYN, REF, AUTH, HLTH, OPS, EXPL or INTG semantics, and no new technical proposition is required to reconcile ARCH-001–ARCH-500.

The architecture remains internally consistent when deployed under one governing rule:

**optimization may rearrange implementation work and physical components, but it may not collapse canonical identity, authority, time, evidence, coverage, causality, disclosure, historical replay or active-control distinctions.**
