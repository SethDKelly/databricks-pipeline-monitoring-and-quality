# INTG-118 — Health-Source Conflict & Composition

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 009 Group 04

DQX, Lakeflow expectations, anomaly detection, data profiling, Metric Views, structural metadata and independent queries may legitimately disagree because they evaluate different propositions, rules, windows or source versions.

No newest/strictest/vendor-native/source-count winner is implicit. Same-proposition conflicts follow accepted authority/evidence rules; different propositions remain sibling Assessments rather than forced conflict.
