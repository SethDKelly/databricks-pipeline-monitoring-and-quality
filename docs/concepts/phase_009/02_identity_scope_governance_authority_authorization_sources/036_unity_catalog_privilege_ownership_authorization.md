# INTG-036 — Unity Catalog Privilege & Ownership Authorization

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 009 Group 02

Unity Catalog privileges, inheritance, ownership and workspace bindings are authoritative inputs for current authorization to Unity Catalog securables within their documented scope.

They do not establish framework capabilities unrelated to those securables, and metadata visibility surfaces may be principal-filtered. Current grants do not reconstruct historical grants without qualifying audit/history evidence.
