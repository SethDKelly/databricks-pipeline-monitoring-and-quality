# INTG-030 — Semantic-Facet Source Ownership

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 009 Group 02

Semantic Definition capability is evaluated by facet: business definition, technical description, grain, units, population/calculation meaning, field role and key role may come from different source surfaces.

A source authoritative for one facet does not inherit authority for another. Current availability of comments or descriptions does not establish semantic authority without an accepted AUTH-009 rule.
