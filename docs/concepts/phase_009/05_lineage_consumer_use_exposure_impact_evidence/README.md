# Phase 009 Group 05 — Lineage, Consumer Use, Exposure, Effect & Impact Evidence

**Status:** Next — not started

## Goal

Map concrete topology and downstream-consumption evidence to Lineage and the Impact ladder from candidate through encounter, exposure, downstream effect and consequence.

## Group 04 entry contract

Group 05 may consume exact realized-structure Observations, metric/check results, normative Assessments, Baseline/drift context, reconciliation results, composite-health components and freshness/current-cycle evidence **only where INTG-084–INTG-119 establish them**.

Material Group 04 limitations remain attached: observer-relative schema visibility, consumer-specific compatibility gaps, rule/metric-definition version requirements, source-specific scan/result timing, learned/vendor status semantics, Group 03 exact-input/current-cycle gaps and bounded historical retention.

No health/quality source automatically establishes downstream use. A failed DQX/Lakeflow rule, Databricks anomaly, structural incompatibility, drift result or composite-health state may define an originating condition for Impact analysis but does not prove publication, encounter, exposure, downstream effect, consequence or cause.

## Primary questions

- Which Unity Catalog or other sources provide typed, directed, effective/historical Lineage and at what granularity?
- What relationship families/field/key/population/version details are observable versus unavailable?
- How are planned topology deltas represented without becoming effective Lineage?
- Which sources establish publication/availability and actual consumer query/read/refresh/use?
- Can consumer evidence bind the exact version/state consumed, or only the target object?
- What path, consumer-mode and population coverage exists for exposure and non-exposure conclusions?
- Which evidence can establish downstream metric/quality effect rather than mere exposure?
- Which sources can establish technical, analytical or business consequence, and what remains human/organizational evidence?
- How do cache, extract, stale-safe-state and indirect/multi-hop consumption affect coverage?
- How do Group 03 consumed-version gaps and Group 04 measurement/version gaps constrain exact exposure/effect attribution?
- What retention/latency gaps limit historical Impact replay?

## External-fact requirement

Verify current Unity Catalog table/column lineage, query history/access/audit, dashboard/BI/query usage and any other evaluated consumer-use surfaces. Record exact granularity, source identity, version binding, permission, retention, coverage and known blind spots.

## Boundary

Lineage candidate/reachability ≠ publication ≠ encounter ≠ exposure ≠ effect ≠ consequence ≠ causal attribution. Missing consumer telemetry is not non-exposure. Health/quality status is an originating-state proposition, not evidence that a consumer saw or was affected by it.

Group 06 uses the established operational/Impact evidence envelope to evaluate Investigation, causality and control evidence.
