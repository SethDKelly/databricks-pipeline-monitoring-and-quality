# Phase 009 Group 05 — Lineage, Consumer Use, Exposure, Effect & Impact Evidence

**Status:** Not started

## Goal

Map concrete topology and downstream-consumption evidence to Lineage and the Impact ladder from candidate through encounter, exposure, downstream effect and consequence.

## Primary questions

- Which Unity Catalog or other sources provide typed, directed, effective/historical Lineage and at what granularity?
- What relationship families/field/key/population/version details are observable versus unavailable?
- How are planned topology deltas represented without becoming effective Lineage?
- Which sources establish publication/availability and actual consumer query/read/refresh/use?
- Can consumer evidence bind the exact version/state consumed, or only the target object?
- What path, consumer-mode and population coverage exists for exposure and non-exposure conclusions?
- Which evidence can establish downstream metric/quality effect rather than mere exposure?
- Which sources can establish technical, analytical or business consequence, and what remains human/organizational evidence?
- How do cache, extract, stale-safe-state and indirect/multi-hop consumption affect coverage?
- What retention/latency gaps limit historical Impact replay?

## Boundary

Lineage candidate/reachability ≠ publication ≠ encounter ≠ exposure ≠ effect ≠ consequence ≠ causal attribution. Missing consumer telemetry is not non-exposure.

## Handoff

Group 06 uses the established operational/Impact evidence envelope to evaluate Investigation, causality and control evidence.
