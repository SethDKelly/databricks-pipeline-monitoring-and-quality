# INTG-152 — Vendor Impact/Popularity Context ≠ Realized Impact

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 009 Group 05

Databricks lineage impact-analysis views, table popularity/insights and anomaly-monitoring downstream-impact fields can identify candidates, observed query activity or source-owned importance context.

They do not by themselves establish suspect-state exposure, downstream effect, business consequence, causal attribution or universal severity.
