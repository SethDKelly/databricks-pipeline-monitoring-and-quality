# INTG-006 — Access, Retrieval & Disclosure Capability

Technical retrievability, principal access, internal analytical use and onward disclosure are separate integration dimensions.

A surface may be technically available to the framework while exact values, identities, policy text, annotations, raw rows, consumer details or basis metadata remain restricted for a requester or delivery context.

`restricted`, `redacted`, `permission denied` and `not retrievable` do not mean the underlying proposition is false or absent.

Contracts must record material permission dependencies and whether coarse/redacted/opaque projection is supported without treating aggregation as automatic declassification.
