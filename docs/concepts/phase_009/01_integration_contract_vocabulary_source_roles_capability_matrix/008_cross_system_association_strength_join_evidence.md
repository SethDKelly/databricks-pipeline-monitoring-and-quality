# INTG-008 — Cross-System Association Strength & Join Evidence

Cross-system relationships such as intent↔deployment, deployment↔run, run↔implementation revision, run↔input/output version, consumer↔query or control decision↔enforcement require explicit association evidence.

A contract records the available join mechanism and its semantics: shared immutable identifier, provenance-bearing mapping, deterministic compound key, bounded reconciliation evidence, or weaker heuristic candidate.

Heuristic name/time/repository proximity may produce an Investigation lead or unresolved association candidate but cannot satisfy an exact association proposition.

Association strength is proposition specific; it is not a universal confidence score.
