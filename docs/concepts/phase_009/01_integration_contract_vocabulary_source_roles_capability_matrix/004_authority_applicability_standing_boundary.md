# INTG-004 — Authority Applicability & Standing Boundary

Authority is proposition/category/context/time specific and remains owned by the accepted Assertion Authority model.

A capability row records whether the surface can carry an assertion whose standing is known to be authoritative, co-authoritative, advisory, explicitly non-authoritative, or not yet resolved for the bounded proposition.

Availability, product prominence, synchronization order, source recency, specificity, source count and technical ownership do not establish standing.

Where authority depends on an external rule/configuration, the contract must identify that dependency rather than hard-code universal source precedence.
