# Phase 005 Group 07 — Consolidation / Exit Review

**Status:** Next review group — not yet started

## Goal

Compose Phase 005 Groups 01–06 across representative ecosystem scenarios and determine whether governance/authority/capability/disclosure semantics are coherent without stealing evidence truth or selecting architecture.

## Accepted handoff from Groups 01–06

- Assertion Authority is the 24th concept and AUTH-001–AUTH-053 are accepted;
- authority remains category/facet/context/time scoped with explicit conflict/fallback and no hidden source precedence;
- semantic/schema, responsibility, Classification/criticality, and Policy Context authority remain distinct;
- normative metric/schema/threshold/severity/waiver/control-use governance remains separate from Phase 006 health/statistical truth;
- Capability Authorization remains exact principal/capability/subject/context/time permission truth and supports restricted analytical projection;
- high-consequence proposal/approval/issuance/enforcement/outcome remain separate, with explicit delegation, multi-party, break-glass, automation, and outage semantics;
- disclosure is audience/purpose/context/delivery scoped, result visibility can differ from basis visibility, and abstraction must preserve proposition meaning;
- technical/business/executive/audit projections remain views over one truth;
- high-consequence communication review does not create truth;
- historical disclosure/authorization/authority remains non-rewriting and current authorization governs present disclosure.

## Exit checks

- authority is scoped by category/context/time rather than universal;
- authoritative, advisory, conflicting, corrected, superseded, unknown, unavailable, and conditional states remain representable;
- semantic/governance authority does not imply access/control/disclosure authority;
- metric/Expectation authority does not define Phase 006 statistical/metric/schema-health meaning;
- Capability Authorization remains independent from Responsibility Assignment, Policy Context, Assertion Authority, and source availability;
- causal-confirmation/control permission never weakens Phase 004 evidence requirements;
- action authorization/approval remains separate from issuance/enforcement/outcome;
- break-glass/automation/delegation never become universal permission or evidence shortcuts;
- restricted-data RCA remains useful without declassification or inference leakage;
- technical and business disclosure remain projections over one truth and preserve epistemic/control status;
- view permission remains distinct from publish/high-consequence communication permission;
- historical authority/authorization/disclosure remains reconstructable without granting current access;
- no IAM, approval engine, metric engine, vendor-source monopoly, scheduler, graph, causal, LLM, redaction, or persistence architecture is selected.

## Consolidation scenarios to replay

- A+B→C metric/schema health with different business/technical authorities and restricted raw data;
- authoritative threshold conflict plus bounded waiver;
- schema change affecting metric/Baseline applicability and downstream Lineage;
- restricted RCA with opaque upstream contributor and multiple supported causes;
- confirmed causal claim with restricted confirmer/evidence detail;
- gated execution with multi-party override and later enforcement evidence;
- safeguard activation/release with business-facing communication review;
- break-glass during authorization-source outage;
- technical versus executive/client projection of the same incident;
- historical retained Explanation versus present reconstructed/retrospective Explanation.

## Phase exit direction

Phase 005 exits only when Groups 01–06 compose consistently and leave Phase 006 free to refine health/metric/schema/timing semantics using explicit normative authority and disclosure constraints rather than hidden precedence or architecture.
