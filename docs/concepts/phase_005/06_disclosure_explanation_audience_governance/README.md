# Phase 005 Group 06 — Disclosure, Explanation & Audience Governance

**Status:** Planned — not yet started

## Goal

Govern what authorized audiences may learn from otherwise valid ecosystem truth without creating different truth models for technical and business users.

## Primary review questions

- disclosure of metrics/thresholds, Policy Context, Lineage, Causal Claims, Impact, safeguards, gates, Annotations, and business consequences;
- acknowledgment of restricted entities/paths while keeping details opaque;
- technical detailed diagnostic views versus business semantic health projections;
- high-consequence statements requiring additional review before business/client-facing Explanation;
- safe redaction/abstraction without inference leakage or false absence;
- historical authorization/disclosure detail and current requester limits.

## Boundaries

This group governs permission and review constraints. Phase 006 defines health/metric semantics and Phase 008 defines detailed Explanation/question UX. Explanation remains a projection over authorized truth, not an independent truth source.

## Exit direction

Group 06 exits when disclosure rules can support both detailed engineering analysis and appropriately abstracted business communication without declassification by inference.
