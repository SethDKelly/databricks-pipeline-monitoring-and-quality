# Group 04 — History, Lineage & Change

**Status:** Candidate review group

## Goal

Preserve the temporal and topological evidence needed to answer **what ran**, **what was deployed**, **what depended on what**, and **what changed**.

## Concepts

- [Execution History](execution_history.md)
- [Deployment](deployment.md)
- [Lineage](lineage.md)
- [Change](change.md)

## Critical distinction

These concepts describe evidence and relationships. They can make a potential cause plausible or implausible, but they do not establish causation by themselves.

## Group exit gate

Advance when the ecosystem can reconstruct relevant execution/deployment/topology/change context at an incident time without relying only on current state.
