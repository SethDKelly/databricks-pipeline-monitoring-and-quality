# EXPL-116 — Mosaic, Differencing & Repeated-Query Inference Risk

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Projection safety is evaluated across materially composable disclosures, not only one field or one answer in isolation.

Permitted counts, ranges, timestamps, path summaries, status combinations, role labels, successive refreshes or repeated narrowly varied questions can together reveal restricted identity/state. AUTH-047 therefore remains binding for Explanation composition and `inspectBasis`.

Group 06 does not choose privacy-budget, query-history or redaction technology.
