# EXPL-105 — Safe Abstraction Modes

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

An authorized projection may expose, where separately permitted and semantically valid:

- exact value/state/detail;
- coarser category or bounded range;
- redacted detail;
- authorized alias/generalized identity;
- opaque existence;
- explicit restriction/visibility limitation;
- or no disclosure of the item.

These are projection choices, not new epistemic states. Aggregation, rounding, redaction or generalization is never automatic declassification.
