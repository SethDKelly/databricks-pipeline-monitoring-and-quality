# EXPL-108 — Value, Threshold, Schema & Metric Abstraction

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

A viewer may be permitted to receive an Assessment outcome while exact metric values, sensitive columns, Baseline parameters, thresholds or structural details remain restricted.

Any coarser wording must preserve the governing distinction: `Expectation violated` may remain visible without the threshold; it cannot be converted into `severe failure`, `near miss`, `healthy`, or a fabricated range unless those propositions/details are independently supported and authorized.
