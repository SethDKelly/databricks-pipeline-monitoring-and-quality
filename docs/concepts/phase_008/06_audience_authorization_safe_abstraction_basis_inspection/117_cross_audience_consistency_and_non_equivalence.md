# EXPL-117 — Cross-Audience Consistency Without Identical Detail

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Technical, business, executive, audit and other authorized views may differ in terminology, included statements, granularity and visible basis. They are not required to have identical sentence counts or evidence detail.

Where two audiences are permitted to receive the same bounded proposition at the same temporal perspective, its underlying status/polarity cannot intentionally differ for presentation convenience.

Different visible scope caused by authorization must be explicit enough to avoid treating two differently scoped projections as contradictory truth.
