# EXPL-097 — Material Limitation and Scope Language

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

A limitation is material when omitting it would broaden, strengthen or mis-scope the answer.

Where relevant, state the bounded population/path/time/consumer/profile/version/knowledge-cut scope and the missing/conflicting/restricted/coverage limitation that constrains the conclusion.

Do not bury a material limitation in detail while the headline implies an unqualified answer.
