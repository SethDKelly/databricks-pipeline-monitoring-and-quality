# Phase 008 Group 05 — Uncertainty, Conflict, Negative Claims & Epistemic Language

**Status:** Review complete — accepted

## Goal

Refine precise Explanation language for unknown, conflicting, unavailable, restricted, insufficient, stale, non-comparable, not-applicable, negative-evidence and competing-claim states without inventing a universal confidence score or reassuring narrative completion.

## Group result

Group 05 accepts **EXPL-081–EXPL-100** and **UNC05-01–UNC05-40**.

No new concept is required. Group 05 is a communication refinement over source-owned REF/AUTH/HLTH/OPS states; it creates neither a universal uncertainty concept nor a confidence model.

The central epistemic-language chain is:

**bounded material proposition → source-owned result/status + applicable evidence condition → exact limitation/negative-evidence classification → proposition-faithful wording → material scope/coverage qualification → sibling-safe composition → historical knowledge-cut labeling → Group 06 authorized-projection handoff**.

## Accepted contracts

- **EXPL-081–EXPL-086** — epistemic-state preservation; unknown/unavailable/insufficient/conflict/restricted distinctions.
- **EXPL-087–EXPL-091** — stale/non-comparable/not-applicable/not-evidenced/negative/positive calibration.
- **EXPL-092–EXPL-095** — causal, Impact, execution/control and governance-specific language.
- **EXPL-096–EXPL-099** — competing claims, material limitations, no cross-statement confidence aggregation and historical evolution.
- **EXPL-100** — Group 06 handoff.

## Durable rules

- unknown/unresolved ≠ false/absent/safe;
- unavailable evidence ≠ negative evidence;
- insufficient evidence/indeterminate ≠ weak positive or weak negative;
- conflict ≠ permission to choose a convenient winner;
- restricted/redacted ≠ unavailable/absent;
- stale-for-use ≠ violation by default;
- non-comparable ≠ not applicable;
- not evidenced/not recorded/not known by cutoff ≠ did not occur;
- absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence;
- strong negatives preserve REF opportunity/coverage burdens;
- positive statements use source status rather than invented certainty/probability language;
- supported causal claim ≠ confirmed; rejected requires evidence; confirmed remains REF-017 + AUTH-034 governed;
- Impact unknown/non-exposure/no-effect/no-consequence remain different states;
- degraded/missing control telemetry ≠ fail-open/fail-closed proof;
- missing governance metadata never silently becomes benign/default state;
- competing claims remain multiple unless accepted evidence/authority resolves them;
- material limitations cannot be hidden behind an unqualified headline;
- sibling statements retain independent epistemic states;
- no universal confidence/probability/completeness/RCA/answer-quality score is accepted;
- as-known-at-cut epistemic state ≠ current retrospective resolution.

## Boundary with Group 06

Group 05 determines faithful epistemic wording over the full internally valid answer. Group 06 determines what an audience is currently authorized to receive and how restricted detail can be safely abstracted without changing the underlying proposition/status.

## Architecture boundary

Do not choose confidence algorithms, probabilistic models, score calibration, LLM hedging prompts, UI badges, color scales, alert severities, citation widgets, data-source precedence, authorization systems or technical architecture.
