# EXPL-089 — Not Evidenced / Not Recorded / Not Known Language

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Preserve distinctions among:

- no qualifying evidence currently establishes the proposition (`not evidenced`);
- no qualifying record was observed within a bounded recording/coverage claim (`not recorded`, when supported);
- the proposition was not known by a historical knowledge cut (`not known by cutoff`, when supported).

None automatically establishes that the real-world event/state did not occur or exist.
