# EXPL-082 — Unknown / Unresolved Scope Language

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Use `unknown` or `unresolved` only relative to the exact proposition and scope that cannot presently be resolved.

A known execution fact may coexist with unknown output version, unresolved cause, unknown exposure for one consumer and established exposure for another. Explanation must not upgrade sibling unknowns from a resolved neighboring fact.

`Unknown` never means false, absent, safe, healthy, unrestricted, not exposed, not causal or not enforced.
