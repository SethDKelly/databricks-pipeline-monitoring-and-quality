# EXPL-100 — Group 06 Authorized-Projection Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Define the epistemic-language state Group 06 receives before audience-specific authorization and safe abstraction.

For every material statement, Group 06 may rely on Group 05 to preserve:

- exact proposition/scope/time/knowledge perspective;
- source-owner status vocabulary;
- unknown/unavailable/insufficient/conflicting/restricted/stale/non-comparable/not-applicable distinctions where relevant;
- evidence-backed negative versus absence-of-evidence distinction;
- material limitation/coverage language;
- sibling-statement epistemic independence;
- historical as-known versus retrospective status.

## Boundary

Group 06 may reduce disclosed detail when authorized, but it may not use redaction/abstraction to strengthen, simplify away or reverse these states. It owns projection semantics, not truth or epistemic reevaluation.
