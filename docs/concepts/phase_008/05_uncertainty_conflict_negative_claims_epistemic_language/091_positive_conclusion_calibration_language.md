# EXPL-091 — Positive Conclusion Calibration Language

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Positive conclusions must use the source concept's accepted status and scope rather than generic certainty language.

Examples: `Assessment meets`, `consumer exposed`, `Gate HOLD enforced for this opportunity`, `Causal Claim supported`, `authorization permitted`, `authoritative standing resolved`.

Do not append unsupported probability/confidence adjectives such as `probably`, `very likely`, `certain` or numeric percentages unless a future accepted source proposition explicitly owns such a measure.
