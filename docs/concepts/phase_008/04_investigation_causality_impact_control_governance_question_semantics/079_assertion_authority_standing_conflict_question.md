# EXPL-079 — Assertion Authority, Standing & Conflict Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `which definition/owner/classification/policy/expectation is authoritative?` bind the exact authority target/facet/scope/context/time.

Authority determines governance standing, not factual correctness, evidence sufficiency, causality, compliance, enforcement or Capability Authorization.

Preserve advisory, authoritative, co-authoritative conflict, authority-rule conflict, unknown/unavailable and explicit precedence/fallback semantics. Recency, source count, sync order, specificity or title do not create hidden authority.
