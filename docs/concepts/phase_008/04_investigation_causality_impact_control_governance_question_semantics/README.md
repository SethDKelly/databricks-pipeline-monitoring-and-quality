# Phase 008 Group 04 — Investigation, Causality, Impact, Control & Governance Question Semantics

**Status:** Next — not started

## Goal

Refine `why`, root-cause, downstream Impact/exposure, Safeguard/Gate, responsibility, policy/meaning and capability questions while preserving Investigation, Causal Claim, Impact, control, governance and authorization ownership.

## Accepted input

Consumes **EXPL-001–EXPL-049**, including exact question scope/time, statement/basis traceability, and the direct health/change/execution propositions established in Group 03.

## Primary questions

- How should `why`, `root cause`, contributor and unresolved-cause questions preserve Investigation versus Causal Claim?
- How should reachable/candidate consumers, encounter/exposure, downstream effect, consequence and causal attribution remain separate in business answers?
- How should Safeguard and Gate questions distinguish configuration/proposal/authorization/decision from effective enforcement and realized effect?
- How should responsibility, policy/meaning, Classification and capability questions avoid becoming fault/compliance/authorization shortcuts?
- How should cross-domain answers present competing causes, control overlap and business consequence without inventing a universal RCA/Impact/control state?

## Key boundary

No question wording may turn Lineage, timing, intent, reachability, control overlap, responsibility or policy context into causal truth, Impact truth, authority or authorization by implication.
