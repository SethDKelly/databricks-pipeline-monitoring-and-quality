# EXPL-064 — Origin→Effect/Consequence Causal Attribution Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

When an answer states that an upstream/change/control/origin caused, contributed to, prevented or materially influenced a downstream effect or consequence, the statement is a Causal Claim even when Impact evidence establishes exposure/effect/consequence.

Confirmed upstream causality does not manufacture consumer encounter/effect/consequence, and realized consumer consequence does not identify its cause by adjacency.

Keep Impact truth and causal attribution independently traceable.
