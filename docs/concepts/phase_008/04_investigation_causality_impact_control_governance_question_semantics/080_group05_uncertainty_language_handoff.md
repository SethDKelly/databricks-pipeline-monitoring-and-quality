# EXPL-080 — Group 05 Uncertainty/Epistemic-Language Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Group 05 receives independently bounded inferential/governance statements for:

- Investigation lifecycle/leads/localization;
- Causal Claim role/status/competition/negative conclusions;
- Impact candidate/exposure/effect/consequence/aggregation;
- Safeguard/Gate lifecycle/enforcement/prevention/control effects;
- responsibility, semantics, Classification, Policy Context, Capability Authorization and Assertion Authority.

Each statement retains source owner, proposition scope, temporal/knowledge context, basis roles, limitations, conflicts, negative-evidence burden and authorization context.

## Boundary

Group 05 may refine how unknown/conflicting/unavailable/restricted/insufficient/not-applicable/negative states are communicated. It may not create new truth states, confidence probabilities, causal rankings, control-effectiveness scores or reassuring negatives.

EXPL-050–EXPL-080 are sufficient for inferential/governance question semantics without a new concept.
