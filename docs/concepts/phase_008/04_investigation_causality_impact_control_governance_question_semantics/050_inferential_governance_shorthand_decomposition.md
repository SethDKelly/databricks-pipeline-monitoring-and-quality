# EXPL-050 — Inferential & Governance Shorthand Decomposition

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Resolve broad inferential/governance wording into exact accepted propositions before answering.

Terms such as `why`, `root cause`, `affected`, `protected`, `blocked`, `worked`, `owner`, `responsible`, `policy`, `compliant`, `authorized`, `allowed`, and `authoritative` are routing cues only.

A compound request decomposes when it asks materially different Investigation, Causal Claim, Impact, Safeguard, Gate, semantic/governance, authority, responsibility, or capability propositions.

## Invariants

- wording does not create a truth category;
- one resolved subquestion cannot strengthen another;
- presentation convenience never merges cause, Impact, control, responsibility, policy, authority or permission.
