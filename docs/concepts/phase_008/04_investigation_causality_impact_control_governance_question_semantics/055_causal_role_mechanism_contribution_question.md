# EXPL-055 — Causal Role, Mechanism & Contribution Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Preserve the causal role actually asserted/evaluated, including as applicable `caused`, `contributed to`, `enabled`, `triggered`, `prevented`, or `materially influenced`.

Do not translate every supported contributor into `the cause` or every enabling condition into a direct cause.

Where mechanism/transmission assumptions are material to the Causal Claim, they remain visible in statement basis/limitations.
