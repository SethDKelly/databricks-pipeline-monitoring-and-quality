# EXPL-056 — Causal Claim Status & Confirmation Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions about whether cause is `known`, `proven`, `confirmed`, `supported`, `rejected`, or unresolved must project the exact accepted Causal Claim status and its relevant evidence/authority limitations.

`confirmed` remains REF-017 + AUTH-034 governed. Investigation closure, remediation success, analyst consensus, source count, model confidence, seniority or urgency cannot promote a claim to confirmed.

Narrative words such as `likely` must not invent a probability state not owned by the claim/evidence model.
