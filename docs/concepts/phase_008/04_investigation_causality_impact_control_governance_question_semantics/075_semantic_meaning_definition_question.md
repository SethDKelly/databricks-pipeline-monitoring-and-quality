# EXPL-075 — Semantic Meaning & Definition Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `what does this table/metric mean?`, `what population does it represent?`, or `what is the grain/unit?` route to Semantic Definition for the requested facet/context/time and applicable Assertion Authority standing.

Technical description, business definition, governed schema meaning, grain, unit, population and calculation meaning can be distinct facets.

Semantic meaning does not establish health, realized physical state, policy applicability or authorization.
