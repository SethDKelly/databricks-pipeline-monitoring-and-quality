# EXPL-052 — Investigation Lead, Candidate & Disposition Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions about `what are the leading theories?`, `what are we looking at?`, or `what has been ruled out?` preserve Investigation lead state and its evidence basis/disposition.

A lead is not a hidden Causal Claim. `Examining`, `supported for further inquiry`, `weakened`, `excluded`, or similar lead dispositions must not be rendered as causal probability.

Lead exclusion requires the accepted negative/discriminating evidence burden; lack of support is not rejection.
