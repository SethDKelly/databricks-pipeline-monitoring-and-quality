## Final Explanation reasoning structure

The completed functional chain is:

**bounded business/operational question**
→ **Entity Identity + scope + event/effective time + knowledge perspective**
→ **source-truth-owner routing**
→ **material statement proposition identity**
→ **direct projection or accepted derived cross-concept statement**
→ **statement-relative supporting/contradicting/limiting basis**
→ **domain-specific health/change/execution/inferential/control/governance semantics**
→ **proposition-faithful uncertainty/negative language**
→ **current audience/purpose/delivery authorized projection + safe abstraction**
→ **partial answer / material refresh / retained communication history**
→ **historical/comparative replay and delta classification**.

No arrow creates source truth, evidence, authority, authorization, causality, Impact, control enforcement or execution.

## Question and answer boundary confirmed

A question is initiating request context, not a truth-owning concept. Its family/wording does not change source authority, evidence sufficiency or authorization.

Material answer statements are bounded propositions projected through Explanation. They preserve source truth owner and epistemic type; rendered wording is not proposition identity.

Compound answers preserve sibling independence. There is no synthetic overall answer truth, incident truth, RCA truth or global operational status.

## Basis and derivation boundary confirmed

Every material statement retains internal statement-to-basis traceability. Supporting, contradicting and limiting roles are statement-relative, and sufficiency remains conclusion-relative.

Duplicate/common-derived evidence is not independent corroboration and evidence count is not confidence.

Derived cross-concept statements require an accepted synchronization/refinement join; prose adjacency never creates causality, exposure, prevention, health or compliance.

## Domain semantics confirmed

Health/change/execution questions preserve run occurrence, lifecycle outcome, output existence, currentness/freshness, profile-bound health, structure/compatibility/comparability/quality, intent/deployment/realized Change, run-specific version use and timing as distinct propositions.

Investigation remains distinct from Causal Claim. Impact preserves candidate/opportunity/exposure/effect/consequence/attribution. Safeguard preserves lifecycle/enforcement/prevention/recovery. Gate preserves readiness/decision/enforcement/execution. Responsibility, meaning, Classification, Policy Context, Capability Authorization and Assertion Authority remain separate governance propositions.

Narrative shorthand never collapses those distinctions.

## Uncertainty and negative-evidence boundary confirmed

Unknown, unavailable, insufficient/indeterminate, conflicting, restricted/redacted, stale, non-comparable, not-applicable, not-evidenced/not-recorded/not-known-by-cutoff and evidence-backed negative states remain distinguishable where material.

Absence of evidence never becomes evidence of absence. Strong negatives retain their exact source-specific opportunity/coverage/authority burdens.

No numeric or generic probability/confidence layer is required.

## Authorization and safe projection boundary confirmed

Audience/purpose/delivery are context rather than permission. Requester visibility does not imply onward disclosure. Result, basis, provenance and detail visibility can differ.

Safe abstraction is epistemically monotone and not automatic declassification. Hidden material limitations constrain or withhold the visible conclusion. Opaque existence and `inspectBasis` metadata can themselves be sensitive.

Cross-audience views remain projections of one truth and need not have identical detail.

## Progressive maturity and retention boundary confirmed

A trustworthy partial answer is valid. Elapsed time and repeated recomposition do not mature truth.

Material refresh depends on source/evidence/derivation/authorization/question-context change. Basis enrichment can occur without status change. Statement removal does not automatically mean false/retracted.

Retained Explanation snapshots are evidence of actual communication, not timeless truth. Current-preferred projections link/supersede without rewriting prior communication. Missing retained communication cannot be reconstructed as exact history.

## Historical/comparative boundary confirmed

Phase 008 preserves four distinct explanatory views:

1. **historical source state** — source-owned facts/actions/results effective for the historical scope;
2. **as-known-at-cut Explanation** — what could be concluded using evidence available by knowledge cut `K`;
3. **actual retained communication** — what was in fact communicated for a historical audience/context, when retained;
4. **current retrospective Explanation** — what is concluded now about the historical scope using later accepted evidence/corrections.

Current authorized projection is applied independently to whichever view the present requester asks to see.

Explanation diffs classify source/status, basis/knowledge, scope, materiality, authorization/detail, presentation and retained-versus-reconstructed differences. `The answer changed` is not an umbrella truth or cause.

## Non-rewriting discipline confirmed

Later evidence may improve retrospective understanding without rewriting what was known, decided, authorized or communicated earlier.

Current authority/authorization/reference/topology/control state is never projected backward by convenience. Corrections and supersessions remain visible as history.

An earlier answer can be historically authentic yet unsuitable for current use.
