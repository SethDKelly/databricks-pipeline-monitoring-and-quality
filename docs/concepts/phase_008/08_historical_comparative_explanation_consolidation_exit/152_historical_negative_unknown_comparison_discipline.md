# EXPL-152 — Historical Negative & Unknown Comparison Discipline

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 08

Historical comparisons preserve the exact distinction among `unknown at K`, `not evidenced by K`, `not recorded by K`, `unavailable by K`, `restricted`, and an evidence-backed negative proposition.

A later positive conclusion may resolve an earlier unknown without making the earlier unknown erroneous. A later negative conclusion still requires the source-specific opportunity/coverage burden.

No comparison converts missing historical telemetry into `did not happen`.
