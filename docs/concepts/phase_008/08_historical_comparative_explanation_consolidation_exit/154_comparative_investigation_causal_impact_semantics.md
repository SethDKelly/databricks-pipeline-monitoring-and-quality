# EXPL-154 — Comparative Investigation, Causal & Impact Semantics

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 08

Historical comparison preserves Investigation/localization versus Causal Claim and candidate/opportunity/exposure/effect/consequence distinctions.

An Investigation may close and later reopen; a localization point may move; causal status may change under its owning rules; exposure/effect/consequence may become retrospectively established as evidence matures.

No comparison promotes `first observed` into `root cause`, `reachable` into `exposed`, or `exposed` into consequence/cause.
