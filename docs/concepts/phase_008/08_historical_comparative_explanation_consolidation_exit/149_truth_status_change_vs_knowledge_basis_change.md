# EXPL-149 — Truth/Status Change vs Knowledge/Basis Change

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 08

A different answer at two cuts does not necessarily mean the underlying historical state changed.

Examples:

- exposure may have been historically real while changing from `unknown at K1` to `exposed at K2` because evidence arrived;
- a Causal Claim may actually transition from `supported` to `confirmed` only when the source claim status changes under REF-017 + AUTH-034;
- a fixed historical Gate decision can remain unchanged while retrospective enforcement interpretation changes.

Comparison states which layer changed.
