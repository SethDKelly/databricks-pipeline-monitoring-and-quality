# Phase 008 Group 02 — Answer Structure, Statement Selection & Basis Traceability

**Status:** Review complete — accepted

## Goal

Refine how a bounded question from Group 01 becomes an evidence-grounded Explanation composed of material answer statements whose proposition identity, source ownership, epistemic type, basis, limitations and summary/detail behavior remain explicit.

## Group result

Group 02 accepts **EXPL-013–EXPL-028** and scenario suite **AS02-01–AS02-30**.

No new concept is required. Explanation remains the projection/communication owner only; every substantive proposition remains owned by its accepted source concept.

The central composition chain is:

**bounded question/subquestion → candidate source-owned propositions → question-relevant/material statement selection → direct projection or explicit cross-concept derivation → statement-level epistemic/source identity → supporting/contradicting/limiting basis → summary/detail composition → internal basis-inspection linkage → bounded answer-coverage result**.

No stage creates stronger truth merely by composition.

## Accepted contracts

- **EXPL-013** — material answer statement proposition identity.
- **EXPL-014** — statement source ownership and epistemic type preservation.
- **EXPL-015** — direct projection versus derived cross-concept statement.
- **EXPL-016** — question relevance, materiality and statement selection.
- **EXPL-017** — answer-bearing, contextual and limitation statement roles.
- **EXPL-018** — supporting, contradicting and limiting basis roles.
- **EXPL-019** — conclusion-relative basis sufficiency and traceability.
- **EXPL-020** — basis cardinality, independence, common derivation and deduplication.
- **EXPL-021** — explicit cross-concept join logic and non-transitive composition.
- **EXPL-022** — headline, summary and detail semantic equivalence.
- **EXPL-023** — omission, compression and material-qualification preservation.
- **EXPL-024** — multi-statement answer composition and sibling independence.
- **EXPL-025** — primary-answer selection and ordering without universal priority scoring.
- **EXPL-026** — internal basis-inspection identity and provenance.
- **EXPL-027** — answer coverage/completeness without confidence-score substitution.
- **EXPL-028** — Group 03 domain-question handoff.

## Durable rules

- rendered sentence wording is not statement identity;
- an answer statement is a projection artifact, not an independent truth state;
- statement proposition identity preserves subject, predicate/conclusion, scope, event/effective time and knowledge perspective when material;
- source truth owner and epistemic type survive composition;
- a direct projection cannot be strengthened by paraphrase;
- a cross-concept derived statement requires explicit semantic join logic and sufficient basis for the derived proposition;
- juxtaposition, temporal proximity, Lineage, Change Intent, Deployment or common source membership do not create causality by composition;
- question relevance/materiality governs selection, but Criticality/priority does not become truth/evidence strength;
- context can clarify an answer without becoming the answer-bearing conclusion;
- limitation statements are first-class and must not be hidden when omission would strengthen the apparent conclusion;
- support, contradiction and limitation are statement-relative roles; linked evidence retains its source truth owner;
- evidence counts do not become confidence by arithmetic; duplicate/common-derived basis does not become independent corroboration;
- summaries may omit detail but may not change proposition scope, polarity, epistemic state or material limitation;
- headline/detail/basis views over the same statement must remain semantically compatible;
- one supported sibling statement cannot strengthen an unresolved sibling;
- multi-statement answers do not create a synthetic global health/cause/Impact/control state;
- primary-answer ordering is question/context specific and no universal severity/importance score is introduced;
- internal statement-to-basis traceability is mandatory even when later authorization rules restrict visible basis inspection;
- answer coverage is evaluated against bounded subquestions/material statements, not converted into a universal percentage or confidence score.

## Boundary with later groups

Group 02 defines structural answer semantics, not domain-specific wording. Group 03 applies these contracts to health/change/execution questions. Group 04 applies them to Investigation/causality/Impact/control/governance. Group 05 owns detailed uncertainty/conflict language. Group 06 owns authorized disclosure/safe abstraction of statements and basis. Group 07 owns refresh/retention evolution. Group 08 owns historical/comparative Explanation and exit.

## Architecture boundary

Do not select response templates, LLMs, deterministic generation engines, citation UI, retrieval/search architecture, persistence schema or basis-storage model.
