# EXPL-019 — Conclusion-Relative Basis Sufficiency & Traceability

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02

## Requirement

A material answer statement may be emitted at a given epistemic strength only when its basis satisfies the accepted evidence/authority/coverage requirements for that exact proposition.

Traceability must allow the framework to identify:

- which source proposition(s) support the statement;
- which accepted derivation/synchronization rule was used when derived;
- which contradiction/limitation is material;
- relevant event/effective and knowledge-time context;
- any source authority/authorization state relevant to how the proposition was resolved.

## Invariants

Basis sufficiency is conclusion relative. Evidence sufficient for `candidate` may be insufficient for `exposed`; evidence sufficient for `supported` may be insufficient for `confirmed`; no-run or no-effect conclusions retain their bounded negative-evidence burdens.

Explanation cannot use rhetorical certainty to exceed source sufficiency.
