# EXPL-042 — Intent-to-Realization Match & Divergence Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 03

## Requirement

`Did the change go as planned?` or `did reality match the intent?` uses the accepted derived intent-to-realization comparison for an exact intent revision/component and realized slice.

Allowed source vocabulary remains:

- `matched`;
- `partially matched`;
- `diverged`;
- `not realized` with sufficient negative evidence;
- `not evidenced`;
- `indeterminate`;
- `conflicting`;
- `unavailable`.

## Rules

Match/divergence does not itself establish health, acceptability, authorization or causality. Partial rollout remains slice-specific and no universal realization percentage is introduced.
