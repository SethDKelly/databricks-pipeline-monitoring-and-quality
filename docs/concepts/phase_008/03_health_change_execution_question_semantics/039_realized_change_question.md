# EXPL-039 — Realized Change Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 03

## Requirement

`What changed?` asks for evidence-established realized **Change** over a bounded before/after state, subject/facet and time interval.

## Rules

- planned/registered intent is not itself realized Change;
- deployment activity is not itself realized Change;
- realized Change may exist without a matching registered intent;
- a Change statement does not by itself establish health, cause, authorization or business consequence;
- rollback/reversion is represented as another historical transition rather than deletion of the earlier Change;
- current-state difference and historical transition are not interchangeable without temporal context.
