# EXPL-049 — Group 04 Inferential-Question Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 03

## Requirement

Define the domain-stable input Group 04 receives for Investigation, causality, Impact, control and governance questions.

Group 04 may rely on Group 03 to keep independently traceable:

- execution occurrence/lifecycle/output/currentness propositions;
- health dimensions/profiles, structure, comparability, Expectations and reconciliation;
- realized Change, Change Intent, Deployment attempt/activation and intent-realization comparison;
- run-specific implementation/input/output version evidence;
- repeated-execution continuity;
- dependency/sequence/waiting/consumption;
- expected-work/opportunity/no-run evidence burdens;
- timing/delay/lateness facts;
- historical event/knowledge perspectives.

## Handoff boundary

Group 04 may use these as evidence/context but may not convert timing, Lineage, change, health, execution, responsibility, policy, Gate or Safeguard state into causality/Impact/authority by narrative implication.

## Exit statement

EXPL-029–EXPL-049 make direct health/change/execution questions answerable without adding a new concept or weakening Phase 006/007 truth boundaries.
