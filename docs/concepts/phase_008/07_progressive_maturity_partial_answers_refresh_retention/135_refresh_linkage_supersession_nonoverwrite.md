# EXPL-135 — Refresh Linkage, Supersession & Non-Overwrite

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

When retained, a refreshed Explanation links to prior snapshot(s) and may be designated current/superseding for present use without erasing prior communication history.

Supersession of an Explanation means a newer projection should be preferred for its declared current context; it does not rewrite the earlier source state, knowledge cut, authorization or communicated content.

Correction/retraction of high-consequence communication remains separately governed by AUTH-049.
