# EXPL-121 — Partial Explanation Eligibility & Bounded Coverage

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

A trustworthy Explanation may be emitted when one or more bounded subquestions have valid, authorized material statements even if other subquestions remain unresolved, unavailable, restricted or not yet enriched.

- Partial delivery preserves Group 01 subquestion identity and Group 02 statement/basis traceability.
- Unanswered siblings remain explicit where material; they do not inherit the status of answered siblings.
- Partial coverage is proposition/subquestion specific, not a global completeness score.
- Waiting for broader enrichment is not required when the narrower answer is already useful and faithful.
