# EXPL-138 — Change Summary & Notification Semantics

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

A refresh/change summary may state which material propositions, statuses, bases, limitations, scopes or visibility conditions changed and why those changes are material.

It must not translate a source transition into stronger language than the source permits: e.g. `new evidence moved the claim from supported to confirmed` only when Causal Claim actually transitioned under REF-017/AUTH-034.

Notification channel, event architecture, cadence and subscriber UX remain deferred.
