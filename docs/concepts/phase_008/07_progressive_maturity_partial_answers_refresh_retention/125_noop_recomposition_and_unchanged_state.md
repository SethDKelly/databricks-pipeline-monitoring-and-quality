# EXPL-125 — No-Op Recomposition & Unchanged State

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

Recomposing an Explanation over materially unchanged proposition state, basis, temporal perspective and authorized projection is a semantic no-op even if wording or generation time differs.

A system may retain or suppress such recompositions according to later retention architecture, but it must not describe them as evidence maturation, confirmation, resolution or substantive update merely because they are newer.
