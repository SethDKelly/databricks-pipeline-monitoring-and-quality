# EXPL-127 — Conflict & Contradiction Refresh

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

New contradicting or conflicting evidence must be routed through the owning REF/AUTH/HLTH/OPS proposition before Explanation wording changes.

A previously supported statement may become weakened/unresolved/conflicting only when its source state or accepted derivation changes. Explanation does not independently downgrade or choose a winner.

Prior retained wording remains a record of the prior authorized communication, not an editable draft of current truth.
