# Explanation

**Canonical key:** `concept.explanation`

**Kind:** CONCEPT

**Authority:** CANONICAL CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `concept.explanation`

**Owns current question:** How can an authorized audience receive a proposition-bounded, evidence-grounded, time-aware account without Explanation becoming a truth source?

**Stable IDs:** N/A

## Current semantics

Explanation owns the initiating bounded question/request, requested conclusion type, resolved subject/scope, event/effective time and knowledge perspective, audience/requester/purpose/delivery context, material statements and source epistemic types, statement-to-basis references/roles, authorized governance context, limitations/redactions, composition time, and retained version/snapshot linkage when applicable.

## Actions

- `compose` — produce an authorized task-relative account from source-owned truth.
- `composeAt` — compose for explicit event/effective time and knowledge cut/perspective.
- `inspectBasis` — expose requester-authorized statement basis/provenance/limitations.
- `refresh` — produce a new version after material source/evidence/authorization/context change without overwriting retained prior communication.

## Invariants / boundaries

- Explanation is a projection/composition over authorized concept state; it cannot manufacture Observation, Assessment, Causal Claim status, Impact, authority, evidence sufficiency, control result, or new truth.
- Material statements retain proposition identity, truth owner, scope, polarity, epistemic strength, and traceable supporting/contradicting/limiting basis.
- Observation ≠ Assessment ≠ Change Intent ≠ Deployment ≠ Change ≠ Causal Claim ≠ Impact ≠ Annotation survives prose composition.
- `consistent with intent` never becomes `caused by intent`; Lineage reachability never becomes exposure; exposure never becomes downstream effect/consequence.
- Unknown, unavailable, insufficient, conflict, restricted, stale-for-use, non-comparable, not-applicable, not-evidenced/not-known-by-cut, and evidence-backed negative conclusions remain distinct; no universal answer-confidence/completeness score is accepted.
- Negative-form questions do not reduce evidence/coverage burdens.
- Safe abstraction may reduce detail or narrow a conclusion but cannot strengthen scope, polarity, causal role, epistemic status, negative coverage, or authority standing.
- Restricted evidence is not retrieved/used merely to smuggle a conclusion into an unauthorized summary; hidden material limitations must constrain/narrow/withhold visible conclusions.
- Current retrospective truth ≠ `as-known-then` truth ≠ retained authentic historical communication.
- Progressive maturity requires material source/evidence/authorization/context change, not elapsed time or rewording.

## Ambiguity / evidence

Prefer a narrower supported statement over plausible narrative completion. Sibling statements/subquestions retain independent answerability/epistemic state.

## Synchronizations / related canonical resources

All truth-owning concepts can contribute through authorized projection. Capability Authorization governs current requester visibility; Assertion Authority/evidence sufficiency remain separate. Detailed EXPL contracts remain with CKR-G until migrated.

## Non-goals

UI/rendering technology, new evidence, causal/Impact/control promotion, governance replacement, access grants, or mandatory LLM/generative architecture.
