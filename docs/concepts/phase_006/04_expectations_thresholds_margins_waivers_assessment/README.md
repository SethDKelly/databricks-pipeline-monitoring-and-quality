# Phase 006 Group 04 — Expectations, Thresholds, Margins, Waivers & Assessment Semantics

**Status:** Next — not yet started

## Goal

Define how applicable Observations and descriptive Baseline comparisons are interpreted against explicit normative Expectations while preserving conflict, bounded exceptions, evidence limitations and underlying measurement truth.

## Accepted handoff from Groups 01–03

- HLTH-001–HLTH-008 provide exact metric/check definition, subject/grain/window binding, applicability, profile and provenance semantics;
- HLTH-009–HLTH-018 provide consumer/interface-specific structural compatibility and scoped structural-change consequences;
- HLTH-019–HLTH-029 provide explicit Baseline reference membership, comparability dimensions/states, fixed/rolling/seasonal/cohort/post-change references, reference sufficiency, approximation uncertainty, distribution comparison, normalization, refresh/contamination controls and multi-Baseline ambiguity;
- Observation ≠ Baseline membership/summary ≠ comparative Assessment ≠ normative Expectation;
- `within Baseline` does not mean healthy and `outside Baseline` does not mean failed;
- repeated abnormal behavior can become historically typical while remaining normatively unacceptable;
- raw values can be non-comparable while an explicitly defined normalized measure remains comparable;
- low-volume, sparse, approximate or unresolved evidence can limit a normative conclusion even when the Expectation itself is authoritative;
- a new/post-change regime can have insufficient Baseline history while a separately established Expectation remains immediately evaluable if current evidence is sufficient;
- AUTH-016–AUTH-023 govern who may establish/revise/waive/retire rules; Group 04 defines functional evaluation rather than authority.

## Review scope

- hard criteria versus warning/failure bands;
- absolute, relative and asymmetric thresholds/margins;
- inclusive/exclusive boundary semantics and units/denominators;
- normative Expectation versus descriptive Baseline comparison;
- evaluation where Baseline is absent, non-comparable or ambiguous;
- threshold evaluation with low-volume, approximate, sampled or otherwise uncertain evidence;
- simultaneously valid rules in different dimensions/contexts versus true normative conflict;
- bounded waiver/exception/suspension presentation;
- effect of waiver on response/eligibility versus underlying violation truth;
- dimension-level normative Assessment status vocabulary;
- relationship among `meets`, `violates`, `warning`, `degraded`, `atypical`, `unknown`, `conflicting`, `unavailable`, `insufficient evidence` and `not applicable` without conflation;
- severity/priority versus health state;
- evidence suitability required before an Assessment can be produced;
- historical rule/version/waiver binding and reassessment after corrected evidence.

## Questions to resolve

1. What canonical normative Assessment states are needed without creating one universal health score?
2. How should hard thresholds, warning bands and tolerance margins differ semantically?
3. How are relative thresholds bound to their reference/denominator without turning Baseline into normative truth?
4. How should approximation/low-volume uncertainty constrain pass/fail claims near a boundary?
5. How can Baseline atypicality and normative success/failure coexist visibly?
6. How should bounded waivers/suspensions be represented without rewriting a real violation as pass?
7. When do multiple normative rules compose, and when are they genuine unresolved conflicts?
8. How should severity/escalation remain separate from whether a criterion is actually met?

## Boundaries

- Waivers do not create false clean passes.
- Baseline regularity never becomes a threshold without an authoritative Expectation.
- Strictest/business/technical/latest/highest-severity does not silently resolve normative conflict.
- Authority cannot manufacture evidence sufficiency or empirical comparability.
- Do not define A+B→C propagation/reconciliation yet; Group 05 owns that.
- Do not define overall/composite health or control-readiness timing yet; Group 06 owns those.
- Do not select DQX/Metric Views rule realization, statistical libraries, storage or compute architecture.
