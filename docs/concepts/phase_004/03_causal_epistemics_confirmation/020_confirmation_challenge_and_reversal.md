# REF-020 — Challenge, Reversal, and Historical Preservation After Confirmation

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

## Purpose

Ensure confirmed causal conclusions remain challengeable when materially new evidence arrives, while preserving the historical fact that confirmation occurred under an earlier evidence cut and standard.

## Rules

- `confirmed` is a high-consequence current epistemic status, not an immutable historical truth marker.
- Material late/corrected evidence is evaluated under REF-010 and REF-011 against the confirmed claim's proposition, evidence basis, contradiction set, alternatives, and confirmation profile.
- If the new evidence materially undermines a required confirmation condition, the current claim status must be reevaluated rather than retaining confirmation by inertia.
- A formerly confirmed claim may become supported, weakened, unresolved, or rejected according to the later evidence picture.
- The original confirmation event, confirmer, standard/profile, evidence cut, and historical status remain reconstructable.
- A later reversal does not retroactively make historical responders dishonest if the original confirmation legitimately met the then-applicable evidence/authority standard.
- Conversely, historical confirmation does not shield a claim from correction merely because operational decisions were based on it.
- If reevaluation finds the new evidence does not materially affect the confirmation basis, confirmation can remain current with a traceable reevaluation result.
- Reopening an associated Investigation may be warranted when the change is material, but claim reevaluation and Investigation lifecycle remain separate.
- Historical gate/safeguard/actions taken under the earlier causal understanding are not rewritten counterfactually.

## Example

A Deployment claim is confirmed under an accepted profile because evidence then places degradation after activation with an applicable mechanism and alternatives addressed. Later authoritative timing evidence establishes the degradation actually began before activation. The claim is reevaluated and may become rejected. Historical replay still shows that it was confirmed at the earlier knowledge cutoff and why.

## Non-goals

- automatic rollback/remediation;
- legal fault reassignment;
- rewriting historical incident reports;
- automatic Investigation reopen workflow.
