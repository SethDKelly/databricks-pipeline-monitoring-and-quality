# REF-013 — Causal Proposition and Role Binding

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

## Purpose

Require every material Causal Claim to state the causal proposition precisely enough that its evidence burden can be evaluated without relying on vague root-cause language.

## Required proposition frame

A claim identifies, where material:

- proposed cause condition/event/state/change;
- defined effect/outcome;
- subjects and relevant context;
- event/effective-time relationship;
- proposed causal role, such as direct, contributing, enabling, triggering, preventing, primary, or unresolved role;
- mechanism/relationship rationale that makes causal influence possible;
- required transmission/encounter condition when the effect depends on a particular version/state being consumed;
- bounded investigation scope relevant to alternatives.

## Rules

- `upstream`, `recently deployed`, `changed`, `correlated`, or `first observed` is not a complete causal proposition.
- Cause occurrence and effect occurrence must remain separately evidenced.
- A causal role is part of the proposition and cannot be silently upgraded. `Contributed to` is weaker/different from `was the primary cause of`.
- `Primary` requires comparative evidence against other material contributors; it is never the default role.
- A preventing claim such as `safeguard prevented exposure` still requires evidence of the protection mechanism and the outcome it prevented under the applicable evidence standard.
- Claim refinement can narrow or revise a proposition while preserving earlier claim history rather than silently changing what was being evaluated.

## Example

`B changed before C` is correlation context. A bounded claim is: `B's reduced qualifying population contributed to C's row-count reduction for output window W because C consumed B version V through the active join relationship.`

## Non-goals

- selecting a causal algorithm;
- requiring one canonical causal-role taxonomy for every future domain;
- quantitative percentage attribution.
