# Phase 004 Group 02 — Event/Effective Time, Knowledge Cut & Correction

**Status:** Next review group — not yet started

## Goal

Specialize the accepted Group 01 evidence-applicability/coverage/sufficiency framework for exact historical and correction semantics: what evidence is eligible for an `as-known` view, what can legitimately be said to have been known or not known by a cutoff, and how late/corrected evidence propagates into reassessment/reopen behavior without rewriting contemporaneous history.

## Accepted handoff from Group 01

- Evidence sufficiency is conclusion-relative rather than a universal score.
- Evidence must be applicable to the target proposition before it can support/contradict/exclude it.
- Coverage is bounded and multidimensional.
- Negative/absence/exclusion conclusions require adequate opportunity-to-observe and sufficient bounded coverage.
- Missing/unavailable/restricted evidence is not a negative fact.
- Duplicated/common-source evidence is not independent corroboration.
- Applicable conflicts remain explicit unless an accepted authority rule resolves them.
- Sufficiency and current disclosure authorization remain separate.

## Planned questions

- What exactly makes evidence eligible for a historical state cut with event/effective-time target `T` and recorded/knowledge cutoff `K`?
- How are source-event time, event/effective time, collection/retrieval time, ingestion time, assertion time, and correction time distinguished where necessary?
- What evidence is sufficient to state `known by K`, `learned after K`, or `not known by K`?
- When is absence from the retained evidence set insufficient to prove `not known` because collection/retention coverage was incomplete?
- How should late-arriving evidence whose effective time precedes its knowledge time enter later retrospective cuts?
- How should corrections distinguish source correction, conflicting new evidence, semantic reinterpretation, and later source-authority resolution?
- What dependent Assessments, Baselines, Changes, Causal Claims, Impact conclusions, safeguards/gates, Investigations, or Explanations should be reevaluated after material corrections?
- When should a closed Investigation be prompted for reopen versus merely retain a retrospective note?
- What historical outputs must be actually retained versus safely reconstructible?
- How do current authorization and restricted historical evidence constrain replay without changing the historical truth model?
