## Cross-group composition findings

### 1. One evidence framework works across positive and negative conclusions

REF-001–REF-005 consistently govern run existence/absence, output existence/absence, historical `not known`, causal exclusion, non-exposure, gate/safeguard enforcement, and prevented-exposure claims.

Positive existence facts may become trustworthy from narrow direct evidence. Negative or exclusion conclusions require an adequate opportunity to observe plus sufficient bounded coverage of the relevant opportunities/paths.

Missing, restricted, unavailable, out-of-scope, or conflicting evidence remains a limitation rather than a reassuring negative.

### 2. Progressive analytical availability composes with evidence rigor

REF-006–REF-012 allow the framework to expose the narrowest trustworthy result as soon as its evidence is known while preserving event time, source availability, framework knowledge time, and derived evaluation time.

The accepted progression remains:

**immediate operational validation → enriched health evaluation → investigative/RCA reasoning → retrospective/post-operations review**.

For controls/exposure this can further mature as:

**readiness/output facts → gate decision → enforcement evidence → execution/consumption evidence → negative/prevention/causal conclusions**.

A fast result never inherits the truth of slower evidence classes. `Job succeeded` does not become `pipeline healthy`; `gate decided hold` does not become `hold enforced`; an early supported hypothesis does not become confirmed; a safeguard record does not become prevented exposure.

### 3. Historical replay and late evidence remain non-rewriting

REF-006–REF-012 plus REF-030 preserve contemporaneous and retrospective cuts consistently across Assessment, causal status, exposure, readiness, gate/safeguard enforcement, and Explanation.

Late evidence can revise the current retrospective result without backdating framework knowledge or replacing the actual historical decision/action/communication.

### 4. Causality composes without a root-cause shortcut

REF-013–REF-020 support multiple compatible contributors, explicit proposed/supported/weakened/unresolved/rejected/confirmed status, bounded material alternatives, and claim-class confirmation profiles.

Direct control-mechanism evidence can justify strong support quickly, but broader operational/business consequence claims still use the same causal standards. Confirmation remains evidence-gated and separately authority-gated.

### 5. Impact/exposure remains layered

REF-021–REF-023 preserve candidate/reachability, actual affected-state encounter, non-exposure, downstream effect, consequence, and causal attribution as distinct conclusions.

`Not exposed to V` does not imply fresh, current, healthy, or unaffected in every dimension. Safe prior-version use can establish non-exposure to V while independently producing staleness.

### 6. Readiness/control proof remains layered

REF-024–REF-029 preserve:

**prerequisite predicate → readiness result → gate decision → decision enforcement → actual execution**

and separately:

**safeguard proposal/configuration → activation/enforcement → consumer encounter/non-encounter → prevented exposure**.

No configured control, fallback policy, decision record, or lack of downstream activity is silently treated as proof of enforcement.

### 7. Restricted-data analysis remains useful without declassification

Evidence sufficiency remains independent from current requester disclosure. The framework may have enough authorized internal evidence for a conclusion while exposing only a permitted abstracted result and limitation to a requester.

Restricted or opaque evidence is never converted into absence, and historical authorization never becomes reusable current permission.

### 8. Passive monitoring and active control remain operationally distinct

Ungated production remains semantically independent from passive monitoring availability. Explicit Execution Gates may deliberately require stronger/faster control-path evidence and availability because they are active production controls.

Phase 004 defines the evidence distinction but does not select the control architecture, latency budget, timeout policy, or vendor mechanism.
