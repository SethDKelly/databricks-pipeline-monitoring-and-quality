## Cross-scenario evidence checks

The full scenario set consistently preserves:

1. **Positive evidence asymmetry** — direct applicable evidence can often establish existence before comprehensive coverage exists.
2. **Negative-evidence burden** — absence, exclusion, non-exposure, `not known`, and suppression claims require opportunity-to-observe plus bounded coverage.
3. **Progressive maturity** — early operational facts can appear before health/RCA/exposure/post-ops evidence without being promoted into broader conclusions.
4. **Temporal integrity** — event time, source availability, framework knowledge, and evaluation time remain distinguishable.
5. **Causal integrity** — candidate/support/rejection/confirmation remain explicit; multiple contributors and unresolved outcomes are valid.
6. **Control integrity** — readiness, decision, enforcement, execution, safeguard enforcement, consumer encounter, and prevention remain separate.
7. **Authorization integrity** — internal evidentiary sufficiency does not grant current disclosure or operational authority.
8. **Historical integrity** — late/corrected evidence can revise retrospective conclusions but cannot rewrite actual historical actions, claim status, or Explanation.

**All E-01–E-22 pass under REF-001–REF-030.** The accepted Group 01–04 scenario checks also compose without contradiction. No new Concept, synchronization, or refinement contract is required for Phase 004 exit.
