## Cross-scenario replay checks

All scenarios preserve the following seven questions as independently answerable where evidence exists:

1. **What happened?** — event/effective-time facts and actual actions.
2. **What was known then?** — state available under the historical knowledge cutoff.
3. **What was believed/interpreted then?** — actually recorded Assessment, Investigation, Causal Claim, Impact, Annotation, and related state; not inferred merely because evidence existed.
4. **What was authorized then?** — historical Capability Authorization, not current access.
5. **What control state/action applied then?** — actual Execution Gate and Propagation Safeguard state/actions, not counterfactual replacements.
6. **What was actually explained then?** — retained historical Explanation if it exists; otherwise no historical communication is fabricated.
7. **What do we know now?** — current retrospective state, which may differ after corrections/late evidence.

## Consolidation result

**All E-01–E-22 pass.** No new concept boundary is required by the combined scenarios. Remaining unknowns are refinement questions about evidence sufficiency, authority, statistical semantics, integration contracts, control policy, and technical realization rather than missing conceptual ownership.
