# Group 05 — Downstream Impact, Annotation & Explanation

**Status:** Next review group — not yet started

## Goal

Define how downstream Lineage creates Impact candidates, how evidence refines reachability into exposure/effect/consequence, how human Annotation participates without stealing structured truth, and how an authorized audience receives useful Explanation and analytical context even when direct data access is restricted.

## Accepted handoff from Group 04

- Historical Lineage/reachability alone never proves causal Impact.
- Causal Claim owns propositions that an originating condition caused a downstream effect; Impact separately owns reachability/exposure/effect/consequence evidence.
- Investigation may close with supported, multiple, or unresolved claims; Explanation must preserve those epistemic states rather than narratively upgrading them.
- First-observed localization is not root cause and must not be converted into downstream causal certainty.
- Analyst research that establishes facts belongs in Observation/Change; causal propositions belong in Causal Claim; Annotation remains attributed context.
- Prospective Impact Profile remains pre-change candidate/risk context and cannot be substituted for actual downstream exposure/effect evidence.
- Propagation Safeguard can prevent exposure or create delivery consequences; safeguard state itself is neither proof of protected-data defect nor proof of downstream effect.

## Accepted pre-Group-05 authorization handoff

- **Capability Authorization** is accepted as the 22nd concept through a narrow Phase 002 post-exit addendum.
- Direct/raw data read, metadata/governance visibility, derived health/Assessment visibility, Lineage/RCA participation, job/run operational actions, and safeguard-control actions are independent capabilities.
- Denial of direct-data access must not automatically prevent approved Investigation/RCA using derived, aggregate, redacted, or opaque evidence.
- Derived evidence is not automatically unrestricted; metrics, thresholds, table names, Lineage, policy details, responsibility metadata, and causal conclusions may each require their own authorized projection.
- Responsibility Assignment, Classification, Policy Context, and Monitoring Scope do not themselves grant access or operational authority.
- A principal may have job-operation capability without raw-data read, or analysis capability without any production-control authority.
- Explanation and Impact reasoning must operate over the requesting actor's authorized evidence view and must expose limitations without retrieving restricted facts merely to summarize them.

## Planned synchronization questions

- downstream historical Lineage traversal → Impact candidates;
- execution/consumption/version evidence → actual exposure;
- downstream Observation/Assessment/Change → observed effect;
- semantic/criticality/business evidence → consequence context;
- originating-condition → downstream-effect propositions → Causal Claim where causal assertion is made;
- safeguard placement/enforcement → prevented exposure versus operational consequence;
- Annotation → contextual support without mutation/confirmation;
- Capability Authorization + concept state → authorized analytical projection for the requesting principal;
- restricted raw evidence → safe aggregate/redacted/opaque health and RCA context where independently authorized;
- policy/restriction/responsibility/health transparency → useful analyst reasoning without converting those facts into access grants;
- analytical visibility ↔ job-operation/safeguard authority separation;
- all authorized concept state → evidence-grounded Explanation with statement-to-basis traceability, accurate causal status, and explicit redaction/authorization limitations.

## Primary scenarios

E-01, E-03, E-05, E-07, E-08, E-09, E-10, E-12, E-14, E-15, plus restricted-data analyst scenarios where direct data is denied but approved health/RCA evidence remains usable.
