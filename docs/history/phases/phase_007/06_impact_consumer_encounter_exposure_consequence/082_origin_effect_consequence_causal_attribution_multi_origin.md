# OPS-082 — Origin→Effect/Consequence Causal Attribution & Multiple Origins

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 06

## Purpose

Define the exact boundary between Impact evidence and causal attribution for downstream effects/consequences.

## Contract

Impact may establish candidate, encounter/exposure, downstream effect and consequence independently. When an actor/system asserts that an originating condition caused, contributed to, enabled, triggered, prevented or materially influenced a downstream effect/consequence, the proposition belongs to **Causal Claim** under OPS-060/061 and REF-013–REF-020.

For mechanisms that require encounter/transmission, exposure evidence is a material causal dimension. Sufficient non-exposure can contradict such a claim; unknown exposure remains a gap rather than support.

Several originating conditions may contribute to one downstream outcome, and one origin may have different claim states across different consumers/effects.

## Invariants

- exposure ≠ cause.
- consequence ≠ originating-condition attribution.
- confirmed upstream causal claim ≠ confirmed downstream effect/consequence attribution automatically.
- consumer non-exposure can coexist with a confirmed upstream cause for another outcome.
- multiple origins/contributors remain first-class.
