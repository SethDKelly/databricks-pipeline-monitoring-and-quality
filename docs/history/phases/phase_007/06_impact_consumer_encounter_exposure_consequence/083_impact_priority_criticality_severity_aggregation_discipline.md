# OPS-083 — Impact Priority, Criticality, Severity & Aggregation Discipline

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 06

## Purpose

Allow operational prioritization without turning context into evidence or hiding Impact layers behind a universal scalar.

## Contract

Criticality, Classification, Semantic Definition, Responsibility Assignment, known customer/business use, established consequence severity rules or explicit policy may influence review/response priority.

They do not establish reachability, exposure, effect, consequence occurrence, likelihood or causal attribution.

No universal `Impact score`, affected-node count, exposure percentage, severity-weighted descendant sum or criticality×probability formula is accepted by Group 06.

If a governed severity/priority classification exists for a bounded consequence, it remains a separate policy/Assessment result with provenance rather than replacing underlying Impact evidence.

## Invariants

- critical consumer ≠ impacted consumer.
- many reachable nodes ≠ severe realized Impact.
- exposure count ≠ business severity.
- priority ≠ evidence strength.
- summary aggregation must preserve material unknown/not-exposed/effect/consequence distinctions.
