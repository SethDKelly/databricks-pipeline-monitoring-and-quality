# OPS-092 — Alternate Paths, Bypass & Protection Coverage

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 07

## Purpose

Require safeguard reasoning to account for material alternate encounter routes before claiming broad protection or prevention.

## Contract

For a bounded consumer/suspect-state proposition, evaluate:

- material publication/consumption paths known under OPS-075;
- which paths are controlled by the safeguard;
- which paths are outside scope;
- which paths are unresolved/restricted;
- any evidenced bypass or alternate route used during the interval.

A bypass is an actual or sufficiently evidenced path outside the intended/effective protection that can carry the relevant state; mere theoretical graph reachability is not automatically a bypass.

## Invariants

- one controlled path ≠ global protection.
- unknown alternate path blocks strong global prevention where material.
- bypass possibility ≠ bypass occurrence.
- bypass occurrence does not erase successful protection on other paths.
- restricted alternate-path evidence ≠ no alternate path.
