# OPS-104 — Historical Safeguard Replay, Ownership & Group 08 Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 07

## Purpose

Close Group 07 with non-rewriting historical semantics, exact cross-concept ownership and a clean handoff to Execution Gate refinement.

## Contract

Historical safeguard reconstruction distinguishes:

- protected/suspect state and then-effective topology;
- proposal/authorization/request facts;
- effective enforcement by path/scope/interval;
- encounter opportunities and Impact results known then;
- release/expiry/effective reopening;
- late/corrected control or exposure evidence;
- current retrospective prevented-exposure/causal interpretation.

Ownership remains:

- **Propagation Safeguard** — protection proposition, lifecycle/action facts, effective enforcement scope and release/expiry history;
- **Capability Authorization** — permission/approval truth;
- **Lineage** — path/topology truth;
- **Execution History/Observation/Assessment** — runtime/output/health truth;
- **Impact** — encounter/exposure/effect/consequence truth and prevention context;
- **Causal Claim** — causal attribution beyond the REF-028 bounded prevention result;
- **Execution Gate** — start/admission control, refined next.

## Group 08 handoff

Group 08 may consume exact safeguard effective intervals, protected paths, stale/non-delivery effects, release state and unresolved control telemetry. It must not infer gate HOLD/ADMIT from safeguard state or merge output/consumption protection with execution admission.

## Invariants

- later evidence can revise retrospective enforcement/prevention without rewriting historical actions or operator knowledge.
- Propagation Safeguard ≠ Execution Gate.
- safeguard release ≠ gate admit.
- protected/non-exposed consumer ≠ execution was held.
