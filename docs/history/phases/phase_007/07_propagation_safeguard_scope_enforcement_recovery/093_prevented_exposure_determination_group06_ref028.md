# OPS-093 — Prevented Exposure Determination Using Group 06 + REF-028

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 07

## Purpose

Define the exact cross-concept conditions for saying a safeguard prevented suspect-state exposure.

## Contract

A prevented-exposure determination requires the applicable REF-028 elements, now specialized with Group 06:

- exact suspect state and consumer/use proposition;
- an applicable encounter opportunity or other materially operative opportunity;
- historical path showing the state could have reached the consumer through the protected surface;
- evidence-established enforcement for that opportunity/path;
- Impact evidence that the suspect state did not reach the consumer through the controlled route;
- adequate material alternate-path coverage;
- explicit limitations where any element is incomplete.

Prevented exposure is a derived cross-concept result over Safeguard + Impact + Lineage/Execution evidence, not a Propagation Safeguard lifecycle state.

## Invariants

- `active + not exposed` ≠ prevented exposure by default.
- prevented exposure is consumer/state/path/time specific.
- prevention ≠ healthy/current delivery.
- broader claims about delay or business outcomes remain Causal Claims.
