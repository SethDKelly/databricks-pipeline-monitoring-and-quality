# OPS-097 — Extension, Renewal, Scope Revision & Supersession

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 07

## Purpose

Represent changes to an active protection without rewriting the scope or authority that existed earlier.

## Contract

An extension/renewal/scope revision binds:

- the prior safeguard/protection interval;
- revised effective interval and/or protected scope;
- exact added/removed consumers, paths, states or cohorts where material;
- separate authorization under AUTH-037 where required;
- request/issuance versus effective enforcement evidence;
- provenance and knowledge time.

Materially different protection may supersede the prior configuration while preserving both historical intervals.

## Invariants

- extension authority ≠ release authority.
- requested extension ≠ effective continued enforcement.
- widening one dimension does not silently widen all dimensions.
- narrowing scope does not erase earlier protected scope.
- renewal is not proof the original concern persists or is causal.
