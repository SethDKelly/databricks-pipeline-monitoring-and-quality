# AUTH-050 — Status-Preserving Language and Non-Overstatement in Disclosure

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06

## Purpose
Require audience-facing communication to preserve accepted epistemic, normative, exposure, readiness, authority, and control distinctions rather than strengthening them through simplified wording.

## Required preservation examples

- `supported` or `leading` Causal Claim must not become `confirmed root cause`;
- multiple supported contributors must not be collapsed into one cause unless the underlying causal state supports it;
- `reachable` must not become `exposed` or `affected`;
- `not exposed to suspect version` must not become `fresh`, `healthy`, or `safe`;
- `gate hold decision` must not become `hold enforced` without enforcement evidence;
- `gate override` must not become `prerequisite ready`;
- `safeguard active` must not become `prevented exposure` without the accepted evidence standard;
- `released` must not become `healthy`;
- `waived/suspended` must not become an underlying clean pass;
- `authoritative assertion` must not become infallible fact;
- Classification/Policy Context must not become compliance certification;
- unavailable/restricted evidence must not become a reassuring negative.

## Invariants

- Simplification may reduce detail but cannot strengthen the underlying proposition.
- Material conflict, unknown, restriction, and temporal perspective remain visible at an audience-appropriate abstraction when needed to avoid misleading meaning.
- Communication style does not alter concept state.
- Phase 008 may choose phrasing patterns, but it must satisfy these semantic constraints.
