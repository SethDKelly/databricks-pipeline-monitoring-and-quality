## Exit-gate review

### 1. Authority target binding and conflict semantics — PASS

Authority is bound to an explicit category/facet/scheme/type, subject scope, context, and effective time. Source count, recency, ingestion order, availability, ownership/title, or apparent specificity do not create hidden precedence. Authoritative assertion conflict and authority-rule conflict remain explicit when no accepted resolver applies.

### 2. Semantic/schema governance ownership — PASS

Business meaning, technical schema meaning, grain/key roles, responsibility types, Classification/criticality schemes, and Policy Context applicability can have different authoritative holders. Declared/governed schema meaning remains separate from normative compatibility and realized schema Observation/Change.

### 3. Normative health governance — PASS

Metric-profile inclusion, structural/schema Expectations, thresholds/margins, severity, waivers, and high-consequence-use eligibility can be governed independently. Baseline remains descriptive until adopted into an Expectation, and authority cannot manufacture empirical comparability after structural Change.

### 4. Capability Authorization and restricted analysis — PASS

Permission is exact principal/capability/subject/context/time truth. Raw data, schema, metric values, thresholds, Lineage, RCA basis, causal/Impact/control detail, governance actions, and Explanation can be independently authorized. Authorized Analytical Projection remains a view over existing truth rather than declassification.

### 5. High-consequence action authority — PASS

Request/proposal, approval, issuance, control-plane acceptance, enforcement/effect, and outcome remain distinct. Causal confirmation remains jointly evidence- and authority-gated. Gate override does not rewrite readiness; safeguard release does not prove health; break-glass and automation require explicit bounded authority.

### 6. Disclosure and Explanation governance — PASS

Audience/purpose/context/delivery shape what may be disclosed but do not grant permission themselves. Result visibility may differ from basis visibility. Safe abstraction preserves status. Technical/business/executive/audit views remain projections over one truth, and communication review cannot create truth.

### 7. Restricted-data RCA — PASS

The framework can use restricted evidence only when its own processing principal is authorized. A requester may receive exact detail, safe abstraction, opaque existence, or a limitation depending on their capabilities. Restricted evidence never becomes absent merely because the requester cannot inspect it.

### 8. Historical replay and correction — PASS

Authority, authorization, normative rule, approval/delegation/break-glass, action, disclosure, and retained Explanation histories preserve effective time and knowledge time. Later corrections or revocations can change present resolution without rewriting what was known, permitted, done, or communicated earlier.

### 9. Phase 004 evidence integrity — PASS

No Phase 005 contract weakens REF-001–REF-030. Negative evidence still requires opportunity and bounded coverage. Causal confirmation still requires its evidence profile. Exposure, readiness, gate enforcement, safeguard prevention, and historical negative claims keep their Phase 004 evidence burdens.

### 10. Architecture neutrality — PASS

The authority model does not require a particular IAM product, RBAC/ABAC model, authority-rule engine, approval workflow, orchestrator, Databricks feature, GitHub Actions pattern, redaction technology, metric engine, graph database, or LLM. Concrete source mappings and technical realization remain later-phase work.
