## Phase-wide accepted invariants

### Authority does not manufacture truth

Assertion Authority can determine which governance assertion has standing. It cannot make an operational Observation occur, make evidence sufficient, make a Baseline comparable, prove a cause, prove control enforcement, or create compliance.

### Normative governance does not define empirical/statistical behavior

Phase 005 determines who may establish metric profiles, schema Expectations, thresholds, severity, waivers, and control-use eligibility. Phase 006 remains responsible for what metrics mean operationally, how Baselines compare, how structural compatibility is evaluated, and how health dimensions compose.

### Permission does not imply authority, action, or outcome

Capability Authorization answers whether a principal may perform or view an exact capability. Permission to edit/approve a governance rule does not automatically establish authoritative standing, and permission/approval does not prove action issuance, enforcement, or success.

### High-consequence control remains layered

Causal confirmation remains jointly evidence- and authority-gated. Gate override does not rewrite readiness. Safeguard release does not prove health. Break-glass does not create universal permission or evidence. Automation requires exact explicit grant.

### Restricted analysis remains useful without declassification

The framework may internally use restricted evidence only when independently authorized to process it. Requesters may receive exact state, authorized abstraction, opaque existence, or limitation according to their own capabilities. Hidden evidence is never treated as absent.

### Disclosure does not create another truth model

Technical, business, executive, client, and audit views may differ in detail, vocabulary, and visible basis. They cannot intentionally contradict or strengthen the same underlying state. Communication review may govern release but cannot make a statement true.

### Historical replay remains non-rewriting

Historical authoritative standing, permission, approval, action, disclosure, and retained Explanation remain distinct from present retrospective resolution and current requester access. Later correction can change present understanding without rewriting what was known, authorized, done, or communicated then.
