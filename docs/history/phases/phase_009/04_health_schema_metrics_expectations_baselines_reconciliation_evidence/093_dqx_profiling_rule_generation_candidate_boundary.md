# INTG-093 — DQX Profiling / Rule-Generation Candidate Boundary

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 009 Group 04

DQX profiling and generated/AI-assisted checks can propose useful metric/rule candidates from observed statistics and optional business context.

Generated rules remain proposals until governed Expectation authority accepts them. Statistical commonness or AI generation cannot manufacture normative thresholds, semantic applicability, severity, or Assertion Authority.
