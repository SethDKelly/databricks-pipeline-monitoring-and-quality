# INTG-002 — Proposition Binding & Truth-Owner Boundary

Every capability row names the **accepted proposition or proposition family** the surface may inform, plus the subject/scope/context for which that mapping applies.

A source contract states what evidence can be obtained; it does not move truth ownership from Entity Identity, Observation, Assessment, Deployment, Execution History, Lineage, Causal Claim, Impact, Safeguard, Gate, governance concepts or Explanation into the integration itself.

One surface may inform several propositions through separate bounded mappings. One proposition may require several source surfaces.

Queryable fields, convenient labels or vendor status values do not create a new umbrella product status.
