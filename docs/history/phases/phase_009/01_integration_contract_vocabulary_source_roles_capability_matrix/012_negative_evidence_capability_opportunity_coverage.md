# INTG-012 — Negative Evidence Capability & Opportunity Coverage

A capability that returns positive records is not automatically capable of proving absence.

For a strong negative, the contract records whether the source can establish the relevant opportunity/population/path/window, whether collection/query coverage is sufficient over that bound, whether the surface was operational and semantically closed for that proposition, and what exceptions/sampling/omissions exist.

`No record returned` while coverage, availability or opportunity is unknown remains `not evidenced`/unknown rather than `did not occur`.

Negative capability is proposition specific: support for `no run` does not establish `no output`, `not exposed`, `no effect` or `not enforced`.
