# EXPL-141 — Comparative Request Identity & View Binding

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 08

A comparative Explanation binds each compared side independently: subject/proposition, material scope, event/effective window, knowledge cut, requested perspective and audience/purpose context.

`before`/`after`, `then`/`now`, `incident`/`retrospective` and similar wording are not sufficient identities by themselves.

A comparison is valid only after each side resolves to a bounded proposition/view. Different propositions may be juxtaposed as context, but they are not represented as a same-proposition change.
