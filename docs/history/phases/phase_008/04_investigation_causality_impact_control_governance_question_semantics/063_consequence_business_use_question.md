# EXPL-063 — Consequence & Business-Use Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions about `business impact`, `customer impact`, `decision impact`, or `what did this cost/change operationally?` preserve technical, analytical and business/process consequence evidence separately.

Publication ≠ view; view ≠ decision reliance; reliance ≠ adverse consequence.

A consequence category organizes evidence but does not itself prove severity, monetary loss, policy breach or causal attribution.
