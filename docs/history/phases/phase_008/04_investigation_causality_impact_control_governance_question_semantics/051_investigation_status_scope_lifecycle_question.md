# EXPL-051 — Investigation Status, Scope & Lifecycle Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `what are we investigating?`, `is the investigation closed?`, or `what did it conclude?` route to Investigation's bounded question, scope, lifecycle, outcome and knowledge cut.

`closed` means the Investigation lifecycle closed for its recorded reason. It does not mean root cause was confirmed, all leads were rejected, remediation succeeded, or downstream Impact is resolved.

Scope revisions/reopenings remain historically visible.
