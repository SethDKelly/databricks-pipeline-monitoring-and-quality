# EXPL-073 — Control-Induced Effect & Causal Attribution Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Control facts may coexist with delay, missed delivery, stale serving, non-delivery, prevented exposure, or business consequences.

Narrow source-owned control facts remain Gate/Safeguard truth. Broader assertions that a Gate/Safeguard caused or prevented an operational/business outcome use the accepted Causal Claim standards, except the narrowly defined REF-028 prevented-exposure determination.

Competing causes such as scheduler/compute outage remain visible.
