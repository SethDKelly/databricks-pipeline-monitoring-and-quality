# EXPL-061 — Encounter Opportunity & Exposure Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `did consumer C see/use the bad data?` bind the exact originating state/version, consumer/use/interface/path, encounter mode, time and knowledge cut.

Preserve:

**opportunity ≠ availability/publication ≠ actual encounter ≠ suspect-state exposure**.

`Not exposed` requires the accepted path/opportunity/version coverage; a safe result on one path or missing telemetry cannot establish a global negative.
