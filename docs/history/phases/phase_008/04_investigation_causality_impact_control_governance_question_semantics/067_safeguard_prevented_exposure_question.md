# EXPL-067 — Safeguard Prevented-Exposure Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

`Did the safeguard prevent exposure?` is a derived proposition governed by REF-028/OPS-093, not a synonym for `Safeguard active` or `consumer not exposed`.

The answer requires the exact suspect state/consumer/use, materially operative encounter opportunity, protected path, evidence-established enforcement, negative suspect-state encounter on that path and sufficient alternate-path coverage.

No encounter opportunity means no prevention credit.
