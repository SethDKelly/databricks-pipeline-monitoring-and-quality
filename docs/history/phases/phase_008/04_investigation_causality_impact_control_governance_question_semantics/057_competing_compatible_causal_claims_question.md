# EXPL-057 — Competing & Compatible Causal Claims Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

When multiple Causal Claims remain applicable, answer them independently with their own role/status/basis.

Claims may be mutually exclusive alternatives, compatible contributors, sequential mechanisms, or independently unresolved.

No winner is selected by recency, graph distance, severity, Criticality, evidence-item count, analyst rank, model ranking or narrative simplicity.
