# EXPL-074 — Responsibility, Ownership, Accountability & Fault Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

`Who owns this?` binds the requested responsibility type/context/time: technical remediation, business accountability, stewardship, on-call, privacy/security responsibility, or another governed responsibility.

Responsibility Assignment ≠ Assertion Authority ≠ Capability Authorization ≠ causal responsibility ≠ policy breach/fault.

A responsible party may be the right contact without being the cause, authoritative semantic source, permitted operator, or blameworthy actor. Explanation must not infer fault from ownership.
