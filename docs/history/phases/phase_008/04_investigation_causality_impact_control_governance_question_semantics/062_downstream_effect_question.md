# EXPL-062 — Downstream Effect Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `did the report break?` or `what changed downstream?` route to exact downstream Observation/Assessment/Change evidence linked through Impact context.

Exposure can exist without a monitored effect. A downstream effect can be observed while exposure remains unknown.

`No effect` is a strong bounded conclusion requiring coverage for the relevant dimensions; one passing metric does not prove every dimension unchanged.
