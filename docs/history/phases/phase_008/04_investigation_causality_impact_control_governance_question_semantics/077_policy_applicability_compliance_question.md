# EXPL-077 — Policy Applicability & Compliance Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `what policy applies?` route to Policy Context for the exact subject/use/context/time and applicable authority standing.

Policy Context can state declared applicability/handling restrictions. It does not prove access enforcement, control operation, legal interpretation, policy breach or compliance.

`Are we compliant?` cannot be answered affirmatively/negatively from Classification, Policy Context, health, control configuration or absence of incidents alone unless a separately accepted compliance truth model later exists.
