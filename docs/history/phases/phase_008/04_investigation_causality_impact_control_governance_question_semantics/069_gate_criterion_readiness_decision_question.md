# EXPL-069 — Gate Criterion, Readiness & Decision Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `why was this blocked?`, `was it ready?`, or `what did the gate decide?` preserve:

**exact criterion/evidence suitability ≠ readiness result ≠ Gate decision basis/action**.

A HOLD may be normal, override-related, fallback-related or otherwise explicitly based; it does not imply the prerequisite was unhealthy unless that proposition is separately established.

Readiness becoming ready does not itself prove a later ADMIT.
