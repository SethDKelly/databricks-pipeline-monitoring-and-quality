# EXPL-059 — Causal Negative, Exclusion & Rejection Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Strong statements such as `X did not cause Y`, `X was not a contributor`, or `we ruled X out` require the applicable causal/negative evidence burden.

Lack of support, unavailable evidence, a later timestamp from an incomparable clock, or failure to reproduce is not automatically sufficient rejection.

A rejected Causal Claim and an excluded Investigation lead remain distinct propositions.
