# EXPL-060 — Impact Candidate & Reachability Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

Questions such as `what could be affected?`, `what is downstream?`, or `who is in the blast radius?` distinguish prospective/reachable candidates from realized Impact.

Lineage/path relevance may establish candidate context; it does not establish encounter opportunity, exposure, downstream effect or consequence.

Prospective blast radius and realized Impact remain separately labeled.
