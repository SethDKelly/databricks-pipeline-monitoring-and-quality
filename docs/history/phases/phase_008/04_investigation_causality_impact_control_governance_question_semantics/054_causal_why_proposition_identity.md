# EXPL-054 — Causal `Why` Proposition Identity

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 04

## Requirement

A causal answer binds an explicit cause→effect proposition with subject/state, effect, role, material mechanism/transmission context, relevant time/scope and knowledge perspective.

`Why did Y happen?` is not answered causally by merely listing temporally nearby Change, Deployment, Lineage, execution, health, reconciliation or control facts.

Those facts may be basis for a Causal Claim; they do not become the claim through prose.
