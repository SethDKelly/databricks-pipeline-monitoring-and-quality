# EXPL-026 — Internal Basis-Inspection Identity & Provenance

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02

## Requirement

Every material answer statement must support internal inspection back to the exact source concept state/evidence and derivation context used to compose it.

Internal basis identity retains, as applicable:

- statement proposition identifier/context;
- source concept/entity/reference;
- source state/proposition version or historical cut;
- evidence/provenance references;
- supporting/contradicting/limiting role;
- derivation/join rule when cross-concept;
- temporal perspective;
- authorization/redaction context applied at composition time.

## Boundary

This contract defines internal traceability, not what every audience may view. Group 06 governs authorized basis inspection and safe abstraction. Restricted basis can remain internally traceable while visible inspection is redacted/abstracted/denied.

## Invariants

Visible citation UI is not required here. Internal basis identity cannot be replaced by a prose-only assertion with no recoverable source linkage.
