# EXPL-024 — Multi-Statement Answer Composition & Sibling Independence

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02

## Requirement

Compose compound answers as sets/structures of independently traceable material statements rather than one synthetic answer truth.

For each subquestion/statement preserve independent:

- proposition identity;
- source owner/type;
- epistemic state;
- basis/limitations;
- temporal scope;
- authorization/disclosure state when later projected.

## Example

For `What failed, why, and who was affected?`, the answer may simultaneously contain:

- failed completeness Assessment;
- supported-but-unconfirmed Causal Claim;
- one exposed consumer, one not exposed consumer, and one unknown consumer.

The failed Assessment does not strengthen the cause; exposed consumer A does not determine consumer C.

## Invariants

No universal synthetic `overall answer confidence`, `overall incident state`, `root cause`, or `global impact` is created for narrative convenience.
