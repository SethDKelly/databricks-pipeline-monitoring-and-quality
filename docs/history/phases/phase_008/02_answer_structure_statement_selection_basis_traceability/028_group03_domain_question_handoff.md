# EXPL-028 — Group 03 Domain-Question Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02

## Requirement

Define the stable structural input Group 03 receives for health/change/execution question semantics.

For each bounded Group 01 subquestion, Group 03 may rely on Group 02 to preserve:

- stable material statement proposition identity;
- direct-projection versus derived-statement classification;
- source owner and epistemic type;
- question-relative materiality/statement role;
- supporting/contradicting/limiting basis;
- conclusion-relative sufficiency/burden;
- common-derivation/independence information;
- explicit cross-concept join rule when derived;
- summary/detail equivalence constraints;
- sibling-statement independence;
- internal statement-to-basis traceability;
- bounded answer coverage state.

## Handoff boundary

Group 03 may refine which health/change/execution propositions answer particular business questions and how accepted domain states should be communicated. It may not create new answer truth, relax evidence burdens, turn ordering/materiality into confidence, or redefine the structural traceability rules accepted here.

## Exit statement

EXPL-013–EXPL-028 are sufficient for domain-specific question semantics without adding an Answer concept.
