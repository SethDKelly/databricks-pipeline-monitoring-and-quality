# EXPL-023 — Omission, Compression & Material-Qualification Preservation

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02

## Requirement

Define safe omission/compression as removal of detail that is non-material to the bounded answer proposition while retaining any qualification whose absence would materially change interpretation.

Material qualifications can include:

- unresolved/conflicting causal state;
- partial consumer/path coverage;
- stale/non-current safe state;
- restricted evidence that materially limits specificity;
- historical knowledge-cut perspective;
- partial rollout/slice scope;
- control enforcement uncertainty.

## Invariants

- omission ≠ evidence of absence;
- hidden contradiction/limitation cannot be converted into an unqualified positive statement;
- narrower supported statement is preferred over broader plausible summary;
- a summary may say less, but cannot imply more.

Detailed uncertainty-language conventions are deferred to Group 05; this contract establishes the structural preservation requirement.
