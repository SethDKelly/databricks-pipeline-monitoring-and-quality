# EXPL-025 — Primary-Answer Selection & Ordering Without Universal Priority Score

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02

## Requirement

Allow Explanation to order/highlight material statements relative to the bounded question, purpose and audience context without treating ordering as epistemic strength or source authority.

Ordering may consider:

- directness to the requested conclusion;
- material business/operational relevance;
- explicit Criticality/priority context;
- need to surface a blocking limitation/contradiction;
- temporal sequence when the question requests chronology.

## Invariants

- first statement ≠ most true/authoritative;
- Criticality/priority ≠ evidence strength/probability/Impact;
- no universal answer-importance, severity, confidence or relevance score is accepted;
- ordering cannot suppress a material limitation needed to interpret the highlighted conclusion safely.
