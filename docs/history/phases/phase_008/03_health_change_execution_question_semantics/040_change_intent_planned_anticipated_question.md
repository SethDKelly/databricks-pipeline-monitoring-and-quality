# EXPL-040 — Change Intent, Planned & Anticipated Question

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 03

## Requirement

`Was this planned?`, `what was supposed to change?`, and `was this effect expected?` must bind the exact **Change Intent** revision/component, target/facet and anticipated effect.

## Rules

- a registered intent supports only the bounded planned proposition it actually declares;
- no matching known intent ≠ proven unplanned human change;
- an undeclared effect is `not declared/anticipated in the registered intent`, not automatically `unintended`;
- intent does not prove deployment, activation, realization, health or cause;
- overlapping intents remain separate unless accepted association evidence supports comparison.

Strong `unplanned` wording requires the OPS-017 planning/governance evidence burden.
