# EXPL-090 — Absence of Evidence versus Evidence of Absence

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Explanation must distinguish missing/insufficient evidence from an evidence-backed negative conclusion.

Strong negatives such as `no run`, `no output`, `not exposed`, `no effect`, `no consequence`, `not realized`, `not enforced`, `no bypass`, `unassigned`, or `unplanned` require their accepted opportunity/coverage/authority burden.

Negative-form user wording never lowers that burden.
