# EXPL-099 — Historical Epistemic Language Evolution

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Historical Explanation must distinguish the epistemic state at the requested knowledge cut from current retrospective interpretation.

`Unknown at 10:15` can coexist with `retrospectively established from evidence learned at 11:05`. Later evidence may resolve, weaken, reject or confirm a proposition without rewriting what was legitimately unknown/conflicting/unavailable earlier.

`Not known by cutoff` never becomes a claim that the real-world event had not occurred.
