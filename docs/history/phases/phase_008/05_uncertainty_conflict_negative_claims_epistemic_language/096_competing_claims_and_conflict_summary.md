# EXPL-096 — Competing Claims and Conflict Summary

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

When several materially relevant propositions disagree or compete, summarize the disagreement without manufacturing a winner.

Explanation may identify what each claim asserts, its current status, material supporting/contradicting/limiting basis, and any accepted resolver/authority outcome. Ordering or prominence remains presentation and does not change epistemic state.

Do not convert plurality into a confidence-weighted ranking by basis count or source count.
