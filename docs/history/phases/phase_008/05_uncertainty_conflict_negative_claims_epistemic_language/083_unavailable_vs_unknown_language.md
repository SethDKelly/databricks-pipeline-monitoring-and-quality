# EXPL-083 — Unavailable versus Unknown Language

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Distinguish inability to obtain/use a required evidence source (`unavailable`) from inability to resolve the proposition after considering the usable basis (`unknown`/`unresolved`).

Unavailable telemetry may explain why a proposition remains unresolved, but the evidence-source condition and the proposition result are not interchangeable.

Do not phrase unavailable evidence as proof that an event/control/path did not exist.
