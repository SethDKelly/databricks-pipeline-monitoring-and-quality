# EXPL-098 — Cross-Statement Uncertainty Aggregation Discipline

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

A multi-statement Explanation does not receive one derived uncertainty/confidence state by averaging or collapsing its statements.

One statement may be confirmed, another supported, another conflicting, another unavailable and another evidence-backed negative. Preserve these states independently.

No universal Explanation confidence, certainty, completeness, risk, RCA or answer-quality score is accepted.
