# EXPL-093 — Impact / Exposure Negative Language

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 05

## Requirement

Preserve the accepted Impact vocabulary rather than collapsing uncertainty into `unaffected`.

Distinguish at least candidate/reachable, no relevant encounter opportunity, exposed, not exposed, safe/other-state encounter, encountered-state unknown, downstream effect established/unknown/no-effect where proven, and consequence established/unknown/no-consequence where proven.

One safe consumer/path never licenses `nobody affected`.
