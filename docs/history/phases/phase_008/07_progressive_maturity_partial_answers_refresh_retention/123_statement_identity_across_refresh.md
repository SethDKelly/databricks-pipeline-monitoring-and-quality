# EXPL-123 — Statement Identity Across Refresh

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

Group 02 proposition identity persists across refresh when requested conclusion, subject, material scope, event/effective time and knowledge perspective remain the same.

Wording, ordering, visible detail, citation rendering or basis-detail visibility may change without creating a new material proposition.

A changed subject/scope/time/knowledge perspective is a different proposition and must not be presented as a simple status revision of the old one.
