# EXPL-139 — Retention, Reconstructability & Missing Snapshots

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

Retained Explanation history and reconstructible Explanation history are different.

- Retained snapshot: evidence of actual prior composed/communicated content and projection context.
- Reconstructed prior Explanation: a present derivation using historical source state/knowledge cut and current permitted disclosure rules unless another context is explicitly modeled.

Missing retained history must remain missing; reconstruction cannot be labeled as the exact message shown then.

Concrete retention periods/storage architecture are deferred.
