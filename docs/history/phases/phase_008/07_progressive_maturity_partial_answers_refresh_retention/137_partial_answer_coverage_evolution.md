# EXPL-137 — Partial Answer Coverage Evolution

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

Refresh may move individual subquestions among answered, partially answered, unresolved, unavailable, restricted, not-applicable or other accepted states as evidence and authorization change.

Coverage changes are tracked by bounded proposition/subquestion. The framework does not derive a universal percentage-complete, maturity, confidence or answer-quality score from the number of resolved statements.
