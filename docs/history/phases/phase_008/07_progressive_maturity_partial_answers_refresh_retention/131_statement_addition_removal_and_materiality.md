# EXPL-131 — Statement Addition, Removal & Materiality

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 07

A statement can enter or leave the current Explanation because evidence changed, the underlying proposition changed, materiality changed, or authorized visibility changed.

`Removed from the current answer` therefore does not mean `false`, `retracted`, `resolved`, `nonexistent` or `no longer relevant in reality` unless the owning proposition establishes that meaning.

Delta history should preserve the reason class where available.
