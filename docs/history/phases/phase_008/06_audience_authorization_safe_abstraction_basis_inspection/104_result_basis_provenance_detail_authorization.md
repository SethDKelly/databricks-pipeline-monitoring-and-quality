# EXPL-104 — Result, Basis, Provenance & Detail Authorization

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Preserve AUTH-045 separation among visibility of a result, its supporting/contradicting/limiting basis, source/provenance identity, authority/authorization metadata and exact values/details.

Authorization to see a conclusion does not automatically authorize every Observation, threshold, schema detail, Lineage edge, causal source, confirmer identity or control record behind it.

Internal Group 02 statement-to-basis traceability remains complete even when visible basis inspection is partial or absent.
