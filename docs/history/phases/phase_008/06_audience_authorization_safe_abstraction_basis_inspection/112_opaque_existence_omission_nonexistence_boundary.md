# EXPL-112 — Opaque Existence, Omission & Nonexistence Boundary

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

`Hidden`, `redacted`, `omitted`, and `opaque existence` are not interchangeable with `does not exist`.

Acknowledging that a restricted entity/evidence item exists is itself a disclosure and requires authorization. When opaque existence is not permitted, Explanation may omit the item or provide only an independently authorized general limitation; it must not expose item count/type/identity through phrasing.

Omission can never be used as negative evidence.
