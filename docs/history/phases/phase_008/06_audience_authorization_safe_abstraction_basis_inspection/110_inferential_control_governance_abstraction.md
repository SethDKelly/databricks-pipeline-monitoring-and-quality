# EXPL-110 — Causal, Impact, Control & Governance Abstraction

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

When exact causal, Impact, control, responsibility, policy, authority or authorization detail is restricted, any visible coarse statement must retain the Group 04/05 layer and status.

Examples: confirmed causal status may be visible without confirmer identity; an exposure result may be visible without consumer name; a Gate override may be visible without operator identity; special handling may be visible without policy text.

The abstraction may not convert these into blame, compliance, unrestricted access, prevention, recovery or other stronger propositions.
