# EXPL-101 — Projection Request, Audience, Purpose & Delivery Binding

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Before visible Explanation composition, bind the requester/current principal, intended audience, purpose/use, subject/context, temporal perspective, delivery mode and material information/detail classes through accepted AUTH-044/AUTH-024–032 semantics.

Audience labels such as `engineer`, `business`, `executive`, `client` or `auditor` are descriptive context only. They do not grant access, publication rights or basis visibility.

A private answer, exported report, forwarded summary and client-facing publication can therefore resolve differently even when they refer to the same underlying proposition.
