# EXPL-113 — Mixed Authorization Within a Derived Statement

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

For a Group 02 derived cross-concept statement, conclusion authorization and basis authorization are evaluated separately.

A derived conclusion may be disclosed with restricted basis only when the conclusion itself already exists as an internally valid, provenance-bearing proposition and its disclosure is authorized under AUTH-030/031/044–053. Explanation may not inspect otherwise hidden facts and invent a new coarse inference merely because the final sentence seems less sensitive.

Restricted inputs therefore do not grant an inference privilege to Explanation.
