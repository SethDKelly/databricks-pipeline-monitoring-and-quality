# EXPL-106 — Epistemic Monotonicity of Authorized Abstraction

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Safe abstraction may preserve the exact proposition or weaken/reduce detail, but it may not strengthen scope, polarity, causal role, epistemic status, negative coverage or authority standing.

Examples:

- `supported contributor` cannot become `confirmed root cause`;
- `exposure unknown` cannot become `not exposed`;
- `HOLD issued` cannot become `HOLD enforced`;
- `restricted basis` cannot become `no contradictory evidence`;
- `policy applies` cannot become `compliant`.

Authorized projection is therefore epistemically monotone.
