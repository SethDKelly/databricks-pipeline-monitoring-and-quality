# EXPL-115 — Restricted Visible Basis & Internal Traceability

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Visible basis restriction never deletes, weakens or rewrites the internal statement-to-basis linkage required by EXPL-013–EXPL-028.

An Explanation may show `basis restricted` while retaining exact internal support/contradiction/limitation provenance for authorized audit/review paths. Conversely, a visible citation/reference does not transfer authority or permission to inspect the referenced material.
