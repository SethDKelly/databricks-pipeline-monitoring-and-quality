# EXPL-120 — Group 07 Progressive-Maturity Handoff

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Group 07 receives an Explanation model in which:

- material proposition identity and internal basis are stable;
- source/epistemic state is preserved;
- current visible projection is separately authorized by conclusion/context/basis/detail/delivery;
- safe abstraction is epistemically monotone;
- restricted detail does not become absent;
- mixed authorization can narrow or withhold statements without changing internal truth;
- cross-audience consistency does not require identical detail;
- historical and current authorization/disclosure remain separate;
- retained prior Explanation snapshots preserve what was actually communicated.

Group 07 may refine answer refresh/maturity/retention but may not use newly available evidence or newly broadened authorization to silently rewrite an earlier retained Explanation.
