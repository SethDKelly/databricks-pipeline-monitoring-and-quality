# EXPL-102 — Requester, Target Audience & Delegation Boundary

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 06

## Requirement

Distinguish the principal requesting/composing an Explanation from the target audience that will receive it. Permission for the requester to inspect a fact does not imply permission to disclose that fact to another audience.

If composition/export/publication is performed on behalf of another audience, the relevant disclosure capability and delivery context must be independently satisfied. Explanation does not infer delegation from organizational role, responsibility, prior access or possession of the underlying evidence.
