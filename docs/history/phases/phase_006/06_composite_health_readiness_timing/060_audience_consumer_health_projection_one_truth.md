# HLTH-060 — Technical, Business, Executive & Consumer Health Projection over One Truth

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 006 Group 06

## Purpose

Define audience/use-specific health projections without creating separate technical and business health realities.

## Rules

- Audience-specific views project from the same underlying component/composite Assessments and accepted profile semantics.
- Different legitimate use profiles can produce different bounded composite propositions; that is not the same as rewriting one proposition for different audiences.
- A technical view may expose exact metric/check/component detail while a business/executive view exposes a smaller authorized summary.
- Reduced detail cannot strengthen `indeterminate`, `conflicting`, `unavailable`, `degraded`, waived, or warning state into `healthy`.
- Hidden/restricted component identity remains restricted rather than absent; a safe limitation/opaque component can remain visible where authorized.
- Consumer-specific structural/freshness/business criteria can make one consumer-use composite healthy while another is degraded or unresolved.
- Disclosure governance from AUTH-044–AUTH-053 continues to determine which details may be shown.
- Explanation/UX wording remains a later phase concern; Phase 006 defines the underlying health semantics only.

## Invariant

One underlying truth can support multiple authorized projections, but not contradictory health truth for the same bound profile/use/time proposition.
