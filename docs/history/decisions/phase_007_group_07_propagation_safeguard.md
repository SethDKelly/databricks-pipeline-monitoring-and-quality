# Decision Records — Phase 007 Group 07 Propagation Safeguard

Continues after D-539.

### D-540 — Group 07 requires no new concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 07
Propagation Safeguard remains protection-control owner; prevention remains a derived cross-concept result and recovery remains source-owned.

### D-541 — Bind every safeguard to exact protected state/surface/scope/time
**Status:** Accepted
Generic asset-wide `quarantined` state is insufficient when version/path/cohort scope differs.

### D-542 — Decompose safeguard lifecycle/action facts
**Status:** Accepted
Proposal, authorization, request, enforcement, extension, expiry and release facts remain independently evidenced.

### D-543 — Protection placement is an explicit semantic boundary
**Status:** Accepted
No universal upstream-most/downstream-most/least-disruptive placement rule is accepted.

### D-544 — Applicability is path/opportunity/scope specific
**Status:** Accepted
Active interval overlap alone does not prove a safeguard was material to a consumer opportunity.

### D-545 — Activation/request is not enforcement
**Status:** Accepted
REF-027 evidence remains required for effective protection.

### D-546 — Enforcement is independently resolved across paths/cohorts/regions
**Status:** Accepted
Partial enforcement is first-class and does not collapse to global success/failure.

### D-547 — Reject universal enforcement/protection percentages
**Status:** Accepted
Aggregate ratios cannot replace material protected/unprotected/unknown members.

### D-548 — Alternate paths must be considered before global protection/prevention
**Status:** Accepted
One controlled path cannot hide an unresolved or exposed material route.

### D-549 — Bypass possibility and bypass occurrence are distinct
**Status:** Accepted
Theoretical reachability alone is not actual control bypass.

### D-550 — Prevented exposure is derived, not a safeguard lifecycle state
**Status:** Accepted
It composes Safeguard enforcement with Group 06 Impact/path/opportunity evidence under REF-028.

### D-551 — `Active + not exposed` is insufficient for prevention
**Status:** Accepted
Material opportunity/control/path evidence is still required.

### D-552 — No encounter opportunity does not create safeguard prevention credit
**Status:** Accepted
A validly active safeguard may simply be incidental to non-exposure.

### D-553 — Safe prior-state serving can be protection plus staleness
**Status:** Accepted
Blocked suspect V does not imply fresh/current/healthy delivery.

### D-554 — Missing output is not a quarantined object
**Status:** Accepted
Safeguard may instead hold current-cycle advancement/presentation or another propagation boundary.

### D-555 — Protection success does not suppress downstream effects/consequences
**Status:** Accepted
Delay, staleness and non-delivery remain Observation/Assessment/Impact facts.

### D-556 — Extension/renewal/scope revision is separately bounded and authorized
**Status:** Accepted
Revised protection does not rewrite prior intervals or scope.

### D-557 — Scheduled expiry and effective expiry are distinct
**Status:** Accepted
Clock/configuration alone is not runtime proof unless applicable semantics/evidence establish effective expiry.

### D-558 — Release request/authorization is not effective release
**Status:** Accepted
External removal of protection is independently evidenced.

### D-559 — Release may be partial
**Status:** Accepted
Path/cohort-specific release does not imply all protection ended.

### D-560 — Release does not create health/currentness/recovery truth
**Status:** Accepted
Post-release state is independently observed/assessed.

### D-561 — Propagation Safeguard does not own recovered state
**Status:** Accepted
Execution/Observation/Assessment/Impact retain post-protection state ownership.

### D-562 — Degraded control telemetry preserves unknown/conflicting enforcement
**Status:** Accepted
Missing telemetry is not control success/failure or fail-open/fail-closed behavior.

### D-563 — Configured fallback is not actual fallback application
**Status:** Accepted
REF-029 remains binding and no universal fallback policy is chosen.

### D-564 — Overlapping safeguards retain independent identity and materiality
**Status:** Accepted
First activated/last released does not automatically identify the effective protector.

### D-565 — Safeguard-induced operational effects remain separate domain evidence
**Status:** Accepted
Protection state does not own delay/stale/non-delivery truth.

### D-566 — Broader control-effect attribution belongs to Causal Claim
**Status:** Accepted
The REF-028 bounded prevented-exposure determination is the narrow exception; delay/business-effect causality uses REF-013–REF-020.

### D-567 — Historical safeguard reconstruction is bitemporal/non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted
Late enforcement/exposure evidence can revise retrospective prevention without rewriting actual actions or then-known state.

### D-568 — Propagation Safeguard and Execution Gate remain separate controls
**Status:** Accepted
Safeguard protects output/publication/consumption; Gate controls start/admission. Neither state implies the other's action.

### D-569 — OPS-086–OPS-104 and SG07-01–SG07-36 are accepted; Group 07 exits complete
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24. OPS-001–OPS-104 is accepted through Group 07; SYN-001–SYN-035, REF-001–REF-030, AUTH-001–AUTH-053 and HLTH-001–HLTH-066 remain unchanged. Phase 007 Group 08 — Execution Gate, Fallback/Override & Control-Induced Operational Effects is next.
