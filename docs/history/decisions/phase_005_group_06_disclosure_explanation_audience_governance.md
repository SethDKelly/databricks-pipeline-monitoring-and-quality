# Decision Records — Phase 005 Group 06 Disclosure, Explanation & Audience Governance

Continues after D-234.

### D-235 — Group 06 requires no new concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Capability Authorization and Authorized Analytical Projection govern what an audience may receive; Explanation owns composed/retained communication state; source concepts retain truth. No separate Audience View, Disclosure, or Report truth concept is required.

### D-236 — Material disclosure is audience/purpose/context/delivery scoped
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Disclosure binds requester/audience, information class/detail, subject/context, purpose, temporal perspective, and delivery scope. Audience labels never grant permission by themselves.

### D-237 — Result visibility and basis visibility are independently governable
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
An audience may receive an Assessment/causal/control result without exact metrics, thresholds, evidence, source identities, authority rules, or actor identities. Restricted basis remains restricted rather than absent.

### D-238 — Safe abstraction must preserve proposition meaning
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Redaction, ranges, categorical summaries, and opaque existence may reduce detail only when separately authorized and semantically valid. They must not create false absence, false precision, or stronger status.

### D-239 — Inference leakage is evaluated compositionally
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Individually permitted facts can combine through counts, topology, timing, differencing, or repeated narrowing to disclose restricted state. Composite/repeated-query leakage is therefore a disclosure-governance concern.

### D-240 — Technical, business, executive, and audit projections share one truth
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Audience projections may differ in vocabulary, detail, and visible basis but cannot intentionally contradict or strengthen the same underlying concept state.

### D-241 — View permission does not imply publish/disclose permission
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Private inspection, export, forwarding, business communication, client disclosure, and retained external/audit publication may require independently governed capabilities/conditions.

### D-242 — High-consequence communication review cannot create truth
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Review/approval may be required before release of specified causal, consequence, compliance-adjacent, control, or external incident statements, but communication approval cannot make evidence sufficient, cause confirmed, control enforced, data healthy, or policy complied with.

### D-243 — Disclosure must preserve epistemic/normative/control status
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Simplified wording cannot turn supported into confirmed, reachable into exposed, not-exposed into fresh, hold-decision into enforced hold, active safeguard into prevented exposure, release into health, waiver into clean pass, or authoritative standing into factual infallibility.

### D-244 — Actor, authority, authorization, and control metadata can be independently sensitive
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
A result/action status may be visible while confirmer, approver, operator, authority holder, delegation, break-glass, service-principal, or governing-rule details remain opaque. Internal provenance remains retained.

### D-245 — Human-context disclosure preserves attribution class
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
If Annotation content is disclosed while author identity is restricted, the material must still be represented as human-provided context where that distinction matters; it must not be transformed into machine Observation or authoritative fact.

### D-246 — Historical disclosure is distinct from historical truth and current disclosure
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Actual retained historical Explanation/communication, reconstructed as-known-then Explanation, retrospective Explanation, historical authorization, and current requester disclosure authorization remain separate.

### D-247 — Current authorization governs present historical disclosure
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
Facts disclosed to a historical actor are not automatically repeatable to a current requester, and current access does not imply the requester possessed the same access historically.

### D-248 — Unknown/conflicting/unavailable disclosure state never becomes permission
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
A system may withhold disclosure under unresolved state, but operational refusal does not fabricate an explicit deny or imply the hidden fact does not exist. No universal disclosure fail-open/fail-closed rule is accepted.

### D-249 — Group 06 scenario suite passes
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 06
DISC-01–DISC-26 pass across audience-specific metric detail, opaque topology/causality, control state, high-consequence publication, Annotation, historical replay, and mosaic leakage without a new concept or architecture choice.
