# Decision Records — Phase 008 Group 03 Health, Change & Execution Question Semantics

Continues after D-676.

### D-677 — Group 03 requires no new domain-question concept
**Status:** Accepted
Health/change/execution questions project accepted HLTH/OPS truth through Explanation.

### D-678 — Operational shorthand must decompose to exact propositions
**Status:** Accepted
`good`, `healthy`, `current`, `live`, `ran` and similar terms do not create a universal state.

### D-679 — Execution occurrence and lifecycle outcome remain separate
**Status:** Accepted
`ran` does not imply `succeeded`, failed, cancelled or complete lifecycle evidence.

### D-680 — Execution success does not establish output existence
**Status:** Accepted
Output is independently evidenced and can exist after failed/partial execution.

### D-681 — Output existence does not establish currentness, freshness or health
**Status:** Accepted
Qualification and health retain separate propositions.

### D-682 — Freshness/currentness is exact-use/current-cycle bound
**Status:** Accepted
Latest successful execution/output is not a substitute for the governing temporal proposition.

### D-683 — Health answers require an explicit dimension/profile/use binding
**Status:** Accepted
No intrinsic universal asset health state is introduced.

### D-684 — Different legitimate health profiles can yield different bounded results
**Status:** Accepted
This is proposition difference, not audience-driven truth rewriting.

### D-685 — Structural compatibility, realized structure and statistical comparability remain separate
**Status:** Accepted
Engine compatibility or successful execution does not collapse these distinctions.

### D-686 — Baseline typicality is not normative health
**Status:** Accepted
Typical/unusual and meets/violates can coexist independently.

### D-687 — Warning, waiver, severity and priority do not rewrite criterion truth
**Status:** Accepted
A waived violation remains a violation; warning may coexist with meets.

### D-688 — Reconciliation result does not create causal truth
**Status:** Accepted
Mismatch can localize/support inquiry without becoming root cause.

### D-689 — `What changed?` routes to realized Change
**Status:** Accepted
Intent or deployment activity is not substituted for evidence-established before/after Change.

### D-690 — `Was it planned?` binds exact Change Intent revision/component
**Status:** Accepted
Registered intent proves only its declared proposition.

### D-691 — No matching known intent does not prove unplanned change
**Status:** Accepted
Strong `unplanned` retains OPS-017 planning/governance evidence requirements.

### D-692 — Deployment attempt, outcome and activation remain separate
**Status:** Accepted
Successful CI/workflow does not automatically prove runtime activation.

### D-693 — Activation does not prove downstream effect
**Status:** Accepted
Change/health/Impact remain independently evidenced.

### D-694 — Intent-to-realization match/divergence does not establish health or cause
**Status:** Accepted
Comparison remains a derived bounded result only.

### D-695 — Run-specific version answers require Execution History binding evidence
**Status:** Accepted
Current deployment state is not enough by convenience.

### D-696 — Active Deployment does not automatically establish run implementation state
**Status:** Accepted
Run-specific composite facets may remain unknown.

### D-697 — Latest upstream output does not establish consumed input
**Status:** Accepted
Consumption requires encounter/version evidence.

### D-698 — Retry, restart, rerun and backfill retain source-specific continuity semantics
**Status:** Accepted
Repeated activity is not normalized into one universal run notion.

### D-699 — Dependency, actual precedence, waiting and consumption remain distinct
**Status:** Accepted
Each stronger proposition requires its own basis.

### D-700 — Expected work, opportunity, Gate state and execution remain separate
**Status:** Accepted
No phantom execution is created from scheduling/control context.

### D-701 — Strong `no run/output/consumption` answers require bounded negative evidence
**Status:** Accepted
Missing telemetry remains a limitation.

### D-702 — Operational timing/lateness does not create causal attribution
**Status:** Accepted
Overlap with deployment, outage or control is not proof of cause.

### D-703 — Historical domain answers preserve event time and knowledge cut
**Status:** Accepted
As-known historical state and current retrospective interpretation may differ non-rewritingly.

### D-704 — Compound operational questions preserve sibling independence
**Status:** Accepted
Run, output, freshness, health, change and version statements do not resolve one another by grouping.

### D-705 — Causal `why` questions retain Causal Claim requirements
**Status:** Accepted
Group 03 supplies direct state evidence; Group 04 owns inferential question refinement.

### D-706 — No universal operational-status/health/version-confidence score is introduced
**Status:** Accepted
Question convenience cannot collapse independent domain states into a scalar.

### D-707 — EXPL-029–EXPL-049 and HCE03-01–HCE03-36 are accepted; Group 04 is next
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24 and Phase 008 proceeds to Investigation, Causality, Impact, Control & Governance Question Semantics.
