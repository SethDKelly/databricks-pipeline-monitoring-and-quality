# Decision Records — Phase 004 Group 03 Causal Epistemics and Confirmation

This file continues the durable decision sequence after D-110 in [`phase_004_group_02_time_knowledge_correction.md`](phase_004_group_02_time_knowledge_correction.md).

### D-111 — Causal propositions must bind cause, effect, role, context, and time

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

A material Causal Claim must identify the proposed causal condition, defined outcome, relevant subjects/context/time, causal role, and material mechanism/transmission assumptions. `Upstream`, `recently changed`, `deployed`, or `first observed` is not a complete causal proposition.

### D-112 — Accept six first-class causal epistemic statuses

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

The accepted functional vocabulary is `proposed`, `supported`, `weakened`, `unresolved`, `rejected`, and `confirmed`. Status history is append-only in meaning and transitions are not required to follow a linear ladder.

### D-113 — `Unresolved` means evaluated but non-discriminating; `rejected` requires evidence

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

`Unresolved` is distinct from `proposed`: substantive evaluation has occurred but evidence remains insufficient/conflicting/non-discriminating/unavailable/restricted. `Rejected` is stronger than lack of support and requires sufficient contradiction/exclusion evidence under the applicable claim standard.

### D-114 — Causal support/contradiction is multidimensional, not one score

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Cause/effect occurrence, temporal ordering, relationship/mechanism applicability, encounter/transmission, semantic direction, contrasts/interventions, alternatives, and negative/exclusion coverage are evaluated as applicable to the claim. The framework does not sum them into a universal causal confidence number.

### D-115 — Material alternatives are bounded by Investigation scope

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Stronger causal status considers a bounded set of material alternatives known under the relevant evidence cut. The framework does not require elimination of every imaginable cause. Compatible contributors are not treated as mutually exclusive merely because multiple claims exist.

### D-116 — `Confirmed` is a separate claim-specific evidence gate

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Confirmation is not `strongly supported` or `leading hypothesis`. An explicit claim-class confirmation profile/standard must identify the required evidence dimensions/conditions. Different causal classes may legitimately require different evidence profiles.

### D-117 — Confirmation has common minimum conditions

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Every confirmation requires a sufficiently bound proposition, sufficient cause/effect evidence, required causal ordering/mechanism/transmission conditions, review of material contradiction and alternatives, sufficient coverage for relied-upon exclusions, named/versioned confirmation-standard provenance, resolved confirmation authority/capability, and a provenance-bearing confirmation action.

### D-118 — Phase 004 does not assign confirmation authority

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Neither a human title nor an automated process receives causal-confirmation authority by default. Phase 005 will refine authority/capability/source-precedence semantics. Automated confirmation remains possible only if a later accepted standard explicitly permits it, authorization resolves appropriately, and the evidence gate is satisfied.

### D-119 — Multiple causal contributors are first-class

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

The framework does not require one root cause. Multiple claims may remain supported or confirmed when causally compatible. Support/confirmation of one contributor does not automatically reject another.

### D-120 — Qualitative causal roles do not imply quantitative attribution

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Roles such as direct, contributing, enabling, triggering, preventing, primary, or unresolved role can be used where evidence supports them. `Primary` is a comparative assertion requiring comparative evidence. No role implies a percentage attribution.

### D-121 — RCA maturity may progress without waiting for confirmation

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

The framework should surface the strongest epistemic status justified by currently known evidence: proposed/candidate results may appear quickly, supported/weakened/unresolved results may follow, and retrospective confirmation review may occur later. Elapsed time does not create evidence, and latency goals never justify status inflation.

### D-122 — Confirmed claims remain challengeable and historically preserved

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 03

Material late/corrected evidence can cause a currently confirmed claim to be reevaluated and move to supported, weakened, unresolved, or rejected. The earlier confirmation event, evidence cut, confirmer, and applicable standard remain reconstructable rather than being erased.

### D-123 — Group 03 exit gate satisfied; Group 04 is next

**Status:** Accepted

REF-013–REF-020 establish causal proposition binding, epistemic status, multidimensional evidence evaluation, alternative discrimination, confirmation gating/authority separation, multiple contributors, progressive RCA maturity, and post-confirmation challenge semantics. No new Concept or Phase 003 synchronization is required.
