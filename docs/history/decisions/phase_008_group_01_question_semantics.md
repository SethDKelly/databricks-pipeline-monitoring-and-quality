# Decision Records — Phase 008 Group 01 Question Semantics

Continues after D-628.

### D-629 — Review Phase 008 as eight dependency-ordered functional groups
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 foundation
The grouping is question contract; answer structure/basis; health/change/execution questions; Investigation/causality/Impact/control/governance questions; uncertainty/negative language; audience/authorization; progressive maturity/refresh; historical/comparative consolidation.

### D-630 — Use EXPL-### for Phase 008 refinement contracts
**Status:** Accepted
The namespace refines business questioning and Explanation; it does not create an Explanation subsystem architecture or a new `Answer` concept.

### D-631 — Group 01 requires no new Question concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 01
The initiating question/request remains bounded Explanation request context. Source concepts retain substantive truth ownership.

### D-632 — Question identity is proposition/context based rather than wording based
**Status:** Accepted
Requested conclusion, subject, scope and temporal context identify the material question; natural-language equality is insufficient.

### D-633 — Question family is descriptive routing metadata, not truth or authority
**Status:** Accepted
Exact requested conclusion remains authoritative over family labels; no closed universal question taxonomy is required.

### D-634 — Resolve question targets through Entity Identity
**Status:** Accepted
Names/aliases alone cannot silently select environment/version/historical subjects when identity is ambiguous.

### D-635 — Question scope is proposition specific
**Status:** Accepted
Environment, version, run, consumer, population, metric/profile, path, control and time dimensions remain explicit when material.

### D-636 — Broad wording does not justify broad inference
**Status:** Accepted
A narrower evidence-supported proposition must not be generalized merely because the user asked an asset-wide or consumer-wide question.

### D-637 — Event/effective time and knowledge cut are independent question coordinates
**Status:** Accepted
Historical question semantics inherit the accepted bitemporal model.

### D-638 — A past-event question without an earlier knowledge cut is current retrospective by default
**Status:** Accepted
It must not be presented as `what was known then` unless that perspective is actually bound by request context.

### D-639 — Current, as-known historical and current retrospective questions remain distinct
**Status:** Accepted
They may legitimately produce different answers without contradiction.

### D-640 — Compound questions may decompose into independent subquestions
**Status:** Accepted
Decomposition follows proposition/truth-owner/evidence-burden boundaries rather than prose convenience.

### D-641 — One answered subquestion cannot strengthen an unresolved sibling
**Status:** Accepted
Health/execution/Impact/cause/control statements retain independent epistemic state in one Explanation.

### D-642 — Route each requested conclusion to its accepted truth owner
**Status:** Accepted
Explanation selects/composes but never becomes health, cause, Impact, control, governance or authorization truth owner.

### D-643 — Causal `why` wording does not relax Causal Claim requirements
**Status:** Accepted
Lineage, timing, intent, Deployment or localization evidence cannot be promoted to cause through question phrasing.

### D-644 — Question intent does not grant evidence access or operational capability
**Status:** Accepted
Asking for a fact/action is independent from Capability Authorization and current disclosure.

### D-645 — Answerability is separate from semantic applicability, evidence availability/sufficiency, integration support and authorization
**Status:** Accepted
These limitations remain distinct and may coexist.

### D-646 — Partial answerability is valid
**Status:** Accepted
A compound or broad question can have supported subanswers while other conclusions remain unresolved/restricted/unavailable.

### D-647 — Material ambiguity must remain explicit or be resolved through bounded interpretations
**Status:** Accepted
The framework does not silently choose a convenient entity, profile, scope, time or meaning simply to answer.

### D-648 — Shorthand such as healthy/okay/safe/affected/blocked/planned/owner must resolve to underlying accepted propositions
**Status:** Accepted
No universal asset health, safety, Impact or control state is created for conversational convenience.

### D-649 — Negative-form questions retain the exact source negative-evidence burden
**Status:** Accepted
Missing evidence cannot become `no run`, `not exposed`, `no effect`, `no path`, `not enforced`, `unplanned` or another strong negative.

### D-650 — EXPL-001–EXPL-012 and BQ01-01–BQ01-24 are accepted; Group 02 is next
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24. Phase 008 proceeds to Answer Structure, Statement Selection & Basis Traceability.
