# Decision Records — Phase 005 Group 01 Authority Vocabulary and Conflict Resolution

Continues after D-160.

### D-161 — Assertion Authority is the 24th accepted concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Repeated cross-concept source-precedence/conflict/history behavior requires an independent truth owner. Assertion Authority resolves authoritative standing for bounded assertion targets without owning the underlying assertion, evidence sufficiency, permission, or enforcement.

### D-162 — Authority is target-, context-, and time-scoped
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
No source/actor/vendor is globally authoritative. Authority resolves against a bound concept/category/facet/scheme/type, subject scope, context, effective interval, and knowledge cut where relevant.

### D-163 — Recording an assertion and granting authoritative standing are separate
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Source assertions remain provenance-bearing regardless of standing. Advisory/non-authoritative assertions may enrich or challenge state without displacing authoritative resolution.

### D-164 — Assertion Authority and Capability Authorization are independent
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Permission to submit/revise an assertion does not make it authoritative. Authoritative standing does not grant unrelated read, edit, operational, safeguard, gate, or confirmation capability.

### D-165 — Responsibility, policy, classification, scope, title, and ownership do not imply assertion authority
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Responsibility Assignment, Policy Context, Classification, Monitoring Scope, repository ownership, job creator identity, administrator status, organizational title, or technical ownership cannot silently grant authoritative standing.

### D-166 — Hidden precedence is prohibited
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Source count/majority, recency alone, synchronization/ingestion order, availability, repository ownership, and apparent scope specificity are not precedence rules. More-specific-wins behavior must be explicit if desired.

### D-167 — Co-authoritative disagreement remains authoritative conflict
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Multiple holders may be explicitly co-authoritative. If their assertions disagree and no accepted resolver applies, preserve authoritative assertion conflict rather than choosing a winner.

### D-168 — Explicit precedence and fallback require accepted rules and evidenced conditions
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Sole authority, co-authority, ordered precedence, and conditional/fallback authority are permitted only when explicitly defined. An unavailable primary does not promote another source unless an accepted fallback rule applies and its condition is evidenced.

### D-169 — Authority rules require provenance and governing basis
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Authority rules cannot self-validate. Conflicting authority rules remain conflict unless an accepted governing/higher-order rule resolves them. Phase 005 does not select the technical trust-root mechanism.

### D-170 — Authority history is bitemporal and non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Prospective revisions, supersession, corrections, and late discovery remain distinct. Later correction can revise retrospective authority resolution without rewriting what authority was known/used at an earlier cutoff.

### D-171 — Authority cannot manufacture evidence sufficiency, factual correctness, or enforcement
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 01
Authoritative standing does not waive REF-001–REF-030, prove a physical/operational fact, prove compliance, or prove an external control operated. Authoritative assertions can be corrected or challenged.

### D-172 — Group 01 exit gate satisfied; Group 02 next
**Status:** Accepted
Assertion Authority plus AUTH-001–AUTH-008 provide the common authority/conflict vocabulary needed by later Phase 005 groups. Group 02 — Semantic, Responsibility, Classification, Policy & Criticality Governance is next and has not started.
