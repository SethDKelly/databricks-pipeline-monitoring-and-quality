# Decision Records — Phase 007 Group 03 Prospective Blast Radius / Change-Aware Review

Continues after D-440.

### D-441 — Group 03 requires no new concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 03
Prospective scenario topology and change-aware review remain derived views over Change Intent, Lineage, Impact, health/governance concepts and authorization state.

### D-442 — Bind prospective review to exact intent revision/component and evaluation cut
**Status:** Accepted
Latest intent/current topology cannot be substituted for what was actually reviewed.

### D-443 — Planned topology is evaluated through a scenario overlay, not written into Lineage
**Status:** Accepted
Effective relationships and planned additions/removals/modifications retain distinct provenance/state.

### D-444 — Planned removals create path-loss/change candidates
**Status:** Accepted
A current downstream consumer does not disappear merely because the proposal would remove its dependency.

### D-445 — Impact remains the candidate/reachability truth owner
**Status:** Accepted
Prospective candidate basis may be effective-path, planned-added-path, path-loss/change or indeterminate; none establishes exposure/effect/consequence.

### D-446 — Candidate relevance is field/key/population/interface/consumer/version scoped
**Status:** Accepted
Asset reachability cannot automatically answer a narrower semantic question.

### D-447 — Prospective structural compatibility reuses Phase 006 semantics
**Status:** Accepted
Compatibility against proposed state is proposal-bound and never proof of realized production compatibility.

### D-448 — Change-aware health/reference review is scoped, not a global reset
**Status:** Accepted
Metric/profile/Expectation/Baseline/composite review follows material semantic dependencies of the proposal.

### D-449 — A prospective Baseline break is not empirical non-comparability
**Status:** Accepted
Change Intent may prepare/review a break; realized context and evidence establish actual comparability.

### D-450 — Transformation changes trigger exact reconciliation review
**Status:** Accepted
Join/filter/aggregation/dedupe/merge/null/cast/current-cycle changes do not create generic downstream metric propagation or predicted reconciliation failure.

### D-451 — Readiness/control assumptions may be reviewed without creating control state
**Status:** Accepted
Review of prerequisites, health suitability or AUTH-023 eligibility does not enable/configure/decide a Gate or Safeguard.

### D-452 — Separate analytical review relevance from governed obligation, approval and control
**Status:** Accepted
Topology/review evidence cannot manufacture policy or deployment enforcement.

### D-453 — Authority over review/use cannot manufacture evidence truth
**Status:** Accepted
AUTH-020 and related authority rules govern decisions; they do not establish topology, compatibility or comparability by authority alone.

### D-454 — Criticality affects priority/context, not probability or Impact
**Status:** Accepted
High criticality cannot strengthen path evidence or prove likely exposure/consequence.

### D-455 — Reject a universal prospective risk score
**Status:** Accepted
No numeric/qualitative universal risk, severity-weighted blast-radius, path-count or shortest-path score is accepted.

### D-456 — Candidate completeness is bounded and authorization-aware
**Status:** Accepted
Known candidates may coexist with non-exhaustive topology; restricted/opaque paths remain limitations rather than absence.

### D-457 — `No candidate/no blast radius` is a strong negative conclusion
**Status:** Accepted
It requires sufficient bounded topology, semantic relevance and alternate-path coverage.

### D-458 — Mixed rollout remains slice-specific
**Status:** Accepted
Active canary evidence may inform remaining prospective review but cannot be projected as future-slice realized fact.

### D-459 — Prospective review history is non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted
Retained historical review, reconstructed as-known-then review and retrospective recomputation remain distinct.

### D-460 — Group 04 must reconstruct actual execution/version use independently
**Status:** Accepted
Proposed versions, scenario topology, candidate paths and review findings cannot substitute for runtime evidence.

### D-461 — OPS-021–OPS-033 and P03-01–P03-30 are accepted; Group 03 exits complete
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24. OPS-001–OPS-033 is accepted through Group 03; SYN-001–SYN-035, REF-001–REF-030, AUTH-001–AUTH-053 and HLTH-001–HLTH-066 remain unchanged. Phase 007 Group 04 — Execution Reconstruction, Dependency Sequence & Version Use is next.
