# Lineage Relationship Taxonomy, Historical Topology & Operational Relevance

**Canonical key:** `operations.lineage-topology`

**Kind:** CONTRACT

**Authority:** CANONICAL CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.OPS`

**Owns current question:** How are typed Lineage relationships, historical topology, evidence, traversal relevance and completeness represented without turning graph reachability into status, exposure, Impact or causality?

**Stable IDs:** OPS-001–OPS-009

## Current semantics

Operational reasoning chain: **bounded Lineage proposition → evidence-backed relationship resolution → question-bound traversal relevance → bounded topology completeness/historical replay**.

### OPS-001 — Lineage Relationship Proposition Identity & Direction
Bind each relationship to exact source, target, direction, family/role, semantic scope, context/version, effective interval and evidence basis; an unqualified edge is insufficient.

### OPS-002 — Minimum Operational Lineage Relationship Taxonomy
Use at least data_derivation, production, operational_dependency, publication and consumption_path as semantic families; none proves health, execution, exposure or cause.

### OPS-003 — Relationship Scope, Granularity & Semantic Role
Preserve asset/field/key/population/consumer/interface/version scope and relationship role so broad reachability cannot answer a narrower operational question.

### OPS-004 — Effective Topology, Historical Time & Relationship Lifecycle
Separate planned topology, effective topology, historical event/effective intervals and framework knowledge time; later discovery may improve retrospective topology without rewriting as-known state.

### OPS-005 — Relationship Evidence, Existence/Absence & Coverage
Resolve exact relationship existence as established/absent/unknown/conflicting/unavailable; `absent` requires opportunity-to-observe plus sufficient bounded coverage.

### OPS-006 — Assertion Authority, Source Disagreement & Empirical Separation
Keep governed Assertion Authority for relationship assertions separate from empirical evidence sufficiency and source disagreement; no universal source precedence applies.

### OPS-007 — Traversal Profile, Operational Relevance & Path Composition
Bind traversal to question, direction, allowed families, time/cut, semantic scope, depth and authorization; multi-hop relevance requires semantic composition rather than graph distance.

### OPS-008 — Topology Completeness, Missing Edges & Restricted Projection
Topology completeness is bounded to relationship universe, scope, time/cut, depth and evidence/authorization coverage; restricted or missing edges are not absence.

### OPS-009 — Topology Transition, Cross-Concept Ownership & Group 02 Handoff
Relationship transitions remain Lineage truth; realized Change, execution, exposure, Impact, control and causality stay with their own concepts.

## Invariants / boundaries

- Lineage ≠ causality.
- Lineage relation ≠ metric/status/governance propagation.
- reachable ≠ operationally relevant ≠ exposed/affected.
- planned topology ≠ effective topology.
- current topology ≠ historical topology.
- missing edge evidence ≠ absent edge.
- Assertion Authority ≠ evidence sufficiency.
- operational dependency ≠ Execution Gate state/enforcement.
- consumption path ≠ exposure.

## Cross-concept ownership

OPS refinement coordinates accepted concepts; it does not create an `Operations` truth owner. Lineage, Change Intent, Deployment, Change, Execution History, Investigation, Causal Claim, Impact, Propagation Safeguard and Execution Gate retain their accepted concept ownership. REF governs evidence/negative/causal proof; AUTH governs assertion/capability/high-consequence authority; HLTH governs health, evidence suitability and readiness inputs.

## Historical / disclosure rule

Event/effective state, framework knowledge cut and current retrospective interpretation remain distinct. Current requester authorization controls present disclosure; restricted or unavailable evidence is not absence and a safe projection cannot strengthen underlying truth.

## Architecture boundary

This contract is implementation-neutral. It does not select graph/event storage, source integrations, orchestration/control mechanisms, scoring algorithms, persistence schema, polling/streaming behavior or concrete operational SLAs.
