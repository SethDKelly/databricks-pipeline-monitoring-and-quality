# Semantic, Responsibility, Classification & Policy Authority

**Canonical key:** `auth.governance-authority`

**Kind:** AUTHORITY

**Authority:** CANONICAL CURRENT AUTHORITY

**Migration record:** `stable_family.AUTH`

**Owns current question:** Who/what is authoritative for descriptive semantic/governance assertions and how are derivation, local context and cross-facet conflict bounded?

**Stable IDs:** AUTH-009–AUTH-015

## Current semantics

### AUTH-009 — Semantic Facet and Schema-Meaning Authority
Business definition, technical description/schema declaration, grain, units, population, calculation meaning, field roles and key roles may have different authorities. Governed schema meaning ≠ normative compatibility Expectation ≠ realized schema Observation/Change.

### AUTH-010 — Responsibility-Type Authority and Assignment Governance
Technical, business, stewardship, security/privacy, operational and platform responsibilities are independently governed assignment types. Assignment does not grant broader authority or access.

### AUTH-011 — Classification-Scheme and Criticality Authority
Classification/criticality authority is scheme/context specific. Criticality may prioritize work but does not prove exposure, effect, consequence, health or cause.

### AUTH-012 — Policy-Context Applicability Authority
Authority over policy text/reference may differ from authority over subject/context applicability. Classification can inform applicability but cannot manufacture it; Policy Context ≠ authorization/enforcement/compliance/legal interpretation.

### AUTH-013 — Contextual Overrides, Local Governance, and Cross-Facet Conflict
Context-specific assertions can coexist. Narrow/local assertions do not implicitly outrank broader ones; same-target conflict follows explicit AUTH-001–AUTH-008 rules.

### AUTH-014 — Derived, Inherited, and Propagated Governance Assertions
Lineage, repository/container membership, tags, parent state or inference do not silently propagate semantics, responsibility, classification, policy, criticality or authority. Derived assertions require provenance and standing.

### AUTH-015 — Governance State Separation from Normative Health and Operational Truth
Descriptive governance authority cannot become schema/data health, Impact, Capability Authorization, control-enforcement or compliance truth.

## Invariants / boundaries

Declared/governed schema meaning ≠ normative schema contract ≠ realized schema state ≠ compatibility Assessment. Declared key role does not prove uniqueness/nullability.
