# Decision Records — Phase 004 Group 05 Consolidation and Exit

Continues after D-139.

### D-140 — REF-001–REF-030 are sufficient for Phase 004 exit
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
The accepted refinement range composes across E-01–E-22 and the Phase 004 scenario checks without a new Concept, Phase 003 synchronization, or REF-031. Group 05 is consolidation/exit review rather than a new refinement behavior group.

### D-141 — Negative-evidence semantics are unified across domains
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Run/output absence, `not known by cutoff`, causal exclusion, non-exposure, control suppression, and prevented-exposure conclusions all require an adequate opportunity to observe plus sufficient bounded coverage appropriate to the proposition. Missing/restricted/unavailable evidence is never a reassuring negative.

### D-142 — Progressive analytical availability does not weaken evidence burden
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
The framework may expose the narrowest trustworthy result as soon as its evidence is known. Immediate operational validation, enriched health, RCA, and post-ops review may mature at different times. Fast-path availability never promotes execution success into health, gate decision into enforcement, support into confirmation, or safeguard state into prevention.

### D-143 — Evidence availability and production-path dependency remain separate
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Passive monitoring remains out-of-band/non-blocking for ungated production. Explicit gates are active controls and may require stronger/faster evidence/control availability. Phase 004 defines this semantic split but does not set SLOs, vendor mechanisms, or architecture.

### D-144 — Internal evidence sufficiency does not grant disclosure or control authority
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
A conclusion may be internally sufficiently evidenced while a requester sees only an authorized safe abstraction/limitation. Evidence sufficiency, source authority, Capability Authorization, operational authority, safeguard/gate authority, and causal-confirmation authority remain independent.

### D-145 — Causal epistemics compose with progressive RCA and multiple contributors
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Proposed/supported/weakened/unresolved/rejected/confirmed status, bounded alternatives, compatible contributors, claim-class confirmation profiles, and challenge/reversal remain coherent across runtime, data, Deployment, gate, safeguard, downstream effect, and business-consequence scenarios. No forced single root cause or universal score is required.

### D-146 — Exposure and health remain independent after consolidation
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Candidate/reachability, affected-state encounter, non-exposure, downstream effect, consequence, and causal attribution remain distinct. Safe prior-version encounter can establish non-exposure to a suspect state while freshness/delivery is independently stale or degraded.

### D-147 — Readiness, decision, enforcement, and execution remain separate conclusions
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Prerequisite predicates produce a criterion-relative readiness result; readiness may inform a gate decision; a decision requires separate enforcement evidence; actual downstream execution remains Execution History. No configured/enabled gate or fallback policy proves opportunity-specific enforcement.

### D-148 — Safeguard enforcement and prevented exposure remain separate conclusions
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Safeguard proposal/configuration/activation records do not by themselves establish boundary enforcement or prevented exposure. Prevention requires material enforced effect on the relevant path plus sufficient negative-consumption/version and alternate-path coverage.

### D-149 — Historical reevaluation is non-rewriting across every Phase 004 domain
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Late/corrected evidence may revise current Assessment, causal, exposure, readiness, control-enforcement, and prevention conclusions while preserving what evidence was known, what action occurred, what status was recorded, and what Explanation existed at the earlier knowledge cut.

### D-150 — Source authority remains deliberately unresolved by Phase 004
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 05
Evidence applicability/coverage/sufficiency and conflict preservation do not decide which source/actor is authoritative. Majority vote, source count, recency alone, repository ownership, or refinement order cannot substitute for Phase 005/009 authority semantics.
