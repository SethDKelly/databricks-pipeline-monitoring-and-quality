# Decision Records — Phase 007 Group 05 Investigation / Localization / Causal Handoff

Continues after D-484.

### D-485 — Group 05 requires no new concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 05
Investigation remains bounded-inquiry owner; Causal Claim remains causal-proposition/epistemic owner. Localization and leads are Investigation state, not new concepts.

### D-486 — Bind every Investigation to an exact question/outcome/scope/time/knowledge cut
**Status:** Accepted
Scope revisions are historical and cannot silently rewrite the original inquiry.

### D-487 — Investigation lifecycle is independent of causal status
**Status:** Accepted
Open/active/paused/closed/reopen behavior does not imply any Causal Claim transition.

### D-488 — Candidate leads are inquiry state, not hidden causal hypotheses
**Status:** Accepted
Lead generation basis/provenance/limitations remain explicit; lead disposition organizes inquiry only.

### D-489 — Investigation links source-owned evidence rather than copying truth
**Status:** Accepted
Evidence roles are inquiry-relative while source concept semantics/provenance remain authoritative.

### D-490 — Contradicting evidence and gaps remain first-class
**Status:** Accepted
Leading-theory convenience cannot suppress contradiction, restriction, conflict or missing evidence.

### D-491 — Distinguish first observed deviation from earliest evidenced state change
**Status:** Accepted
Neither is the earliest true deviation or cause by default.

### D-492 — Transformation-boundary localization is a separate proposition
**Status:** Accepted
First reconciliation/structural boundary mismatch may localize propagation without explaining why it occurred.

### D-493 — First downstream consumer effect remains distinct from upstream localization
**Status:** Accepted
Impact owns downstream effect/exposure/consequence semantics.

### D-494 — Lineage traversal/reachability/path length never becomes causal ranking
**Status:** Accepted
Localization uses question-bound relevance and bounded coverage only.

### D-495 — Execution/version/change contrasts are evidence, not cause
**Status:** Accepted
First post-change run, shared version, rollback/retry contrast and temporal precedence can support/contradict later claims without automatic promotion.

### D-496 — Multiple simultaneous deviations and branches are first-class
**Status:** Accepted
Investigation never requires one winning root cause.

### D-497 — Lead exclusion requires bounded negative/discriminating evidence
**Status:** Accepted
Lack of support or missing telemetry is not exclusion/rejection.

### D-498 — Causal language triggers explicit Causal Claim handoff
**Status:** Accepted
Any assertion that X caused/contributed/enabled/triggered/prevented/materially influenced Y belongs in Causal Claim.

### D-499 — Investigation priority/localization does not transfer as Causal Claim status
**Status:** Accepted
Claims begin/evolve only under REF-013–REF-020 evidence semantics.

### D-500 — Investigation cannot own or mutate Causal Claim epistemic status
**Status:** Accepted
It may reference/display claim state only.

### D-501 — Confirmation remains REF-017 plus AUTH-034 gated
**Status:** Accepted
Closure, consensus, remediation, model output or incident role cannot substitute for confirmation evidence/authority.

### D-502 — Operational resolution and causal resolution are independent
**Status:** Accepted
The framework can close/narrow/mitigate an operational problem while causal claims remain unresolved or merely supported.

### D-503 — Unresolved/no-actionable-conclusion closure is valid
**Status:** Accepted
Investigation quality is not measured by whether a root cause was produced.

### D-504 — Historical Investigation state is bitemporal and non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted
Current retrospective localization does not replace what investigators knew at an earlier cut.

### D-505 — Material late/corrected evidence can justify reopen
**Status:** Accepted
Reopen preserves prior closure and follows REF-011 materiality rather than rewriting history.

### D-506 — Restricted/opaque evidence limits localization without becoming absence
**Status:** Accepted
Investigation visibility does not bypass evidence authorization/disclosure.

### D-507 — Analysts and automation use the same evidence/provenance discipline
**Status:** Accepted
Origin does not create evidence strength, truth or confirmation authority.

### D-508 — Reject universal RCA confidence/risk/hypothesis scoring
**Status:** Accepted
No graph-distance, severity, Criticality, vote, model-confidence or elapsed-time formula is accepted as causal probability.

### D-509 — Prospective blast-radius membership does not become retrospective causal support by itself
**Status:** Accepted
Incident-time realized topology/execution/evidence remains necessary.

### D-510 — Group 06 must establish Impact encounter/effect independently
**Status:** Accepted
Investigation localization and even confirmed upstream causality do not manufacture consumer exposure or consequence.

### D-511 — OPS-050–OPS-066 and I05-01–I05-34 are accepted; Group 05 exits complete
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24. OPS-001–OPS-066 is accepted through Group 05; SYN-001–SYN-035, REF-001–REF-030, AUTH-001–AUTH-053 and HLTH-001–HLTH-066 remain unchanged. Phase 007 Group 06 — Impact, Consumer Encounter, Exposure & Consequence is next.
