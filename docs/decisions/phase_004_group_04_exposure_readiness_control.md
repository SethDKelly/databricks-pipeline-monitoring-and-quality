# Decision Records — Phase 004 Group 04 Exposure, Consumption, Readiness & Control Evidence

Continues after D-123.

### D-124 — Bind exposure to affected state and encounter mode
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
A material exposure determination identifies affected subject/state/version/window, downstream candidate, historical relationship, encounter mode, consumer opportunity/window, and exact exposure conclusion. Reachability/timing alone are insufficient.

### D-125 — Positive exposure requires actual encounter evidence
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Execution/refresh/use evidence must sufficiently associate the consumer with the affected state. Generic downstream activity or availability is not proof of consumption/use.

### D-126 — Non-exposure requires bounded negative-consumption coverage
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
`Not exposed` requires adequate opportunity-to-observe plus sufficient coverage of relevant encounter paths. Missing telemetry, inaccessible consumers, unresolved versions, or unknown alternate paths cannot become a reassuring negative.

### D-127 — Safe-version use, inactivity, and unknown exposure remain distinct
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
`No encounter opportunity`, `no encounter`, `safe-version encounter`, `unknown-version encounter`, `unavailable evidence`, and `affected-state encounter` remain distinct. Safe-version use may still be stale.

### D-128 — Readiness is relative to an explicit prerequisite criterion
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
No upstream subject is globally `ready`. Readiness resolves against an explicit gate/downstream context and criterion/profile. Successful execution alone is sufficient only when that is the whole criterion.

### D-129 — Readiness predicates do not substitute for each other
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Completion, output existence, identity/version, currentness, freshness, publication availability, and named quality conditions remain independently evaluable when required. An unresolved required predicate is not readiness; fallback does not convert uncertainty into readiness.

### D-130 — Gate decision, enforcement, and actual execution are separate
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
A readiness result is not a gate decision; a decision/request is not automatically external enforcement proof; and gate state is not Execution History.

### D-131 — Hold and admit enforcement are evidentially asymmetric
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
A reliable run during an applicable unoverridden hold contradicts full hold enforcement. Lack of a run supports hold enforcement only with sufficient opportunity/history coverage. An admit not followed by a run does not prove admission failed because the gate only removes its own barrier.

### D-132 — Configured gate state does not prove opportunity-specific enforcement
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Enforcement is bound to the specific target, execution opportunity, decision, control boundary/mechanism, effective interval, and evidence source.

### D-133 — Safeguard enforcement is boundary- and scope-specific
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Proposal/configuration/request/intent is not enforcement. Active/enforced state requires evidence for the exact protected context, boundary, consumer/path scope, and interval.

### D-134 — Prevented exposure requires material control effect
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
`Safeguard active + consumer not exposed` is insufficient when no relevant encounter opportunity existed or the safeguard was incidental. Prevention requires enforced control on the relevant path, sufficient negative consumption/version evidence, and adequate alternate-path coverage.

### D-135 — Blocking suspect state does not prove fresh/healthy delivery
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
A consumer may be protected from affected V while receiving an older state or no delivery. Exposure, freshness, delivery, and quality remain separate.

### D-136 — Fallback policy is not proof of actual fallback behavior
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Configured unavailable-state behavior is intended control semantics. Actual recognition, application, enforcement, and downstream outcome require evidence. Missing control telemetry does not prove fail-open, fail-closed, success, or failure.

### D-137 — Control-effect causal claims use the accepted causal standard
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Direct deterministic mechanism evidence can justify strong support quickly when the applicable profile is met, but broader delay/non-delivery consequence claims still require REF-013–REF-020 review and confirmation authority when confirmation is asserted.

### D-138 — Late evidence revises retrospective conclusions, not historical actions
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 04
Late enforcement, execution, refresh, version, or consumption evidence may revise current readiness/exposure/prevention/causal conclusions. It does not rewrite earlier gate decisions, safeguard actions, actual executions, or Explanations.

### D-139 — Group 04 exit gate satisfied; Group 05 is next
**Status:** Accepted
REF-021–REF-030 establish the required exposure/readiness/control evidence standards. No new Concept or Phase 003 synchronization is required. Phase 004 Group 05 — Consolidation / Exit Review is next and has not started.
