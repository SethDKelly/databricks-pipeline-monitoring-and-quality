# Decision Records — Phase 004 Group 01 Evidence Sufficiency and Coverage

This file continues the durable decision sequence after D-088 in [`phase_003_group_06_historical_replay_and_exit.md`](phase_003_group_06_historical_replay_and_exit.md).

### D-089 — Evidence sufficiency is conclusion-relative, not a universal evidence score

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Evidence is evaluated against a bounded proposition/conclusion, subject/context/time, and explicit decision standard. The same evidence can be sufficient for a narrow existence claim and insufficient for a broader absence, exclusivity, exposure, readiness, or causal claim. The framework does not attach a universal `trust` or `confidence` score to evidence.

### D-090 — Evidence applicability precedes support/contradiction

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Evidence must align sufficiently with the target subject, property/semantics, time, grain/version, derivation, and relevant relationship context before it can legitimately support, contradict, or exclude a proposition. Proximity or graph reachability alone is not applicability.

### D-091 — Coverage is bounded and multidimensional

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Coverage is always stated relative to an explicit observation universe/window and can include temporal, population/partition, source/query, identity/version, measurement, sampling, instrumentation, and derivation dimensions. `Complete coverage` without a declared universe is not accepted terminology.

### D-092 — Negative/absence evidence requires opportunity to observe

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

A negative conclusion requires a mechanism capable of detecting the relevant event/state if it occurred and sufficient coverage of the bounded opportunities where it could have occurred. Empty retrieval alone is not evidence of absence.

### D-093 — Missing telemetry, unavailable evidence, and restricted evidence are not negative facts

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Query failure, monitoring outage, unavailable integration, authorization restriction, out-of-scope evidence, or unresolved identity/version association produces an evidence limitation/unknown state. These conditions must not be represented as zero, false, no event, not exposed, or excluded.

### D-094 — Apparent source multiplicity does not imply independent corroboration

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Duplicated, mirrored, or commonly derived evidence must not gain artificial strength merely because it appears in multiple stores or systems. Provenance should preserve common derivation/duplication where known. Complementary evidence can jointly satisfy different required parts of a conclusion without being treated as independent copies of the same fact.

### D-095 — Applicable conflicts remain explicit unless an accepted authority rule resolves them

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Conflicting applicable evidence is preserved. The framework does not use majority vote, recency alone, synchronization order, repository ownership, or source count to silently choose a winner. Detailed source-authority/precedence rules remain a Phase 005 concern.

### D-096 — Sufficiency evaluation is separate from user disclosure authorization

**Status:** Accepted — Phase 004 Group 01

Capability Authorization can restrict which basis details a requester may see without changing the underlying evidence relationship. A safe Assessment/Impact/claim result may be disclosed with redacted basis where independently authorized. If the framework itself cannot access evidence required by the standard, however, that is an evidence-availability/sufficiency limitation rather than mere presentation redaction.

### D-097 — Group 01 exit gate satisfied; Group 02 is next

**Status:** Accepted

REF-001–REF-005 establish the common evidence applicability, coverage, negative-evidence, corroboration/conflict, and conclusion-specific sufficiency framework needed by later Phase 004 groups. No new Concept or Phase 003 synchronization is required.
