# Decision Records — Phase 006 Group 07 Consolidation / Exit

Continues after D-382.

### D-383 — Group 07 requires no new concept and no HLTH-067
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 006 Group 07
Groups 01–06 compose using existing concepts and HLTH-001–HLTH-066. No Composite Health, Reconciliation, Metric, Threshold, Readiness Suitability or other new truth owner is required, and no additional Phase 006 refinement contract is needed.

### D-384 — Phase 006 health reasoning remains a layered proposition chain
**Status:** Accepted
Measurement identity/evidence, structural/comparability context, component Assessment, transformation reconciliation, composite Assessment, freshness/maturity, exact-use suitability and readiness remain distinct propositions. Success at one layer never manufactures the next.

### D-385 — Applicability, selection, support, availability and outcome remain independent
**Status:** Accepted
Semantic applicability, profile selection, source support/computability, current availability, Observation and Assessment outcome cannot be collapsed into one metric status.

### D-386 — Structural compatibility and empirical comparability remain independent
**Status:** Accepted
Consumer-specific structural compatibility does not prove historical/statistical comparability. Structural change triggers scoped review rather than global metric/Baseline reset.

### D-387 — Baseline typicality remains descriptive and independent from normative health
**Status:** Accepted
Typical behavior can violate an Expectation and atypical behavior can meet it. Baseline adaptation never converts chronic violation into normative acceptance.

### D-388 — Evidence uncertainty and reference limitations survive normative evaluation
**Status:** Accepted
Low volume, sparse reference history, approximation/sampling uncertainty, non-comparable references, source unavailability and conflict remain material to the exact conclusion. Authority/threshold existence cannot manufacture precise pass/fail.

### D-389 — Warning, severity, waiver and criterion truth remain separate through composition
**Status:** Accepted
Warning/proximity, severity/priority, Baseline typicality and waiver/disposition never replace the bound criterion outcome. `violates + waived response` remains a violation.

### D-390 — Transformation reconciliation remains explicit and non-propagating
**Status:** Accepted
Lineage does not copy metric values or statuses. Joins, filters, aggregation, dedupe, union/merge/upsert, value derivation and multi-input alignment use exact transformation/version semantics, and derived reconciliation retains source limitations.

### D-391 — Reconciliation/localization remains below causality
**Status:** Accepted
A first deviation, join mismatch or reconciled discrepancy can materially localize Investigation evidence but cannot by itself confirm a causal proposition or downstream Impact.

### D-392 — Composite health remains profile/use/context bound and dimension preserving
**Status:** Accepted
No universal majority, weighted average or numeric health score is canonical. Composite shorthand is valid only under explicit component roles/logic and preserves decisive violations plus unresolved/conflicting/unavailable/waived qualifiers.

### D-393 — Different consumer health conclusions can be simultaneously valid
**Status:** Accepted
Consumer/use-specific profiles can yield different bounded health propositions over the same underlying evidence. Audience projection may reduce authorized detail but cannot change a fixed proposition's truth.

### D-394 — Evaluation recency remains distinct from evidence freshness
**Status:** Accepted
Recomputing a result now does not make old evidence current. Freshness/staleness is relative to the exact use and current-cycle/version requirements; no universal TTL is accepted.

### D-395 — Progressive maturity is evidence-driven
**Status:** Accepted
Immediate operational, fast core/schema, enriched DQ/reconciliation/distribution, diagnostic and retrospective horizons mature when their required evidence is sufficient. Elapsed time never upgrades result maturity.

### D-396 — Narrow trustworthy results do not wait for broader slow evidence
**Status:** Accepted
Supported narrow facts may be emitted immediately while broader composite health remains pending. Later enriched results can revise the broader conclusion without rewriting earlier valid narrow facts.

### D-397 — Readiness suitability is exact-use and outcome-neutral
**Status:** Accepted
A suitable violation can support `not ready`; a stale/unsuitable `meets` result cannot support `ready`. Suitability preserves evidence, comparability, cycle, availability and maturity requirements.

### D-398 — AUTH-023 eligibility and Phase 006 suitability remain independent
**Status:** Accepted
Control-use eligibility cannot make stale/unavailable/immature/non-comparable evidence suitable, and suitable evidence cannot create high-consequence eligibility.

### D-399 — Health, suitability, readiness and control behavior remain distinct
**Status:** Accepted
Health Assessment ≠ evidence suitability ≠ readiness result ≠ gate/control authorization ≠ gate decision ≠ enforcement ≠ actual execution. Unavailable evidence does not imply fail-open or fail-closed behavior.

### D-400 — Passive monitoring remains non-blocking by default
**Status:** Accepted
Monitoring or enriched-health degradation does not delay ungated production. Only an explicit active-control configuration may make selected evidence a prerequisite.

### D-401 — Historical health replay is bitemporal and non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted
Historical analysis binds then-effective metric/schema/reference/Expectation/transformation/composite/freshness/readiness rules and the knowledge cut. Late evidence or new rules may produce retrospective reassessment without rewriting historical Observations/Assessments or actual control behavior.

### D-402 — Phase 006 selects no technical implementation architecture
**Status:** Accepted
Metric computation, DQX/Metric Views, schema-validation placement, storage, streaming/cache, concrete TTL/SLA, scheduler/gate realization and control availability remain later integration/architecture work.

### D-403 — Phase 007 consumes the completed health model rather than reopening it
**Status:** Accepted
Phase 007 owns Lineage/change/Investigation/Impact/safeguard/execution-control refinement and consumes HLTH-001–HLTH-066 as established health semantics. It may define operational relationships/policies but must not reinterpret health, comparability or normative meaning by convenience.

### D-404 — Group 07 consolidation scenarios pass
**Status:** Accepted
H07-01–H07-36 pass without a new concept, HLTH-067, universal score, false pass, blind propagation, causal shortcut, stale-evidence shortcut, control conflation or architecture selection.

### D-405 — Phase 006 exits complete
**Status:** Accepted
