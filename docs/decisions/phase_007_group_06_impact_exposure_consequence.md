# Decision Records — Phase 007 Group 06 Impact / Exposure / Consequence

Continues after D-511.

### D-512 — Group 06 requires no new concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 06
Impact remains candidate/exposure/effect/consequence association owner; source facts and causality retain their existing owners.

### D-513 — Bind realized Impact to exact origin, consumer, state, use and time
**Status:** Accepted
Generic `affected` labels are insufficient where state/version/use scope is ambiguous.

### D-514 — Encounter opportunity is distinct from actual encounter
**Status:** Accepted
Opportunity can exist without use; no opportunity is a separate bounded proposition.

### D-515 — Availability/publication/serving remain below actual downstream use
**Status:** Accepted
Each consumer boundary is separately evidenced.

### D-516 — Encounter evidence is consumer-mode specific
**Status:** Accepted
Execution, materialization, query, cache, application and business use need not share one evidence mechanism.

### D-517 — Adopt bounded exposure vocabulary without a boolean shortcut
**Status:** Accepted
`exposed`, `not exposed`, `safe/other-state encounter`, `encountered-state unknown`, `no relevant encounter opportunity`, `indeterminate`, `conflicting`, `unavailable` remain distinct.

### D-518 — No universal consumer version token is required
**Status:** Accepted
Native provenance/state identities may differ by consumer class.

### D-519 — Group 04 run/version evidence can establish exposure only when encounter binding is sufficient
**Status:** Accepted
Run/refresh timing alone remains insufficient.

### D-520 — Safe prior-state encounter can mean non-exposure while still stale
**Status:** Accepted
`not exposed to suspect V` does not imply fresh/current/healthy.

### D-521 — Cache/replica/snapshot state is first-class encounter context
**Status:** Accepted
Copied state may lag, transition or remain safe independently of source state.

### D-522 — Exposure is not transitively propagated through Lineage
**Status:** Accepted
Material intermediary encounter/output bindings are required for indirect exposure propositions.

### D-523 — Alternate encounter paths are evaluated before global non-exposure
**Status:** Accepted
One excluded path cannot hide an unknown/exposed alternate path.

### D-524 — `Not exposed` requires REF-023 path/opportunity/version coverage
**Status:** Accepted
Missing telemetry remains an evidence limitation.

### D-525 — No opportunity, no encounter and safe-state encounter remain distinct
**Status:** Accepted
They may support different operational conclusions even when suspect-state exposure is absent.

### D-526 — Exposure is event/interval specific rather than permanent
**Status:** Accepted
Repeated and later-safe/later-exposed states preserve history.

### D-527 — Downstream effect remains independent from exposure
**Status:** Accepted
Exposure can exist without monitored degradation; effects can exist while exposure is unknown.

### D-528 — `No downstream effect` requires bounded downstream coverage
**Status:** Accepted
No observed issue or satisfied one-dimensional criterion is not a global negative.

### D-529 — Consequence uses descriptive technical/analytical/business categories without universal severity
**Status:** Accepted
Categories organize evidence rather than manufacturing harm.

### D-530 — Human/business use and decision consequence require use-bound evidence
**Status:** Accepted
Publication/view/use/decision/consequence are not synonyms.

### D-531 — Criticality/Classification/priority do not establish realized Impact
**Status:** Accepted
Context can affect response priority only.

### D-532 — Origin→effect/consequence attribution belongs to Causal Claim
**Status:** Accepted
Impact layers remain evidence for, not substitutes for, causal propositions.

### D-533 — Confirmed upstream cause does not establish every consumer's exposure or outcome
**Status:** Accepted
Encounter/effect/consequence remain consumer-specific evidence questions.

### D-534 — Multiple origins and contributors remain first-class
**Status:** Accepted
One consumer outcome can have several compatible Causal Claims.

### D-535 — Reject a universal Impact/exposure/severity score
**Status:** Accepted
Counts, path length, Criticality and exposure percentages do not become hidden probability/harm formulas.

### D-536 — Historical Impact is bitemporal and non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted
Late evidence can change retrospective exposure/effect/consequence without changing what was known then.

### D-537 — Restricted downstream evidence does not become absence
**Status:** Accepted
Safe projection cannot strengthen underlying Impact state or disclose hidden path/use detail.

### D-538 — Group 07 receives exact encounter opportunities, suspect states and alternate paths
**Status:** Accepted
Safeguard prevention reasoning must use the Impact path/encounter model rather than generic reachability.

### D-539 — OPS-067–OPS-085 and IM06-01–IM06-36 are accepted; Group 06 exits complete
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24. OPS-001–OPS-085 is accepted through Group 06; SYN-001–SYN-035, REF-001–REF-030, AUTH-001–AUTH-053 and HLTH-001–HLTH-066 remain unchanged. Phase 007 Group 07 — Propagation Safeguard Scope, Enforcement, Release & Recovery is next.
