# Decision Records — Phase 005 Group 07 Consolidation / Exit Review

Continues after D-250.

### D-251 — Group 07 requires no new concept or AUTH contract
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
AUTH-001–AUTH-053 compose across the representative Phase 005 consolidation scenarios without an additional truth-owning concept or authority refinement. The catalog remains 24 concepts.

### D-252 — Phase 005 authority is a layered constraint stack, not a new workflow truth owner
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
The accepted composition is source assertion/authority standing → semantic or normative governance resolution → Capability Authorization/Authorized Analytical Projection → high-consequence authorization where applicable → disclosure/Explanation projection. Domain concepts retain the underlying operational/health/causal/control truth.

### D-253 — Authority never substitutes for evidence sufficiency
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Assertion Authority, normative authority, Capability Authorization, action approval, break-glass, or communication review cannot satisfy a Phase 004 evidence burden by themselves.

### D-254 — Conflict remains representable across every authority layer
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Authoritative assertion conflict, authority-rule conflict, normative conflict, authorization conflict, and disclosure conflict remain explicit until an applicable governed resolver exists. No universal source, business, technical, strictest, deny, latest, or specificity precedence is introduced at phase exit.

### D-255 — Descriptive governance, normative governance, permission, control authority, and disclosure remain independent
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Semantic/schema meaning, Expectations/thresholds/waivers, permission to view or act, high-consequence operation/confirmation authority, and disclosure rights may have different holders and conditions. No layer automatically grants another.

### D-256 — Phase 005 does not decide empirical metric/schema-health semantics
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Phase 005 governs who may establish metric profiles, schema Expectations, thresholds, severity, waivers, and control-use eligibility. Phase 006 remains responsible for actual metric taxonomy, Baseline comparability, schema compatibility evaluation, health composition, metric propagation/reconciliation, and result timing.

### D-257 — Structural change triggers scoped governance review without manufacturing comparability
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Schema/grain/key/type Change may trigger review, suspension, or revision of metric/Baseline use, but governance cannot declare empirically incompatible evidence comparable. Unaffected dimensions may remain valid.

### D-258 — Restricted-data analysis remains valid without direct row access or declassification
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Framework processing authorization and requester disclosure authorization remain separate. Authorized Analytical Projection may expose exact state, abstraction, opaque existence, or limitation while hidden evidence remains internally traceable and never becomes absence.

### D-259 — High-consequence action remains lifecycle- and evidence-stage specific
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Request, approval, action issuance, control-plane acceptance, enforcement/effect, resulting domain state, and downstream outcome remain distinct. Gate override, safeguard release, retry, causal confirmation, and break-glass never collapse those stages.

### D-260 — Break-glass, delegation, and automation remain bounded exception mechanisms
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Emergency/delegated/service-principal authority requires explicit scoped rules and does not create universal permission, hidden authority inheritance, raw-data access, readiness, health, causal evidence, or enforcement truth.

### D-261 — Technical, business, executive, client, and audit views share one underlying truth
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Audience projections may differ in vocabulary, detail, provenance visibility, and release requirements but cannot intentionally contradict or strengthen the same concept state. Audience labels do not grant permission.

### D-262 — Communication approval is a release-governance action, not a truth upgrade
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
High-consequence communication may require separate review/approval/release, but that process cannot make evidence sufficient, a cause confirmed, control enforced, data healthy, exposure prevented, or compliance established.

### D-263 — Historical authority, authorization, action, and disclosure are non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Later correction, revocation, or broader current access may change present resolution/disclosure without rewriting what authority applied or was known, what permission existed, what action occurred, or what Explanation was actually communicated at the earlier knowledge cut.

### D-264 — Concrete source/actor mappings and technical enforcement remain deferred
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 005 Group 07
Phase 005 does not select Databricks/Unity Catalog, Collibra, Immuta, GitHub, GitHub Actions, human governance actors, or another system as universally authoritative or as the required IAM/control/disclosure mechanism. Concrete mappings remain Phase 009 and realization remains Phase 010.
