### D-708 — Group 04 requires no new inferential/governance concept
**Status:** Accepted

### D-709 — Inferential/governance shorthand must decompose to exact propositions
**Status:** Accepted
`why`, `affected`, `protected`, `owner`, `policy`, `authorized` and similar wording creates no umbrella status.

### D-710 — Investigation lifecycle does not determine causal status
**Status:** Accepted
Closed/open/paused/reopened inquiry state is independent of Causal Claim confirmation.

### D-711 — Investigation lead is not Causal Claim
**Status:** Accepted

### D-712 — Localization is not root cause
**Status:** Accepted
First observed, earliest evidenced, first boundary and first consumer effect remain distinct.

### D-713 — Causal answers require explicit cause→effect proposition identity
**Status:** Accepted
Timing/Lineage/change/control adjacency is basis at most.

### D-714 — Preserve causal role rather than flattening to `the cause`
**Status:** Accepted
Cause/contributor/enabler/trigger/prevention/material influence remain distinguishable.

### D-715 — Causal Claim status survives Explanation
**Status:** Accepted

### D-716 — `Confirmed` remains REF-017 + AUTH-034 gated

### D-717 — Multiple Causal Claims do not require one winner
**Status:** Accepted

### D-718 — `Root cause` wording does not create singular truth
**Status:** Accepted

### D-719 — Lack of causal support is not rejection

### D-720 — Reachability/candidate status is not realized Impact

### D-724 — Business use and consequence preserve the use chain

### D-725 — Impact causal attribution remains Causal Claim work
