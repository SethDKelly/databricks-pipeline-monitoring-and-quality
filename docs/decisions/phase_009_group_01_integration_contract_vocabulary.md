# Decision Records — Phase 009 Group 01 Integration Contract Vocabulary

Continues after D-899.

### D-900 — Group 01 requires no new product concept
**Status:** Accepted
Integration contracts describe source capability and gaps; accepted concepts retain truth ownership.

### D-901 — Bind contracts to exact source surfaces rather than vendor names
**Status:** Accepted
System/product name alone is too coarse for identity, time, coverage, retention or authority semantics.

### D-902 — Source-surface version/edition context is material when semantics differ
**Status:** Accepted
Material semantic changes require capability re-evaluation.

### D-903 — Every capability row binds an accepted proposition
**Status:** Accepted
Queryable fields do not create an integration-owned umbrella status.

### D-904 — Evidence role does not establish authority
**Status:** Accepted
Observational, assertive, normative, operational, authorization, contextual and derived roles remain descriptive.

### D-905 — Source availability does not establish Assertion Authority
**Status:** Accepted
Standing remains proposition/category/context/time specific.

### D-906 — Assertion Authority does not establish evidentiary sufficiency
**Status:** Accepted
An authoritative assertion can be insufficient for another factual conclusion.

### D-907 — Evidence sufficiency does not establish Capability Authorization
**Status:** Accepted
Permission/disclosure remains independent.

### D-908 — Technical retrievability does not establish onward disclosure
**Status:** Accepted
Internal use and requester-visible projection can differ.

### D-909 — Subject identity/join semantics are first-class capability dimensions
**Status:** Accepted
Source-local references require bounded reconciliation to accepted subjects.

### D-910 — Names and timestamp proximity cannot prove identity or association
**Status:** Accepted
Heuristics may produce candidates only.

### D-911 — Cross-system association strength is proposition specific
**Status:** Accepted
Shared/provenance-bearing keys can support exact joins; no universal join-confidence score is accepted.

### D-912 — Event/effective, recorded/knowledge and retrieval times remain distinct
**Status:** Accepted
A historical event timestamp returned later does not establish prior knowledge availability.

### D-913 — Clock semantics and uncertainty must be recorded when material
**Status:** Accepted
Ordering/sequence claims cannot silently assume synchronized precise clocks.

### D-914 — Evidence grain and context constrain the conclusion grain
**Status:** Accepted
Asset-level or aggregate state cannot silently answer narrower run/field/consumer/path propositions.

### D-915 — Positive-evidence capability and negative-evidence capability are independent
**Status:** Accepted
Ability to record occurrence does not prove ability to establish absence.

### D-916 — Strong negatives require explicit opportunity and coverage capability
**Status:** Accepted
No returned record is insufficient when population/window/path/source health is incomplete or unknown.

### D-917 — Coverage is bounded and non-transitive
**Status:** Accepted
Complete coverage for one mode/path/cohort cannot become global completeness.

### D-918 — Availability and latency affect knowledge eligibility, not event truth
**Status:** Accepted
Outage/delay creates unavailable/late evidence rather than negative facts.

### D-919 — Historical replay capability is distinct from current-state availability
**Status:** Accepted
Current APIs cannot be treated as historical stores by convenience.

### D-920 — Retention horizon is proposition specific
**Status:** Accepted
Current support may coexist with unsupported older replay intervals.

### D-921 — Correction/backfill semantics must preserve non-rewriting history where required
**Status:** Accepted
Late evidence does not enter earlier knowledge cuts without evidence it was available then.

### D-922 — Destructive mutation/deletion can make historical requirements unsupported
**Status:** Accepted
Missing prior state is not reconstructed by assumption.

### D-923 — Duplicate/common-derived surfaces are not independent corroboration
**Status:** Accepted
Different endpoints over common telemetry do not increase evidence independence.

### D-924 — Source conflict remains conflict absent accepted resolution rules
**Status:** Accepted
Recency, specificity, count and matrix order do not create precedence.

### D-925 — Fallback accessibility does not inherit authority
**Status:** Accepted
A fallback source retains its own standing/evidence contract.

### D-926 — Integration support status is proposition/source-set/context bound
**Status:** Accepted
No universal vendor support score is accepted.

### D-927 — Use supported / partially supported / unsupported / unknown-not-verified / not-applicable
**Status:** Accepted
These are feasibility outcomes, not product truth or confidence states.

### D-928 — Unsupported is a valid finding and does not weaken product semantics
**Status:** Accepted
REF/AUTH/HLTH/OPS/EXPL requirements remain intact.

### D-929 — Unknown capability is distinct from unsupported capability
**Status:** Accepted
Unverified documentation/edition behavior is not assumed impossible.

### D-930 — Not applicable is distinct from unavailable or unsupported
**Status:** Accepted
A source irrelevant to a proposition is not an integration failure.

### D-931 — Quota/rate/cost constraints can reduce feasible coverage but not truth semantics
**Status:** Accepted
Any sampling/delay introduced by constraints must be visible in support classification.

### D-932 — Integration observability is separate from monitored product state
**Status:** Accepted
Auth, throttling, pagination, schema drift and source outage must not become product negatives.

### D-933 — Capability matrix rows compose only through explicit product semantics
**Status:** Accepted
Adjacency in the matrix is not transitive proof.

### D-934 — INTG-001–INTG-022 and IC01-01–IC01-40 are accepted
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 009 Group 01
Group 01 is complete; Group 02 is next.
