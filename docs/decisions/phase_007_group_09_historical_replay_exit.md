# Decision Records — Phase 007 Group 09 Historical Replay & Exit

Continues after D-599.

### D-600 — Group 09 requires no new concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 007 Group 09
Historical operational replay is a time-bound view over existing concept histories rather than a new truth-owning concept.

### D-601 — OPS-001–OPS-123 are sufficient; no OPS-124 is required
**Status:** Accepted
Replay and consolidation expose no unresolved functional requirement outside the accepted operational contracts.

### D-602 — Preserve actual retained historical state, as-known-at-cut reconstruction and current retrospective interpretation as distinct views
**Status:** Accepted
These views may legitimately disagree and must not overwrite one another.

### D-603 — Current authorized projection is separate from historical epistemic state
**Status:** Accepted
Disclosure/redaction can change what a requester sees but cannot alter internal truth or historical knowledge state.

### D-604 — Historical replay resolves source-owned facts before derived cross-concept conclusions
**Status:** Accepted
The ordering is a reasoning dependency only and does not select workflow/event/storage architecture.

### D-605 — Event/effective time and recorded/knowledge cut are independent replay coordinates
**Status:** Accepted
A historical question without a knowledge cut must be labeled as current/latest retrospective interpretation rather than implied contemporaneous knowledge.

### D-606 — `Not known/recorded by cutoff` is not a real-world negative by convenience
**Status:** Accepted
Historical absence/negative claims continue to require conclusion-specific opportunity/coverage evidence.

### D-607 — Current topology is never projected backward
**Status:** Accepted
Then-effective Lineage and then-known relationship evidence govern historical topology/relevance.

### D-608 — Planned topology/change never becomes realized history through replay
**Status:** Accepted
Prospective scenario state remains distinct from effective Lineage, Deployment activation, realized Change and actual Impact.

### D-609 — Late Deployment/change evidence may revise retrospective realization comparison without rewriting historical intent/actions
**Status:** Accepted
Change Intent, Deployment and Change histories remain independently addressable.

### D-610 — Late execution/version evidence may revise retrospective run/consumption reconstruction
**Status:** Accepted
The earlier knowledge cut preserves its prior unknown/partial execution state.

### D-611 — First-deviation/localization results may move retrospectively
**Status:** Accepted
A moved earliest evidenced deviation or reconciliation boundary does not rewrite what responders first observed and does not become root cause.

### D-612 — Investigation closure is not evidence immunity
**Status:** Accepted
Material late evidence may reopen an Investigation or alter leads/localization while preserving prior closure history.

### D-613 — Causal Claim status is knowledge-cut specific and non-rewriting
**Status:** Accepted
Later support/contradiction/status evolution does not backfill into an earlier claim state; confirmation remains REF-017 + AUTH-034 governed.

### D-614 — Prospective Impact is never backfilled with later actual Impact
**Status:** Accepted
Later realized exposure/effect/consequence/cause cannot be inserted into an earlier proposal review as known-at-review evidence.

### D-615 — Historical exposure/non-exposure replay retains encounter/path/version coverage burdens
**Status:** Accepted
Late consumer evidence may resolve an earlier unknown but one safe path never proves broad historical non-exposure.

### D-616 — Safeguard prevented-exposure conclusions may change retrospectively without rewriting Safeguard actions
**Status:** Accepted
Prevention remains a derived REF-028 + Impact determination over historical opportunity/path evidence.

### D-617 — Historical Safeguard release remains distinct from post-release recovery
**Status:** Accepted
Later recovered state does not retroactively turn release into recovery truth.

### D-618 — Gate historical decision, delivery, enforcement and execution remain separate under replay
**Status:** Accepted
A retained HOLD/ADMIT/override/fallback action is not rewritten by later enforcement/run evidence.

### D-619 — Late run evidence can contradict historical full-HOLD enforcement without changing the historical HOLD decision
**Status:** Accepted
Decision truth and enforcement truth remain independent propositions.

### D-620 — Valid historical ADMIT can coexist with no execution
**Status:** Accepted
Later scheduler/compute evidence may explain non-execution without invalidating barrier removal.

### D-621 — Historical override/fallback admission never rewrites readiness
**Status:** Accepted
`not ready`/`unknown`/`conflicting`/`unavailable` remains the readiness result unless separately reevaluated from its own evidence.

### D-622 — Historical control telemetry outage creates no retrospective universal fail-open/fail-closed inference
**Status:** Accepted
Run/non-run alone does not identify fallback policy/application/enforcement.

### D-623 — Multiple Gate and Safeguard controls retain independent historical materiality
**Status:** Accepted
No first-control, last-control, most-restrictive, source-precedence or combined-control scalar is accepted.

### D-624 — Control-induced operational effects remain source-owned; broader historical attribution belongs to Causal Claim
**Status:** Accepted
Delay, stale serving, skipped cycle and non-delivery can be historical facts without automatically proving which control caused them.

### D-625 — Historical authority/authorization does not grant current disclosure
**Status:** Accepted
Current requester authorization is independently applied to historical/replay truth.

### D-626 — No universal historical operational state or replay score is accepted
**Status:** Accepted
The system retains proposition-level topology/change/execution/Investigation/Impact/control state rather than flattening an incident into one score/status.

### D-627 — Phase 007 remains implementation-neutral at exit
**Status:** Accepted
Historical replay semantics do not select graph/event/temporal storage, control plane, scheduler, source integration, scoring algorithm or technical architecture.

### D-628 — HR09-01–HR09-36 pass and Phase 007 exits complete
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24; OPS-001–OPS-123 is final; SYN-001–SYN-035, REF-001–REF-030, AUTH-001–AUTH-053 and HLTH-001–HLTH-066 remain unchanged. Phase 008 — Business Questioning and Explanation is next.
