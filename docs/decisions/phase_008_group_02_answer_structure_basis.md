# Decision Records — Phase 008 Group 02 Answer Structure & Basis Traceability

Continues after D-650.

### D-651 — Group 02 requires no new Answer or Statement concept
**Status:** Accepted — Phase 008 Group 02
Explanation owns composition/communication; substantive propositions retain accepted source truth owners.

### D-652 — Material answer statement identity is proposition based, not wording based
**Status:** Accepted
Subject, conclusion, material scope and temporal/knowledge perspective identify the statement proposition.

### D-653 — Preserve source truth owner and epistemic type in every material answer statement
**Status:** Accepted
Narrative composition cannot relabel Observation, Assessment, Causal Claim, Impact, control or governance state into a stronger category.

### D-654 — Distinguish direct projections from derived cross-concept statements
**Status:** Accepted
Derived statements require explicit accepted semantic join logic and basis.

### D-655 — Juxtaposition does not create a derived proposition
**Status:** Accepted
Several true facts placed together do not establish causality, exposure, prevention, health or another cross-concept conclusion without the accepted derivation burden.

### D-656 — Select statements by question-relative relevance/materiality
**Status:** Accepted
The answer need not enumerate all available facts, but omission cannot change the requested conclusion's meaning or epistemic state.

### D-657 — Materiality is not evidence strength
**Status:** Accepted
Criticality/priority/presentation relevance may affect ordering without strengthening truth, probability or confidence.

### D-658 — Distinguish answer-bearing, contextual and limitation roles
**Status:** Accepted
These are Explanation presentation roles only; source truth ownership remains unchanged.

### D-659 — Material limitations are first-class answer content
**Status:** Accepted
A limitation cannot be silently omitted when its absence would overstate scope, certainty, causality, exposure or control effectiveness.

### D-660 — Supporting, contradicting and limiting basis roles are statement relative
**Status:** Accepted
Basis-role assignment does not mutate the source fact/evidence.

### D-661 — Basis sufficiency is conclusion relative
**Status:** Accepted
Evidence adequate for a weaker proposition does not license a stronger one.

### D-662 — Preserve duplicate/common-derived evidence relationships
**Status:** Accepted
Repeated/common-derived basis does not become independent corroboration.

### D-663 — Reject evidence-count confidence arithmetic
**Status:** Accepted
No confidence strength is inferred merely from number of basis items.

### D-664 — Cross-concept derivation must cite the accepted join rule
**Status:** Accepted
Explanation composition follows SYN/REF/AUTH/HLTH/OPS semantics rather than prose adjacency or generic inference.

### D-665 — Cross-concept answer composition is not generally transitive
**Status:** Accepted
Lineage, timing, confirmed upstream cause, control state or other intermediate facts do not automatically propagate into downstream conclusions.

### D-666 — Headline, summary and detail may differ in detail but not epistemic strength
**Status:** Accepted
Rendering depth cannot strengthen or contradict the same underlying statement proposition.

### D-667 — A summary may say less but cannot imply more
**Status:** Accepted
Compression must retain material qualifiers/limitations needed to prevent overclaiming.

### D-668 — Omission is not evidence of absence
**Status:** Accepted
Excluded detail cannot be worded or structured so that users infer a strong negative unsupported by source coverage.

### D-669 — Multi-statement answers preserve sibling independence
**Status:** Accepted
One resolved health/cause/Impact/control statement does not resolve another by narrative grouping.

### D-670 — Reject synthetic global answer truth
**Status:** Accepted
No universal overall answer/incident/root-cause/Impact/control state is created merely to summarize several independent propositions.

### D-671 — Primary-answer ordering is context specific
**Status:** Accepted
Ordering may follow question/purpose/materiality but creates no hidden authority or universal importance rule.

### D-672 — Reject universal answer-importance/severity/confidence scoring
**Status:** Accepted
Phase 008 does not add a scalar ranking that merges truth, priority, criticality, impact or evidence strength.

### D-673 — Internal statement-to-basis traceability is mandatory
**Status:** Accepted
Every material answer statement must remain recoverably linked to its source proposition/evidence and derivation context.

### D-674 — Internal traceability is separate from visible basis disclosure
**Status:** Accepted
Group 06 will govern authorized basis inspection; restricted visible basis does not erase internal provenance.

### D-675 — Answer coverage is proposition/subquestion bound, not confidence
**Status:** Accepted
Partial/unresolved/restricted/unavailable coverage remains explicit and is not collapsed into a percentage confidence score.

### D-676 — EXPL-013–EXPL-028 and AS02-01–AS02-30 are accepted; Group 03 is next
**Status:** Accepted
The concept catalog remains 24. Phase 008 proceeds to Health, Change & Execution Question Semantics.
